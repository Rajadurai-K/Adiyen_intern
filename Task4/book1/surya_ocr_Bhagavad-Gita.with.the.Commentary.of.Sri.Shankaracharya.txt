chapter 1

THE DESPONDENCY OF ARJUNA

धर्मक्षेत्रे कुरुक्षेत्रे समवेता युयुत्सवः ।
मामका: पाण्डवाश्चैव किमकुर्वत सञ्जय ॥ १ ॥

What did Pându's sons and mine do when
they assembled together on the sacred plain of
Kurukshetra, eager for battle, O Samjaya?

दृष्ट्वा तु पाण्डवानीकं व्यूढं दुर्योधनस्तदा ।
आचार्यमुपसंगम्य राजा वचनमत्रवीत् ॥ २ ॥

Having seen the army of the Påndavas
drawn up in battle-array, prince Duryodhana then
approached his teacher and spoke (these) words:

पइयैतां पाण्डुपुत्राणामाचार्यं महतीं चमूम् ।
ब्यदां द्रुपद्पुत्रेण तव शिष्येण धीमता ॥ ३ ॥

"O teacher, look at this grand army of
the sons of Pându, marshalled by thy talented
pupil, the son of Drupada.

अत्र शूरा महेष्वासा भीमार्जुनसमा युधि ।
युयुधानो विराटश्च द्रुपदश्च महारथ: ॥ ४ ॥

"Here are heroes, mighty archers, equal
in battle to Bhîma and Arjuna,-Yuyudhâna,
Virâta, and Drupada, the master of a great car
(mahāratha).*

घृष्टकेतुश्चेकितानः काशिराजश्च वीर्यवान ।
पुरुजित्कुन्तिभोजश्च शैब्यश्च नरपुङ्गवः ॥ ५ ॥

“Dhrishtaketu, Chekitâna, and the valiant
king of Kâsi, Purujit and Kunti-Bhoja and that
eminent man Saibya;

युधामन्युश्च विक्रान्त उत्तमौजाश्च वीर्यवान् ।
सौमद्रो द्रौपदेयाश्च सर्व एव महारथा: ।। ६ ।।

"The heroic Yudhâmanyu and the brave
Uttamaujas; the son of Subhadrâ and the sons of
Draupadî, all masters of great cars (mahârathas).

अस्माकं तु विशिष्टा ये तान्निबोध द्विजोत्तम ।
नायका मम सैन्यस्य संज्ञार्थं तान्त्रवीमि ते ॥ ७ ॥

"But know, O best of the twice-born, who
are the most distinguished among us, the leaders
of my army; these I name to thee by way of
example.

भवान्भीष्मश्च कर्णश्च क्रुपश्च समितिञ्चयः ।
अश्वत्थामा विकर्णश्च सौमद्त्तिस्तथैव च ॥ ८ ॥

"Thyself and Bhîshma, and Karna, and
also Kripa, the victor in war, Asvatthâman and
Vikarna, and also Jayadratha, the son of Soma-
datta ;

अन्ये च बहव: शूरा मदर्थे त्यक्तजीविताः ।
नानाशस्त्रप्रहरणाः सर्वे युद्धविशारदाः ॥ ९ ॥

"And many other heroes who have given
up their lives for my sake, fighting with various
weapons, all well-skilled in battle.

अपर्याप्तं तदस्माकं बलं भीष्माभिरक्षितम् ।
पर्याप्तं त्विदमेतेषाम् बळं भीमाभिरक्षितम् ॥ १० ॥

"This army of ours protected
bv Bhîshma is inadequate, whereas that army of
theirs which is under the protection of Bhîma is
adequate.*

अयनेषु च सर्वेषु यथाभागमवस्थिताः ।
भीष्ममेवाभिरक्षन्तु भवन्तः सर्वे एव हि ।। ११ ॥

"And therefore do ye all, occupying your
respective positions in the several divisions of the
army, support Bhîshma only."

तस्य सञ्जनयन्हर्षं कुरुवृद्धः पितामहः ।
सिंहनादं विनद्योच्चै: शङ्खं दथ्मौ प्रतापवान् ॥ १२ ॥

His mighty grandsire, (Bhîshma), the
oldest of the Kauravas, in order to cheer him,
sounded on high a lion's roar and blew his conch.

तत: राङ्काश्च भेर्यश्च पणवानकगोमुखा: ।
सहसैवाभ्यहन्यन्त स शब्दस्तुमुलोऽमवत् ॥ १३ ॥

Then, all at once, conches and kettle-
drums, cymbols, drums and horns were played
upon, and the sound was a tumultuous uproar.

ततः श्वेतैईयैर्युक्ते महति स्यन्दने स्थितौ ।
माधव: पाण्डवश्चैव दिव्यौ शङ्खौ प्रदृष्मतु: ॥ १४ ॥

Then, too, Màdhava and the son of
Pându, seated in a grand chariot yoked to white
horses, blew their celestial conches.

पाछ्रजन्यं हृषीकेशो देवदत्तं धनञ्जयः ।
पौण्डूं दध्मौ महाशङ्खं भीमकर्मा वृकोदरः ॥ १५ ॥

Hrishîkesa blew the Pànchajanya, and
Arjuna blew the Devadatta. Bhîma, (the doer) of
terrible deeds, blew his great conch Paundra.

अनन्तविजयं राजा कुन्तीपुत्रो युधिष्ठिरः ।
नकुल: सहदेवश्च सुघोषमणिपुष्पकौ ॥ १६ ॥

Prince Yudhishthira, the son of Kunti,
blew the Anantavijaya, while Nakula and Sahadeva
blew the Sughosha and the Manipushpaka.

काइयश्च परमेष्वास: शिखण्डी च महारथ: ।
घृष्टचुम्नो विराटश्च सात्यकिश्चापराजितः ॥ १७ ॥

The king of Kâsî, an excellent archer,
Sikhandin, the master of a great car, Dhrishta-
dyumna and Virâta, and the unconquered Sâtyaki;

द्रुपदो द्रौपदेयाश्च सर्वशः प्रथिवीपते ।
सौभद्रश्च महाबाहुः शङ्कान्दन्मुः प्रथक्पृथक् ॥ १८ ॥

Drupada and the sons of Draupadi, O
lord of earth, and the son of Subhadrà, of mighty
arms, all together blew their respective conches.

स घोषो घार्तराष्ट्राणां हृदयानि व्यदारयत् ।
नभश्च पृथिवीं चैव तुमुले व्यनुनादयन् ॥ १९ ॥

That tumultuous sound rent the hearts
of (the people) of Dhritarâshtra's party, making
both heaven and earth resound.

अथ व्यवस्थितान्दृष्ट्वा धार्तराष्ट्रान्कपिथ्वजः ।
प्रवृत्ते शस्त्रसम्पाते धनुरुद्यम्य पाण्डवः ॥ २० ॥

हृषीकेशं तदा वाक्यमिदमाह महीपते ।
सेनयोरुभयोर्मध्ये रथं स्थापय मेऽच्युत ॥ २१ ॥

यावदेतान्निरीक्षेऽहं योद्धकामानवस्थितान् ।
कैर्मया सह योद्घव्यमस्मित्रणसमुद्यमे ॥ २२ ॥

20-22. Then seeing the people of Dhritarash-
tra's party regularly marshalled, while the discharge
of weapons began, Arjuna, the son of Påndu, whose
ensign was a monkey, O King of earth, took up
his bow and said thus to Krishna :
“O Achyuta (Immortal), place my chariot
between the two armies, that I may just see those
who stand here desirous to fight, and know with
whom I must fight in this strife of battle.

योत्स्यमानानवेक्षेऽहं य एतेऽत्र समागताः ।
धार्तराष्ट्रस्य दुर्बुद्धेर्युद्धे प्रियचिकीर्षवः ॥ २३ ॥

"I will observe those who are assembled
here and are about to engage in battle desirous to
do service in war to the evil-minded
son of
Dhritarâshtra."

एवमुक्तो हृषीकेशो गुडाकेशेन भारत।
सेनयोरुभयोर्मध्ये स्थापयित्वा रथोत्तमम् ॥ २४ ॥

भीष्मद्रोणप्रमुखतः सर्वेषां च महीक्षिताम ।
डवाच पार्थ पदयैतान्समवेतान्कुरूनिति ॥ २५ ॥

24-25. O descendant of Bharata, Hrishîkesa
(Krishna) thus addressed by Gudâkesa (Arjuna)
stationed that excellent car between the two armies
in front of Bhishma and Drona and all the rulers
of earth, and said : " O son of Prithà, look at these
assembled Kauravas."

तत्रापदयत्स्थितान्पार्थः पितृनथ पितामहान् ।
आचार्यान्मातुलान्ञ्रातृन्पुत्रान्पौत्रान्सर्खीस्तथा ॥ २६ ॥
श्वद्युरान्सुहृदश्चैव सेनयोरुभयोरपि ।

26-27. Then the son of Prithā saw arrayed
there in both the armies fathers and grandfathers,
teachers, maternal uncles, brothers, sons, grandsons
and comrades, fathers-in-law and friends.

तान्समीक्ष्य स कौन्तेय: सर्वान्बन्धूनवस्थितान् ॥ २७ ॥
कृपया परयाऽऽविष्टो विषीदन्निदमत्रवीत् ।

27-28. When the son of Kunti saw all the
kinsmen standing, he was overcome with deepest
pity and said thus in sorrow :

दृष्ट्वेमं खजनं कृष्ण युयुत्सुं समुपस्थितम् ॥ २८ ॥

सीदन्ति मम गात्राणि मुखं च परिद्युष्यति ।
वेपधुश्च शरीरे मे रोमहर्षश्च जायते ॥ २९ ॥

28-29. Seeing these kinsmen, O Krishna,
arrayed and desirous to fight, my limbs droop
down, and my mouth is dried up. A tremor comes
on my body and my hairs stand on end.

गाण्डीवं स्रंसते हस्तात्त्वक्चैव परिद्द्यते ।
न च शक्तोम्यवस्थातुं भ्रमतीव च मे मन: ॥ ३० ॥

The Gāndīva slips from my hand, and
my skin is intensely burning. I am also unable to
stand and my mind is whirling round, as it were.

निमित्तानि च पत्र्यामि विपरीतानि केशव ।
न च श्रेयोऽनुपदयामि हत्वा स्वजनमाहवे ॥ ३१ ॥

And, O Kesava, 1 see omens foreboding
evil. Nor do I see any good from killing my
kinsmen in battle.

न काइक्षे विजयं कृष्ण न च राज्यं सुखानि च ।
किं नो राज्येन गोविन्द किं भोगैर्जीवितेन वा ।। ३२ ।।

I desire not victory, O Krisna, nor king-
dom, nor pleasures. Of what avail is dominion
to us, O Govinda? Of what avail are pleasures
and even life ?

येषामर्थे काङ्क्षितं नो राज्यं भोगाः सुखानि च ।
त इमेऽवस्थिता युद्धे प्राणांस्यक्तवा धनानि च ॥ ३.३ ॥
आचार्याः पितरः पुत्रास्तयैव च पितामहाः ।
मातुलाः श्वश्लुराः पौत्राः स्यालाः सम्बन्धिनस्तथा ॥ ३४ ॥

33-34. They for whose sake dominion, enjoy-
ments and pleasures are sought by us are here
standing, having staked their life and wealth:
teachers, fathers, sons as well as grandfathers;
maternal uncles, fathers-in-law, grandsons, bro-
thers-in-law as also (other) relatives.

एतान्न हन्तुमिच्छामि न्नतोऽपि मधुसूदन ।
अपि त्रैलोक्यराज्यस्य हेतोः किं नु महीकृते ॥ ३५ ॥

These, O slayer of Madhu, I do not wish
to kill, though they kill me, even for the sake of
dominion over the three worlds; how much less,
for the sake of the earth!

निहत्य धार्तराष्ट्रान्नः का प्रीतिः स्याज्जनार्दन ।
पापमेवाश्रयेदस्मान्हत्वैतानाततायिनः ॥ ३६ ॥

O Janārdana, what delight shall be ours
ર6
after killing the sons of Dhritasāshtra? On killing
these felons, sin only will take hold of us.

तस्मान्नाही वयं हन्तुं घार्तराष्ट्रान्स्वबान्धवान् ।
स्वजनं हि कथं हत्वा सुखिनः स्याम माघव ॥ ३७ ॥

We had then better not slay our own
kinsmen, the sons of Dhritarāshtra; for, how can
we be happy, O Màdhava, after slaying our own
people ?

यद्यप्येते न पत्रयन्ति लोभोपहतचेतसः ।
कुलक्षयकृतं दोषं मित्रद्रोहे च पातकम् ॥ ३८ ॥

कथं न ज्ञेयमस्माभिः पापादस्मान्निवर्तितुम् ।
कुल्रक्षयकृतं दोषं प्रपद्यद्भिर्जनार्दन ॥ ३९ ॥

38-39. Though these, whose intelligence is
stricken by greed, perceive no evil in the extinction
of families and no sin in treachery to friends, yet,

O Janārdana, should not we, who clearly see evil
in the extinction of a family, learn to refrain from
this sinful deed?

क़ुलक्षये प्रणइयन्ति कुलघर्माः सनातनाः ।
घर्मे नष्टे कुलं कृत्स्नमधर्मोऽभिभवत्युत ॥ ४० ॥

On the extinction of a family, the imme-
morial dharmas * of that family disappear. When
the dharmas disappear, impiety (adharma) over-
takes the whole family. +

अधर्माभिभवात्कृष्ण प्रदुष्यन्ति कुलस्रियः ।
स्रीषु दुष्टासु वार्ष्णेय जायते वर्णसङ्करः ॥ ४१ ॥

By the prevalence of impiety, O Krishna,
the women of the family become corrupt. Women
corrupted, there will be intermingling of castes
(varna-samkara), O descendant of Vrishnis.

सङ्करो नरकायैव कुलन्नानां कुलस्य च ।
पतन्ति पितरो द्येषां छप्तपिण्डोदकक्रियाः ॥ ४२ ॥

Confusion of castes leads the family of
these destroyers of families also to hell; for, their
forefathers fall (down to hell), deprived of the
offerings of pinda (rice-ball) and water.

दोषैरेतैः कुलघानां वर्णसङ्करकारकैः ।
उत्साद्यन्ते जातिघर्माः कळघर्माश्च शाश्वताः ॥ ४३ ॥

By these evil deeds of the destroyers of

families which cause the intermingling of castes,
the eternal dharmas of castes and families are
subverted.

उत्सन्नकुलधर्माणां मनुष्याणां जनार्दन ।
नरके नियतं वासो भवतीत्यनुह्युश्रुम ॥ ४४ ॥

We have heard, O Janârdana, that neces-
sary is the dwelling in hell of the men whose family
dharmas are subverted.

अहो बत महत्पापं कर्तुं व्यवसिता वयम्।
यद्राज्यसुखळोभेन हन्तुं खजनमुद्यताः ॥ ४५ ॥

Alas! we have resolved to commit a great
sin, inasmuch as we are endeavouring to slay our
kinsmen out of a craving for the pleasures of
dominion.

यदि मामप्रतीकारमञ्खं शखपाणय: ।
घार्तराष्ट्रा रणे हन्युस्तन्मे क्षेमतरं भवेत् ॥ ४६ ॥

It would be better for me, if the sons of
Dhritarâshtra, with arms in hand, should slay me
unarmed and unresisting in the battle.

एवमुक्तवाऽर्जुन: सङ्खये स्थोपस्थ उपाविशत् ।
विसृज्य सज्ञरं चापं शोकसंविग्नमानसः ॥ ४७ ॥

Having said thus, Arjuna, sorrow-stricken
in mind, cast aside his bow and arrows in the
midst of the battle and sat down in the chariot.

SANKHYA YOGA.

तं तथा क्रुपयाऽऽविष्टमश्रुपूर्णाकुलेक्षणम् ।
विषीदन्तमिदं वाक्यमुवाच मधुसूदनः ॥ १ ॥

To him who was thus overcome with pity
and afflicted, and whose eyes were full of tears and
agitated, the destroyer of Madhu spoke as follows:

कुतस्त्वा कइमलमिदं विषमे समुपस्थितम् ।
अनार्यज्रष्टमस्वर्ग्यमकीर्तिकरमर्जुन ॥ २ ॥

Whence in (this) perilous strait has come
upon thee this weakness cherished by the unworthy,
debarring from heaven and causing disgrace,
O Arjuna?

क्रैब्यं मा स्म गम: पार्थ नैतत्त्वय्युपपद्यते ।
क्षुद्रं हृदयदौर्बल्यं त्य<del>त्त</del>्वोत्तिष्ठ परन्तप ॥ ३ ॥

Yield not to unmanliness, O son of Prithâ.
It does not become thee. Cast off this base weak-
ness of heart and arise, O tormentor of foes

कथं भीष्ममहं सङ्ख्ये द्रोणं च मघुसूदन ।
इषुभिः प्रतियोत्स्यामि पूजार्हावरिसूदन ॥ ४ ॥

O slayer of Madhu, how shall I assail in
battle with arrows Bhishma and Drona, who are
worthy of worship, O slayer of enemies.

गुरूनहत्वा हि महानुभावान्
श्रेयो भोक्तुं मैंक्ष्यमपीह लोके ।
हत्वाऽर्थकामांस्तु गुरूनिहैव
मुञ्जीय भोगात्रुघिरप्रदिग्घान् ॥ ५ ॥

Better indeed in this world to live even
upon alms than to slay the teachers of high honor.
But, were I to slay these teachers, I should only
in this world enjoy the pleasures of wealth, delights
stained with blood.

न चैतदिद्याः कतरच्यो गरीयो
यद्वा जयेम यदि वा नो जयेयुः ।
यानेव हत्वा न जिजीविषामः
तेऽवस्थिताः प्रमुखे घार्तराष्ट्राः ॥ ६ ॥

And we know not which * is the better
alternative for us; nor do we know whether we
shall conquer them or they will conquer us. Even
the sons of Dhritarâshtra, after killing whom we
do not wish to live, stand arrayed against us.

कार्पण्यदोषोपहतस्वभावः
प्टच्छामि त्वां धर्मसम्मूढचेताः ।
यच्छ्रेय: स्यान्निश्चितं ब्रूहि तन्मे
शिल्यस्तेऽहं शाधि मां त्वां प्रपन्नम् ॥ ७ ॥

My heart contaminated by the taint of
helplessness, my mind confounded about Dharma,*
I ask Thee: Tell me what is absolutely good.
T
am Thy pupil. Instruct me, who have sought
Thy grace.

न हि प्रपद्यामि ममापनुचादू-
यच्छोकमुच्छोषणमिन्द्रियाणाम् ।
अवाप्य भूमावसपन्नमृद्धं
राज्यं सुराणामपि चाधिपत्यम् ॥ ८ ॥

I do not indeed see what can dispel the
grief which burns up my senses, even after attain-
ing unrivalled and prosperous dominion on earth
or even lordship over gods.

सञ्जय उवाच
एवमुक्तवा हृषीकेशं गुडाकेशः परन्तपः ।
न योत्स्य इति गोविन्दमुक्तवा तूर्ष्णी बभूव ह ॥ ९ ||

Having spoken thus to Hrishikesa, Gudà-
kesa, the tormenter of foes, said to Govinda, 'I
will not fight,' and verily remained silent.

तमुवाच हृषीकेशः प्रहुसन्निव भारत ।
सेनयोरुभयोर्मघ्ये विषीदन्तमिदं वचः ॥ १० ॥

To him who was grieving in the midst of
the two armies, O descendant of Bharata, Hrishi-
kesa as if smiling, spoke these words :

अशोच्यानन्वशोचस्त्वं प्रज्ञावादांश्च भाषसे ।
गतासूनगतासूंश्च नानुशोचन्ति पण्डिताः ॥ ११ ॥

For those who deserve no grief thou hast
grieved, and words of wisdom thou speakest. For
the living and for the dead the wise grieve not.*

न त्वेवाहं जातु नासं न त्वं नेमे जनाधिपाः ।
न चैव न भविष्यामः सर्वे वयमतः परम् ॥ १२ ॥

Never did I not exist, nor thou, nor these
rulers of men; and no one of us will ever hereafter
cease to exist.

देहिनोऽस्मिन्यथा देहे कौमारं यौवनं जरा ।
तया देहान्तरप्राप्तिर्धीरस्तत्र न मुह्यति ॥ १३ ॥

Just as in this body the embodied (Self)
passes into childhood and youth and old age, so
does He pass into another body. There the wise
man is not distressed.

मात्रास्पर्शास्तु कौन्तेय शीतोष्णसुखदुःखदाः ।
आगमापायिनोऽनित्यास्तांस्तितिक्षस्व भारत ॥ १४ ॥

The sense-contacts it is, O son of Kunti,
which cause heat and cold; pleasure and pain;
they come and go, they are impermanent. Them
endure bravely, O descendant of Bharata.

यं हि न व्यथयन्लेते पुरुषं पुरुषर्षभ ।
समदुःखसुखं धीरं सोऽमृतत्वाय कल्पते ॥ १५ ॥

That wise man whom, verily, these afflict
not, O chief of men, to whom pleasure and pain are
same, he for immortality is fit.

नासतो विद्यते भावो नाभावो विद्यते सतः ।
डभयोरपि दृष्टोऽन्तस्त्वनयोस्तस्वदर्शिभिः ॥ १६ ॥

Of the unreal no being there is; there is
no non being of the real. Of both these is fhe
truth seen by the seers of the Essence.

अविनाशि तु तद्विद्धि येन सर्वेमिदं ततम् ।
विनाशमव्ययस्यास्य न कश्चित्कर्तुमर्हति ॥ १७ ॥

But know that to be imperishable by
which all this is pervaded. None can cause the
destruction of That, the Inexhaustible.

अन्तवन्त इमे देहा नित्यस्योक्ताः शरीरिणः ।
अनाशिनोऽप्रमेयस्य तस्माद्युध्यस्व भारत ॥ १८ ॥

These bodies of the embodied (Self) who
is eternal, indestructible and unknowable, are said
to have an end. Do fight, therefore, O descendant
of Bharata.

य एनं वेत्ति हन्तारं यश्चैनं मन्यते हतम् ।
उभौ तौ न विजानीतो नायं इन्ति न हन्यते ॥ १९ ॥

Whoever looks upon Him as the slayer,
and whoever looks upon Him as the slain, both
these know not aright. He slays not, nor is He
slain

न जायते म्रियते वा कदाचि-
न्नायं भूत्वा भविता वा न भूयः ।
अजो नित्यः शाश्वतोऽयं पुराणो
न हन्यते हन्यमाने शरीरे ॥ २० ॥

He is not born, nor does He ever die;
after having been, He again ceases not to be; nor
the reverse. Unborn, eternal, unchangeable and
primeval, He is not slain when the body is slain.

वेदाविनाशिनं नित्यं य एतमजमव्ययम् ।
कथं स पुरुषः पार्थं कं घातयति हन्ति कम । २१ ॥

Whoso knows Him as
indestructible,
eternal, unborn and inexhaustible,—How, O son of
Prithâ, and whom does such a man cause to slay,
and whom does he slay?

वासांसि जीणांनि यथा विद्दाय
नवानि गृह्णाति नरोऽपराणि ।
तथा शरीराणि विहाय जीर्णा-
न्यन्यानि संयाति नवानि देही || २२ ||

Just as a man casts off worn-out clothes
and puts on others which are new, so the embodied
(Self) casts off worn-out bodies and enters others
which are new.

नैनं छिन्दन्ति शस्त्राणि नैनं दहति पावक: ।
न चैनं छेदयन्त्यापो न शाषयति मारुतः ॥ २३ ॥

Him weapons cut not, Him fire burns
not, and Him water wets not; Him wind dries
not.

अच्छेद्योऽयमदाद्योऽयमक्केद्योऽशोष्य एव च ।
नित्यः सर्वगतः स्थाणुरचलोऽयं सनातनः ॥ २४ ॥

He cannot be cut, nor burnt, nor wetted,
nor dried up. He is everlasting, all-pervading
stable, firm, and eternal.

अव्यक्तोऽयमचिन्त्योऽयमविकार्योऽयमु<del>च्यतः</del>
तस्मादेवं विदित्वैनं नानुज्ञोचितुमईसि ॥ २५ ॥

He, it is said, is unmanifest, unthinkable
and unchangeable. Wherefore, knowing Him to
be such, thou hadst better grieve not.

अथ चैनं नित्यजातं नित्यं वा मन्यसे मृतम् ।
तथापि त्वं महाबाहो नैनं शोचितमईसि ॥ २६ ॥

But even if thou thinkest of Him as
ever being born and ever dying, even then, O
mighty-armed, thou oughtst not to grieve thus.

जातस्य हि ध्रुवो मृत्युर्धुवं जन्म मृतस्य च ।
तस्माद्परिहार्येऽर्थे न त्वं शोचितुमईसि ॥ २७ ॥

To that which is born, death is indeed
certain; and to that which is dead, birth is certain.
Wherefore, about the unavoidable thing, thou
oughtst not to grieve.

अव्यक्तादानि भूतानि व्यक्तमध्यानि भारत ।
अव्यक्तनिधनान्येव तत्र का परिदेवना ॥ २८ ॥

Beings have their beginning unseen,
their middle seen, and their end unseen again.
Why any lamentation regarding them ?

आश्चर्यवत्पदयति कश्चिदेन-
माश्चर्यवद्वदति तथैव चान्यः ।
आश्चर्यवचैनमन्यः श्रणोति
श्रुत्वाऽप्येनं वेद न चैव कश्चिन् ॥ २९ ।

One sees Him as a wonder; and so also
another speaks of him as a wonder; and as a
wonder another hears of Him; and though hearing,
none understands Him at all.

देही नित्यमवध्योऽयं देहे सर्वस्य भारत ।
तस्मात्सर्वाणि भूतानि न त्वं शोवितुमर्हेसि ॥ ३० ॥

He, the embodied (Self) in every one's
body, can never be killed, O descendant of Bharata.
Wherefore thou oughtst not to grieve about any
creature.

खधर्ममापे चावेल्य न बिकम्पितुमईसि ।
धर्म्याद्धि युद्धाच्छ्रेयोऽन्यत्क्षत्रियस्य न विद्यते ॥ ३१ ॥

Having regard to thine own duty also,
thou oughtst not to waver. For, to a Kshatriya,
there is nothing more wholesome than a lawful
battle.

यदृच्छया चोपपन्नं खर्गद्वारमपावृतम् ।
सुखिन: क्षत्रिया: पार्थ लभन्ते युद्धमीदृशम् ।। ३२ ॥

Happy Kshatriya, O son of Prithâ, find
such a battle as this, come of itself, an open door
to heaven.

अथ चेत्त्वमिर्मं घर्म्यं सङ्गामं न करिष्यसि ।
ततः स्वधर्मं कीर्तिं च हित्वा पापमवाप्स्यसि ॥ ३३ ॥

Now if thou wouldst not fight this lawful
battle, then, having abandoned thine own duty and
fame, thou shalt incur sin.
If, on the other hand, you will not fight this battle which
is enjoined on you as a duty, and which is not opposed to
Law, you will, by neglecting this battle, have abandoned
your duty and lost the fame that you acquired by your
encounter with such persons as Mahadeva. * Thus you will
only incur sin.

अर्कार्तिं चापि भूतानि कथयिष्यन्ति तेऽव्ययाम्।
सम्भावितस्य चाक्रीर्तिर्मरणादतिरिच्यते ॥ ३४ ॥

People, too, will recount thy everlasting
infamy; and, to one who has been esteemed, infamy
is more than death.

भयाद्रणादुपरतं मंस्यन्ते त्वां महारथाः।
येषां च त्वं बहुमतो भूत्वा यास्यसि लाघवम् ॥ ३५ ॥

The great car-warriors will think thou
hast withdrawn from the battle through fear; and,
having been (hitherto) highly esteemed by them,
thou wilt incur their contempt.

अवाच्यवादांश्च बहून्वदिष्यन्ति तवाहिताः ।
निन्दन्तस्तव सामर्थ्यं ततो दुःखतरं नु किम ॥ ३६ ॥

Thy enemies, too, scorning thy power,
will take many abusive words. What is more pain-
ful than that ?

हतो वा प्राप्ससि खर्ग जित्वा वा भोक्ष्यसे महीम ।
तस्मादुत्तिष्ठ कौन्तेय युद्धाय कृतनिश्चयः ॥ ३० ॥

Killed, thou wilt reach heaven; victorious,
thou wilt enjoy the earth. Wherefore, O son of
Kunti, arise, resolved to fight.

सुखदुःखे समे कृत्वा लाभालाभौ जयाजयौ ।
ततो युद्धाय युज्यस्व नैवं पापमवाप्स्यसि ॥ ३८ ॥

Then, treating alike pleasure and pain,
gain and loss, success and defeat, prepare for the
battle, and thus wilt thou not incur sin.

एषा तेऽभिहिता साङ्खये बुद्धिर्योगे त्विमां ऋणु ।
बुध्या युक्तो यया पार्थ कर्मबन्धं प्रहास्यसि ॥ ३९ ॥

This, which has been taught to thee is
wisdom concerning Sankhya. Now listen to wisdom
concerning Yoga, which possessing thou shalt cast
off the bond of action.

नेहाभिक्रमनाशोऽस्ति प्रखवायो न विद्यते ।
खल्पमप्यस्य धर्मस्य त्रायते महतो भयात ॥ ४० ॥

There is no loss of effort here, there is no
harm. Even a little of this devotion delivers one
from great fear.

व्यवसायात्मिका बुद्धिरेकेह कुरुनन्दन ।
बहुशाखा ह्यनन्ताश्च बुद्धयोऽव्यवसायिनाम् ॥ ४१ ॥

Here, O son of Kuru, there is one thought
of a resolute nature. Many-branched and endless
are the thoughts of the irresolute.

यामिमां पुष्पितां वाचं प्रवदन्त्यविपश्चितः ।
वेदवादरता: पार्थं नान्यदस्तीति वादिन: ।। ४२ ।।

कामात्मानः स्वर्गपरा जन्मकर्मफलप्रदाम् ।
क्रियाविशेषबहुलां भोगैश्वर्यगतिं प्रति ।। ४३ ।।

भोगैश्वर्यप्रसक्तानां तयाऽपहृतचेतसाम् ।
व्यवसायात्मिका बुद्धिः समाघौ न विधीयते ॥ ४४ ॥

42-44. No conviction of a resolute nature is
formed in the mind of those who are attached to
pleasures and power, and whose minds are drawn
away by that flowery speech which the unwise-
enamoured of Vedic utterances, declaring there is
nothing else, full of desire, having svarga as their
goal-utter, (a speech) which promises birth as
the reward of actions and which abounds in speci-
fic acts for the attainment of pleasure and power,
O son of Pritha.

त्रैगुण्यविषया वेदा निस्नैगुण्यो भवार्जुन ।
निर्द्वन्द्वो नित्यसत्त्वस्थो निर्योगक्षेम आत्मवान् ॥ ४५ ॥

The Vedas treat of the triad of the
gunas. Be, O Arjuna, free from the triad of the
gunas, free from pairs, free from acquisition and
preservation, ever remaining in the Sattva (Good-
ness), and self-possessed.

याबानर्थ उदपाने सर्वतः सम्प्छतोदके ।
तावान्सर्वेषु वेदेषु ब्राह्मणस्य विजानतः ॥ ४६ ॥

What utility there is in a reservoir by
the side of an all-spreading flood of water, the same
(utility) there is in all Vedas for an enlightened
Brahmana.

कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन ।
मा कर्मफल्रहेतुर्भूमा ते सङ्गोऽस्त्वकर्मणि ॥ ४७ ॥

Thy concern is with action alone, never
with results. Let not the fruit of action be thy
motive, nor let thy attachment be for inaction.

योगस्थः कुरु कर्माणि सङ्गं त्यक्तवा घनञ्जय ।
सिद्धचासिच्द्योः समो भूत्वा समत्वं योग उच्यते ॥ ४८ ॥

Steadfast in devotion do thy works, O
Dhanamjaya, casting off attachment, being the
same in success and failure. Evenness is called
Yoga.

दूरेण ह्यवरं कर्म बुद्धियोगाद्धनञ्जय।
बुद्धौ शरणमन्त्रिच्छ कृपणाः फलहेतवः ॥ ४९ ॥

Verily action is far inferior to devotion in
wisdom (buddhi-yoga), O Dhanamjaya. In wisdom
(buddhi) seek thou shelter. Wretched are they
whose motive is the fruit.

बुद्धियुक्तो जहातीह उमे सुक्कतदुष्कृते ।
तस्माद्योगाय युज्यस्व योगः कर्मसु कौशलम् ॥ ५० ॥

He who is endued with wisdom casts off
here both good deeds and bad deeds. Wherefore
apply thyself to devotion. In regard to actions
devotion is a power.

कर्मजं बुद्धियुक्ता हि फलं त्यक्त्वा मनीषिणः ।
जन्मबन्धविनिर्मुक्ताः पदं गच्छन्त्यनामयम् ॥ ५१ ॥

For, men of wisdom cast off the fruit of
action; possessed of knowledge (and) released from
the bond of birth, they go to the place where there
is no evil.

यदा ते मोहकछिछं बुद्धिर्व्यतितरिष्यति ।
तदा गन्तासि निर्वेदं श्रोतव्यस्य श्रुतस्य च ॥ ५२ ॥

When thy mind shall cross beyond the
mire of delusion, then wilt thou attain to a disgust
of what is yet to be heard and what has been heard.

श्रुतिविप्रतिपन्ना ते यदा स्थास्यति निश्चला ।
समाधावचळा बुद्धिस्तदा योगमवाप्स्यासि ॥ ५३ ॥

When thy mind, perplexed by what thou
hast heard, shall stand firm and steady in the
Self, then wilt thou attain Yoga.

श्चितप्रज्ञस्य का भाषा समाधिस्वस्य केशव ।
स्थितधीः किं प्रभाषेत किमासीत ब्रजेत किम् ॥ ५४ ॥

What, O Kesava!, is the description of
one of steady knowledge, who is constant in con-
templation? How does one of steady knowledge
speak, how sit, how move?

प्रजहाति यदा कामान्सर्वान्पार्थ मनोगतान् ।
आत्मन्येवात्मना तुष्टः स्थितप्रज्ञस्तदोच्यते ॥ ५५ ॥

When a man, satisfied in the Self alone
by himself, completely casts off all the desires of
the mind, then is he said to be one of steady
knowledge.

दुःखेष्वनुद्विग्नमनाः सुखेषु विगतस्पृहः ।
वीतरागभयक्रोघः स्थितधीर्मुनिरुच्यते ॥ ५६ ॥

He whose heart is not distressed in cala-
mities, from whom all longing for pleasures has
departed, who is free from attachment, fear and
wrath, he is called a sage, a man of steady know-
ledge.

यः सर्वत्रानभिस्नेहस्तत्तत्प्राप्य ग्लभाञ्चभम् ।
नाभिनन्दति न द्वेष्टि तस्य प्रज्ञा प्रतिष्ठिता ॥ ५७ ॥

Whoso, without attachment anywhere,
on meeting with anything good or bad, neither
exults nor hates, his knowledge becomes steady.

यदा संहरते चायं कूर्मोऽङ्गानीव सर्वशः ।
इन्द्रियाणीन्द्रियार्थेभ्यस्तस्य प्रज्ञा प्रतिष्ठिता ॥ ५८ ॥

When he completely withdraws the senses
from sense-objects, as the tortoise (withdraws)
its limbs from all sides, his knowledge is steady.

विषया विनिवर्तन्ते निराहारस्य देहिनः ।
रसवर्जं रसोऽप्यस्य परं दृष्ट्वा निवर्तते ॥ ५९ ॥

Objects withdraw from an abstinent man,
but not the taste. On seeing the Supreme, his
taste, too, ceases.

यततो ह्यपि कौन्तेय पुरुषस्य विपश्चितः ।
इन्द्रियानि प्रमाथीनि हरन्ति प्रसमं मनः ॥ ६० ॥

The dangerous senses, O son of Kunti,
forcibly carry away the mind of a wise man, even
while striving (to control them).

तानि सर्वाणि संयम्य युक्त आसीत मत्पर: ।
वशे हि यस्येन्द्रियाणि तस्य प्रज्ञा प्रतिष्ठिता ॥ ६१ ॥

Restraining them all, a man should
remain steadfast, intent on Me. His knowledge
is steady whose senses are under control.

ध्यायतो विषयान्पुंसः सङ्गस्तेषूपजायते ।
सङ्गात्सञ्जायते कामः कामात्क्रोघोऽभिजायते ॥ ६२ ॥

When a man thinks of objects, attachment
for them arises. From attachment arises desire;
from desire arises wrath.

ऋोधाद्भवति सम्मोहः सम्मोहात्स्मृतिविभ्रमः ।
स्मृतिभ्रंशाद्बुद्धिनाशो बुद्धिनाशात्प्रणत्रयति ॥ ६३ ॥

From wrath arises delusion; from delu-
sion, failure of memory; from failure of memory,
loss of conscience; from loss of conscience he is
utterly ruined.

रागद्वेषवियुक्तस्तु विषयानिन्द्रियैश्चरन् ।
आत्मवरयैर्विधेयात्मा प्रसादमधिगच्छति ॥ ३४ ॥

He attains peace, who, self-controlled,
approaches objects with the senses devoid of love
and hatred and brought under his own-control.

प्रसादे सर्वदुःखानां हानिरस्योपजायते ।
प्रसन्नचेतसो ह्याञ्च बुद्धिः पर्यवतिष्ठते ॥ ६५ ॥

In peace there is an end of all his miseries;
for, the reason of the tranquil-minded soon be-
comes steady.

नास्ति बुद्धिरयुक्तस्य न चायुक्तस्य भावना ।
न चाभावयतः शान्तिरशान्तस्य कुतः सुखम् ॥ ६६ ॥

There is no wisdom to the unsteady, and
no meditation to the unsteady, and to the unmedi-
tative no peace; to the peaceless, how can there
be happiness?

इन्द्रियाणां हि चरतां यन्मनोऽनु विधीयते ।
तदस्य हरति प्रज्ञां वायुर्नावामिवाम्भासि ॥ ६७ ॥

For, the mind which yields to the roving
senses carries away his knowledge, as the wind
(carries away) a ship on water.

तस्माद्यस्य महाबाहो निगृहीतानि सर्वशः ।
इन्द्रियाणीन्द्रियार्थेम्यस्तस्य प्रज्ञा प्रतिष्ठिता ।। ६८ ॥

Therefore, O mighty-armed, his knowledge
is steady whose senses have been entirely restrain-
ed from sense-objects.

या निशा सर्वभूतानां तस्यां जागर्ति संयमी ।
यस्यां जान्नति भूतानि सा निशा परयतो मुनेः ॥ ६९ ॥

What is night to all beings, therein the
self controlled one is awake. Where all beings are
awake, that is the night of the sage who sees.

आपूर्यमाणमचलप्रतिष्ठं
समुद्रमापः प्रविशन्ति यद्वत् ।
तद्वत्कामा यं प्रविज्ञान्ति सर्वे
स शान्तिमाप्नोति न कामकामी ॥ ७० ॥

He attains peace, into whom all desires
enter as waters enter the ocean, which, filled from
all sides, remains unaltered ; but not he who desires
objects.

विहाय कामान्य: सर्वान्पुमांश्चरति निःस्पृहः ।
निर्ममो निरहङ्कारः स शान्तिमधिगच्छति ॥ ७१ ॥

That man attains peace, who, abandoning
all desires, moves about without attachment, with-
out selfishness, without vanity.

एषा बाह्मी स्थिति: पार्थ नैनां प्राप्य विमुह्यति ।
स्थित्वाऽस्यामन्तकाळेऽपि ब्रह्म निर्वाणमृच्छति ॥ ७२ ॥

This is the Brahmic state, O son of Pri-
tha. Attaining to this, none is deluded. Remaining
in this state even at the last period of life, one at-
tains to the felicity of Brahman.

chapter 3

KARMA-YOGA.

ज्यायसी चेत्कर्मणस्ते मता बुद्धिर्जनार्दन ।
तर्तिक कर्माणे घोरे मां नियोजयसि केशव ॥ १ ॥

If it be thought by Thee that knowledge
is superior to action, O Janardana, why then dost
Thou, O Kesava, direct me to this terrible action?

व्यामिश्रेणेव वाक्येन बुद्धिं मोहयसीव मे ।
तदेकं वद निश्चिल येन श्रेयोऽहमाप्तुयाम् ॥ २ ॥

With an apparently perplexing speech,
Thou confusest as it were my understanding. Tell
me with certainty that one (way) by which.I may
attain bliss.

लोकेऽस्मिन्द्विविघा निष्ठा पुरा पोक्ता मयाऽनव ।
ज्ञानयोगेन साङ्खयानां कर्मयोगेन योगिनाम् ॥ ३ ॥

In this world a twofold path was taught
by Me at first, O sinless one: that of Sankhyas by
devotion to knowledge, and that of Yogins by
devotion to action.

न कर्मणामनारम्भान्नैष्कर्म्यं पुरुषोऽइनुते ।
न च संन्यसनादेव सिद्धिं समधिगच्छति ॥ ४ ॥

Not by abstaining from action does man
win actionlessness, nor by mere renunciation does
he attain perfection.

न हि कश्चित्क्षणमपि जात तिष्ठयकर्मकत ।
कार्यते ह्यवशः कर्म सर्वः प्रकृतिजैर्गुणैः ॥ ५ ॥

None, verily, even for an instant, ever
remains doing no action; for every one is driven
helpless to action by the energies born of Nature.

कर्मेन्द्रियाणि संयम्य य आस्ते मनसा स्मरन् ।
इन्द्रियार्थान्विमूढात्मा मिथ्याचारः स उच्यते ॥ ६ ॥

He who, restraining the organs of action,
sits thinking in his mind of the objects of the
senses, self-deluded, he is said to be one of false
conduct.

यस्त्विन्द्रियाणि मनसा नियम्यारभतेऽर्जुन ।
कर्मेन्दियैः कर्मयोगमसक्तः स विशिष्यते ॥ ७ ॥

But whoso, restraining the senses by mind
O Arjuna, engages in Karma-Yoga, unattached,
with organs of action, he is esteemed.

नियतं करु कर्म त्वं कर्म ज्यायो ह्यकर्मणः ।
शरीरयात्राऽपि च ते न प्रसिघ्येदकर्मण: ॥ ८ ॥

Do thou perform (thy) bounden duty; for
action is superior to inaction. And even the main-
tenance of the body would not be possible for thee
by inaction.

यज्ञार्थात्कर्मणोऽन्यत्र लोकोऽयं कर्मबन्धनः ।
तद्र्थं कर्म कौन्तेय मुक्तसङ्गः समाचर ॥ ९ ॥

Except in the case of action for Sacrifice's
sake, this world is action-bound. Action for the
sake Thereof, do thou, O son of Kunti, perform,
free from attachment.

सहयज्ञाः प्रजाः सृष्टा पुरोवाच प्रजापतिः ।
अनेन प्रसाविष्यव्वमेष वोऽस्त्विष्टकामघुक्रु ॥ १० ॥

Having first created mankind together
with sacrifices, the Prajapati said, "By this shall
ye propagate; let this be to you the cow of plenty.

देवान्भावयतानेन ते देवा भावयन्तु वः ।
परस्परं भावयन्तः श्रेयः परमवाप्स्यथ ॥ ११ ॥

With this do ye nourish the Gods, and
the Gods shall nourish you; thus nourishing one
another, ye shall attain the supreme good.

इष्टान्भोगान्हि वो देवो दास्यन्ते यज्ञभाविताः ।
तैर्दत्तानप्रदायैभ्यो यो सुइके स्तेन एव सः ॥ १२ ॥

"Nourished by the sacrifice, the Gods
shall indeed bestow on you the enjoyments ye
desire." Whoso enjoys-without offering to Them
-Their gifts, he is verily a thief.

यज्ञशिष्टाशिनः सन्तो मुच्यन्ते सर्वाकलिबर्षेः ।
अञ्जते ते त्वघं पापा ये पचन्त्यात्मकारणात् ॥ १३ ॥

The righteous, who eat the remnant of
the sacrifice, are freed from all sins; but sin do the
impious eat who cook for their own sakes.

अन्नाद्भवन्ति भूतानि पर्जन्यादन्नसम्भवः ।
यज्ञाद्भवति पर्जन्यो यज्ञः कर्मसमुद्भवः ॥ १४ ॥

कर्म ब्रह्मोद्भवं विद्धि ब्रह्माक्षरसमुद्भवम् ।
तस्मात्सर्वगतं ब्रह्म नित्यं यज्ञे प्रतिष्ठितम् ॥ १५ ॥

14-15. From food creatures come forth; the
production of food is from rain; rain comes forth
from sacrifice; sacrifice is born of action; know
thou that action comes from Brahman, and that
Brahman comes from the Imperishable. There-
fore, the all-pervading Brahman ever rests in
sacrifice.

एवं प्रवर्तितं चक्रं नानुवर्तयतीह यः ।
अघायुरिन्द्रियारामो मोघं पार्थं स जीवति ॥ १६ ॥

He who follows not here the wheel thus
set in motion, who is of sinful life, indulging in
senses, he lives in vain, O son of Pritha.

यस्त्वात्मरतिरेव स्यादात्मनृप्तश्च मानवः ।
आत्मन्येव च सन्तुष्टस्तस्य कार्यं न विद्यते ॥ १७ ॥

That man, verily, who rejoices only in
the Self, who is satisfied with the Self, who is con-
tent in the Self alone,-for him there is nothing
to do.

नैव तस्य कृतेनार्थो नाकृतेनेह कश्चन ।
न चास्य सर्वभूतेषु कश्चिद्र्थेव्यपाश्रयः ॥ १८ ॥

For him, there is here no interest what-
ever in what is done or what is not done. Nor is
there in all beings any one ne should resort to for
any object.

तस्मादसक्त: सततं कार्यं कर्म समावर ।
असक्तो ह्याचरन्कर्म परमाप्नोति पुरुषः ॥ १९ ॥

Therefore, without attachment, constant-
ly perform the action which should be done; for,
performing action without attachment, man reaches
the Supreme.

कर्मणैव हि संसिद्धिमास्थिता जनकादयः ।
लोकसंङ्ग्रहमेवापि सम्परयन्कर्तुमर्हसि ॥ २० ॥

By action only, indeed, did Janaka and
others try to attain perfection. Even with a view
to the protection of the masses thou shouldst per-
form (action).

यद्याचरति श्रेष्ठस्तत्तदेवेतरो जनः ।
स यत्प्रमाणं कुरुते लोकस्तद्नुवर्तते ॥ २१ ॥

Whatsoever a great man does, that alone
the other men do; whatever he sets up as the
standard, that the world follows.

न मे पार्थास्ति कर्तव्यं त्रिषु लोकेषु किञ्चन ।
नानवाप्तमवाप्तव्यं वर्ते एव च कर्मणि ॥ २२ ॥

I have nothing whatsoever to achieve in
the three worlds, O son of Pritha, nor is there any-
thing unattained that should be attained; yet I
engage in action.

यदि ह्यहं न वर्तेयं जातु कर्मण्यतन्द्रितः ।
मम वत्मीनुवर्तन्ते मनुष्याः पार्थं सर्वेशः ॥ २३ ॥

For, should I not ever engage in action,
unwearied, men would in all matters follow My
path, O son of Pritha.

उत्सीदेयरिमे लोका न कुर्यां कर्म चेदहम् ।
सङ्करस्य च कर्ता स्यामपहन्यामिमाः प्रजाः ॥ २४ ॥

These worlds would be ruined if I should
not perform action; I should be the cause of
confusion of castes, and should destroy these
creatures.

सक्ताः कर्मण्यविद्वांसो यथा कुर्वन्ति भारत ।
कुर्याद्विद्वांस्तथाऽसक्तश्चिकीर्कुलेकसङ्ग्हम् ॥ २५ ॥

As ignorant men act attached to work, O
Bhârata, so should the wise man act, unattached
from a wish to protect the masses.

न बुद्धिभेदं जनयेदज्ञानां कर्मसङ्गिनाम् ।
जोषयेत्सर्वकर्माणि विद्वान्युक्तः समाचरन् ॥ २६ ॥

Let no wise man cause unsettlement in
the minds of the ignorant who are attached to
action; he should make them do all actions, him-
self fulfilling them with devotion.

प्रकृते: क्रियमाणानि गुणैः कर्माणि सर्वेशः ।
अहङ्काराविमूढात्मा कर्ताऽहमिति मन्यते ॥ २७ ॥

Actions are wrought in all cases by the
energies of Nature. He whose mind is deluded
by egoism thinks 'I am the doer.'

तत्त्ववितु महाबाहो गुणकर्मविभागयोः ।
गुणा गुणेषु वर्तन्त इति मत्वा न सज्जते ॥ २८ ॥

But he who knows the truth, O mighty-
armed, about the divisions of the energies and
(their) functions, is not attached, thinking that the
energies act upon the energies.

प्रकृतेर्गुणसम्मूढाः सज्जन्ते गुणकर्मसु ।
तानकरस्रविदो मन्दान्करस्रविन्न विचालयेत् ॥ २९ ॥

Those deluded by the energies of Nature
are attached to the functions of the energies.
He
who knows the All should not unsettle the unwise
who know not the All.

मयि सर्वाणि कर्माणि संन्यस्याध्यात्मचेतसा ।
निराशीनिर्ममो भूत्वा युध्यस्व विगतब्बरः ॥ ३० ॥

Renouncing all action in Me, with thy
thought resting on the Self, being free from hope,
free from selfishness, devoid of fever, do thou
fight.

ये मे मतमिदं नित्यमनुतिष्ठन्ति मानवाः ।
श्रद्धावन्तोऽनसूयन्तो मुच्यन्ते तेऽपि कर्मभिः ॥ ३१ ॥

Men who constantly practise this teach-
ing of Mine with faith and without cavilling, they
too are liberated from actions.

ये त्वेतदभ्यसूयन्तो नानुतिष्ठन्ति मे मतम् ।
सर्वज्ञानविमूढांस्तान्विद्धि नष्टानचेतसः ॥ ३२ ॥

But those who, carping at this, My
teaching, practise it not,-know them as deluded
in all knowledge, as senseless men doomed to
destruction.

सदृशं चेष्टते खस्याः प्रकृतेज्ञीनवानपि ।
प्रकृतिं यान्ति भूतानि निन्नहः किं करिष्यति ॥ ३३ ॥

Even the man of knowledge acts in con-
formity with his own nature; (all) beings follow
(their) nature; what shall coercion avail?

इन्द्रियस्योन्द्रियस्यार्थे रागद्वेषौ व्यवस्थितौ ।
तयोर्न वज्ञमागच्छेत्तौ ह्यस्य परिपन्थिनौ ॥ ३४ ॥

Love and hate lie towards the object of
each sense; let none become subject to these two;
for, they are his enemies.

श्रेयान्स्वधर्मो विगुणः परधर्मात्स्वनुष्ठितात् ।
खधर्मे निधनं श्रेयः परधर्मो भयावहः ॥ ३५ ॥

Better one's own duty, though devoid of
merit, than the duty of another well discharged.
Better is death in one's own duty; the duty of
another is productive of danger.

अथ केन प्रयुक्तोऽयं पापं चरति पूरुषः ।
अनिच्छन्नापि वार्ष्णेय बलादिव नियोजितः ॥ ३६ ॥

But by what dragged on, O Varshneya,
does a man, though reluctant, commit sin, as if
constrained by force ?

काम एष क्रोध एष रजोगुणसमुद्भवः ।
महाज्ञनो महापाप्मा विद्ध्येनमिह वैरिणम् ॥ ३७ ॥

It is desire, it is wrath, born of the
energy of Rajas, all-devouring, all sinful; that,
know thou, is the foe here.

धूमेनात्रियते वह्रिर्यथाऽऽदर्शो मलेन च ।
ं ययोस्बेनावृतो गर्भस्तया तेनेदमावृतम् ॥ ३८ ॥

As fire is surrounded by smoke, as a
mirror by rust, as the foetus is enclosed in the
womb, so is this covered by it.

आवतं ज्ञानमेतेन ज्ञानिनो नित्यवैरिणा ।
कामरूपेण कौन्तेय दुष्पूरेणानलेन च ॥ ३९ ॥

Covered, O son of Kunti, is wisdom by
this constant enemy of the wise, in the form of
desire, which is greedy and insatiable.

इन्द्रियाणि मनो बुद्धिरस्याधिष्ठानमुच्यते ।
एतैर्विमोहयत्वेष ज्ञानमावृत्य देहिनम् ॥ ४० ॥

The senses, mind, and reason are said to
be its seat; veiling wisdom through these, it
deludes the embodied.

तस्मास्वमिन्द्रियाण्यादौ नियम्य भरतर्षभ ।
पाप्सानं प्रजहि ह्येनं ज्ञानविज्ञाननाञ्चनम् ॥ ४१ ॥

Therefore, O lord of the Bharatas, restrain
the senses first, do thou cast off this sinful thing
which is destructive of knowledge and wisdom.

इन्द्रियाणि पराण्याहुरिन्द्रियेम्यः परं मनः ।
मनसस्तु परा बुद्धिर्यो बुद्धेः परतस्तुं सः ॥ ४२ ॥

They say that the senses are superior ;
superior to the senses is mind; superior to mind
is reason; one who is even superior to reason is He.

एवं बुद्धेः परं बुद्धा संस्तभ्यात्मानमात्मना ।
जहि शत्रुं महाबाहो कामरूपं दुरासदम् ॥ ४३ ॥

Then knowing Him who is superior to
reason, subduing the self by the self, slay thou, O
mighty-armed, the enemy in the form of desire.
hard to conquer.

JNANA-YOGA.

इमं विवस्वते योगं प्रोक्तवानहमव्ययम् ।
विवस्वान्मनवे प्राह मनुरिङ्वाकवेऽन्रवीत् ॥ १ ॥

I taught this imperishable Yoga to Vivas-
vat; Vivasvat taught it to Manu; Manu taught to
Ikshvâku.

एवं परम्पराप्राप्तमिमं राजर्षयो विदुः ।
स काल्रेनेह महता योगो नष्ट: परन्तप ॥ २ ॥

This, handed down thus in succession, the
King-sages learnt, This Yoga, by long lapse of
time, has been lost here, O harasser of foes.

स एवाऽयं मया तेऽद्य योगः प्रोक्तः पुरातनः ।
भक्तोऽसि मे सखा चेति रहस्यं ह्येतदुत्तमम् ॥ ३ ॥

That same ancient Yoga has been to-day
taught to thee by Me, seeing that thou art My
devotee and friend; for, this is the Supreme
Secret.

अपरं भवतो जन्म परं जन्म विवस्वत: ।
कथमेतद्विजानीयां त्वमादौ प्रोक्तवानिति ॥ ४ ॥

Later is Thy birth, and prior the birth of
Vivasvat; how am I to understand that Thou
taughtest this Yoga in the beginning ?

बहूनि मे व्यतीतानि जन्मानि तव चार्जुन ।
तान्यहं वेद सर्वाणि न त्वं वेत्थ परन्तप ॥ ५ ॥

Many births of Mine have passed, as well
as of thine, O Arjuna; all these I know, thou know-
es not, O harasser of foes.

अजोऽपि सन्नव्ययात्मा भूतानामीश्वरोऽपि सन् ।
प्रकृतिं स्वामधिष्ठाय सम्भवाम्यात्ममायया ॥ ६ ॥

Though I am unborn, of imperishable
nature, and though I am the Lord of all beings,
yet ruling over My own nature, I am born by My
own Mâyâ.

यदा यदा हि घर्मस्य ग्लानिर्भवति भारत ।
अम्युत्थानमधर्मस्य तदाऽऽत्मानं सृजाम्यहम् ॥ ७ ॥

Whenever there is a decay of religion, O
Bharata, and an ascendency of irreligion, then I
manifest Myself.

परित्राणाय साधूनां विनाशाय च दुष्कृताम् ।
धर्मसंस्थापनार्थाय सम्भवामि युगे युगे ॥ ८ ॥

For the protection of the good, for the
destruction of evil-doers, for the firm establishment
of religion, I am born in every age.

जन्म कम च मे दिव्यमेवं यो वेत्ति तत्त्वतः ।
त्यक्तवा देहं पुनर्जन्म नैति मामेति सोऽर्जुन ॥ ९ ॥

Whoso knows thus My divine birth and
action in truth is not born again on leaving this
body; he comes to Me, O Arjuna.

वीतरागभयकोधा मन्मया मामुपाश्रिताः ।
बहवो ज्ञानतपसा पूता मद्भावमागताः ॥ १० ॥

Free from passion, fear and anger,
absorbed in Me, taking refuge in Me, purified by
the fire (tapas) of wisdom, many have reached My
being.

ये यथा मां प्रपद्यन्ते तांस्तथैव भजाम्यहम् ।
मम वत्मोनुवर्तन्ते मनुष्याः पार्थं सर्वेशः ॥ ११ ॥

Howsoever men approach Me, even so
do I reward them; My path do men follow in all
things O son of Pritha.

काङ्कन्तः कर्मणां सिद्धिं यजन्त इह देवताः ।
क्षिपं हि मानुषे लोके सिद्धिर्मवति कर्मजा ॥ १२ ॥

They who long after success in actions
sacrifice here to the Gods : for, soon in this world
of man accrues success from action.

चातुर्वर्ण्यं मया सृष्टं गुणकर्मविभागशः ।
तस्य कर्तारमपि मां विख्यकर्तारमव्ययम् ॥ १३ ॥

The fourfold caste has been created by
according to the distribution of energies and
ons; though I am the author thereof, know Me
on-agent and immutable.

न मां कर्माणि लिम्पन्ति न मे कर्मफले स्पूहा ।
इति मां योऽभिजानाति कर्मेभिने स बख्यते ॥ १४ ॥

Actions pollute Me not, nor have I a
desire for the fruit of actions. He who knows Me
thus is not bound by actions.

एवं ज्ञात्वा कृतं कर्म पूर्वेरपि मुमुक्षभिः ।
कुरु कमैंव तस्मारवं पूर्वे: पूर्वतरं कृतम् ॥ १५ ॥

Thus knowing, men of old performed ac-
tion in the hope of liberation: therefore do thou
also perform action as did the ancients in the olden
time.

किं कर्म किमकर्मेति कवयोऽप्यत्र मोहिताः ।
तत्ते कर्म प्रवक्ष्यामि यज्ज्ञात्वा मोक्ष्यसेऽग्लभात् ॥ १६ ॥

What is action? What is inaction-As
to this, even the wise are deluded I shall teach
thee such action, by knowing which thou shalt be
liberated from evil.

कर्मणो ह्यपि बोद्धव्यं बोद्धव्यं च विकर्मणः ।
अकर्मणश्च बोद्धव्यं गहना कर्मणो गतिः ॥ १७ ॥

For, thou hast to know something even
of action, something to know of unlawful action,
and something to know of inaction; hard to under-
stand is the nature of action.

कर्मण्यकर्म यः पत्रयेदकर्माणि च कर्म यः ।
स बुद्धिमान्मनुष्येषु स युक्तः क्रत्स्नकर्मकृत् ॥ १८ ॥

He who can see inaction in action, who can
also see action in inaction, he is wise among men,
he is devout, he is the performer of all action.

यस्य सर्वे समारम्भाः कामसङ्कल्पवर्जिताः ।
ज्ञानाग्निदग्धकर्माणं तमाड्डः पण्डितं बुघाः ॥ १९ ॥

He whose engagements are all devoid of
desires and purposes, and whose actions have been
burnt by the fire of wisdom, him the wise call a
sage.

त्यक्त्वा कर्मफलासङ्गं नित्यनृप्तो निराश्रयः ।
कर्मण्याभिप्रवृत्तोऽपि नैव किञ्चित्करोति सः ॥ २० ॥

Having abandoned attachment for the
fruits of action, ever content, dependent on none,
though engaged in actions, nothing at all does he do.

निराशीर्यतचित्तात्मा त्यक्तसर्वपरिप्रहः ।
ज्ञारीरं केवलं कर्म कुर्वन्नाप्नोति किल्बिषम् ॥ २१ ॥

Free from desire, with the mind and the
self controlled, having relinquished all possessions
doing mere bodily action, he incurs no sin.

यहच्छालाभसन्तुष्टो द्वन्द्वातीतो विमत्सरः ।
समः सिद्धावसिद्धौ च कृत्वाऽपि न निबद्ध्यते ॥ २२ ॥

Satisfied with what comes to him by
chance, rising above the pairs of opposites, free
from envy, equanimous in success and failure,
though acting he is not bound.

गतसङ्गस्य मुक्तस्य ज्ञानावस्थितचेतसः ।
यज्ञायाचरतः कर्म समयं प्रविछीयते ॥ २३ ॥

Of the man whose attachment is gone,
who is liberated, whose mind is established in know-
ledge, who acts for the sake of sacrifice,—his whole
action melts away.

ब्रह्मार्पणं ब्रह्महविब्रह्मामौ ब्रह्मणा हुतम् ।
ब्रह्मैव तेन गन्तव्यं ब्रह्मकर्मसमाधिना ॥ २४ ॥

Brahman is the offering, Brahman the
oblation; by Brahman is the oblation poured into
the fire of Brahman; Brahman verily shall be
reached by him who always sees Brahman in
action.

दैवमेवापरे यज्ञं योगिनः पर्युपासते ।
ब्रह्माप्रावपरे यज्ञं यज्ञेनैवोपजुह्वति ॥ २५ ॥

Other yogins resort to sacrifices to Gods:
in the fire of Brahman others offer the Self by the
Self.

श्रोत्राद्गिनीन्द्रियाण्यन्ये संयमाग्निषु जुह्वति ।
शब्दादीन्विषयानन्य इन्द्रियाग्निषु जुह्वति ॥ २६ ॥

Others offer hearing and other senses in
the fires of restraint; others offer sound and other
objects in the fires of the senses.

सर्वाणीन्द्रियकर्माणि प्राणकर्माणि चापरे ।
आत्मसंयमयोगामौ जुह्वति ज्ञानदीपिते ॥ २७ ॥

And others sacrifice all the functions of
the senses and the functions of the vitality in the
wisdom-kindled fire of the Yoga of Self-restraint.

द्रव्ययज्ञास्तपायज्ञा यागयज्ञास्तथाऽपरे ।
स्वाध्यायज्ञानयज्ञाश्च यतयः संशितत्रताः ॥ २८ ॥

THE BHAGAVAD GITA.
Others are sacrificers by their wealth,
sacrificers by austerity, sacrificers by Yogas, sacri-
ficers by reading and knowledge, ascetics of rigid
vows.

अपाने जुह्वति प्राणं प्राणेऽपानं तथाऽपरे ।
प्राणापानगती रुव्वा प्राणायामपरायणाः ॥ २९ ॥

Others offer pråna (outgoing breath) in
apâna (incoming breath), and apâna in prâna,
restraining the passages of prâna and apâna,
absorbed in prânâyâma (restraint of breath).

अपरे नियताहाराः प्राणान्प्राणेषु जुह्वति ।
सर्वेऽप्येते यज्ञाविदो यज्ञक्षपितकल्मषाः ॥ ३० ॥

Others, with regulated food, offer life-
breaths in life-breaths. All these are knowers of
sacrifice, whose sins are destroyed by sacrifice.

यज्ञशिष्टामृतभुजो यान्ति ब्रह्म सनातनम् ।
नायं छोकोऽस्ययज्ञस्य कुतोऽन्यः कुरुसत्तम ॥ ३१ ॥

Eating of ambrosia, the remnant of the
sacrifice, they go to Eternal Brahman. This
world is not for the non-sacrificer; whence the
other?-O best of Kurus.

एवं बहुविधा यज्ञा वितता ब्रह्मणो मुखे ।
कर्मजान्विद्धि तान्सर्वानेवं ज्ञात्वा विमोस्यसे ॥ ३२ ॥

Thus manifold sacrifices are spread at
the mouth of Brahman. Know them all as born
of action. Thus knowing, thou shalt be liberated.

श्रेयान्द्रव्यमयाद्यज्ञाज्ज्ञानयज्ञ: परन्तप ।
सर्वं कर्माखिलं पार्थ ज्ञाने परिसमाप्यते ॥ ३३ ॥

Superior is wisdom-sacrifice to the sacri-
fice with objects, O harasser of thy foes. All
action, without exception, O son of Prithâ, is
comprehended in wisdom.

तद्विद्धि प्रणिपातेन परिप्रश्नेन सेवया ।
उपदेक्ष्यन्ति ते ज्ञानं ज्ञानिनस्तत्त्वदर्शिनः ॥ ३४ ॥

Know this: by long prostration, by
enquiry, by service, those men of wisdom who
have realised the truth will teach thee wisdom.

यज्ज्ञात्वा न पुनर्मोहमेवं यास्यसि पाण्डव ।
येन भूतान्यशेषेण द्रव्त्यस्यात्मन्यथो मयि ॥ ३५ ॥

Knowing which, thou shalt not again
thus fall into error, O Pandava; and by which,
thou wilt see all beings in thy Self and also in Me.

अपि चेदसि पापेम्यः सर्वेम्यः पापकृत्तमः ।
सर्वे।ज्ञानप्तवेनैन वृजिनं सन्तरिष्यसि ॥ ३६ ॥

Even shouldst thou be the most sinful of
all the sinful, thou shalt verily cross all sin by the
bark of wisdom.

How does wisdom destroy sin ?—Here is an example:
यथैघांसि समिद्घोऽग्निभस्ससात्कुरुतेऽजुन ।
ज्ञानाग्निः सर्वकर्माणि भस्मसात्कुरुते तथा ॥ ३७ ॥

As kindled fire reduces fuel to ashes, O
Arjuna, so does wisdom-fire reduce all actions to
ashes.

न हि ज्ञानेन सदृशं पवित्रमिह विद्यते ।
तत्खयं योगसंसिद्धः काल्रेनात्मनि विन्दति ॥ ३८ ॥

Verily, there exists here no purifier equal
to wisdom. He who is perfected by Yoga finds it
in time in himself by himself.

श्रद्धावाँल्लभते ज्ञानं तत्परः संयतेन्द्रियः ।
ज्ञानं लब्ब्वा परां शान्तिमचिरेणाधिगच्छति ॥ ३९ ॥

He obtains wisdom who is full of faith.
who is devoted to it, and who has subdued the
senses. Having obtained wisdom, he ere long
attains to the Supreme Peace.

अज्ञआश्रद्दधानश्च संशयात्मा विनश्यति ।
नायं लेकोऽस्ति न परो न सुखं संज्ञयात्मनः ॥ ४० ॥

The ignorant, the faithless, and one of
doubting self, is ruined. There is neither this
world, nor the other, nor happiness, for one of
doubting self.

योगसंन्यस्तकर्माणं ज्ञानसञ्ज्ञिसंशयम् ।
आत्मवन्तं न कर्माणि निबघ्नन्ति धनंजय ॥ ४१ ॥

Him who has renounced actions by
Yoga, whose doubts have been cloven asunder by
wisdom, who is self-possessed, actions bind not,
O Dhanamjaya.

तस्मादज्ञानसम्भूतं हृत्स्थं ज्ञानासिनाऽऽत्मनः ।
छित्त्वैनं संशयं योगमातिष्ठोत्तिष्ठ भारत ॥ ४२ ॥

Therefore with the sword of wisdom
cleave asunder this doubt of the Self lying in the
heart and born of ignorance, and resort to Yoga.
Arise, O Bharata.

chapter 5

SAMNYASA-YOGA

संन्यासं कर्मणां कृष्ण पुनर्योगं च शंललि ।
यच्छ्रेय एतयोरेकं तन्मे ब्रुहि सुनिश्चितम् ॥ १ ॥

Renunciation of actions, O Krishna, Thou
praisest, and again Yoga. Tell me conclusively
that which is the better of the two.

संन्यासः कर्मयोगश्च निःश्रेयसकरावुमौ ।
तयोस्तु कर्मसंन्यासात्कर्मयोगो विशिष्यते ॥ २ ॥

Renunciation and Yoga through action
both lead to the highest bliss; but, of the
two,
Yoga through action is esteemed more
than
renunciation of action.

ज्ञेय: स नित्यसंन्यासी यो न द्वेष्टि न कारूक्षाति ।
निर्द्वन्द्वो हि महाबाहो सुखं बन्धात्प्रमुच्यते ॥ ३ ॥

He should be known as a perpetual
renouncer who neither hates nor desires ; for, free
from the pairs of opposites, O mighty-armed, he
is easily set free from bondage.

साङ्ख्ययोगौ पृथग्बालाः प्रवदन्ति न पण्डिताः ।
एकमप्यास्थितः सम्यगुभयोर्विन्दते फलगु ॥ ४ ॥

Children, not the wise, speak of Sankhya
and Yoga as distinct. He who is rightly devoted
to even one obtains the fruits of both.

यत्साङ्ख्यैः प्राप्यते स्थानं तद्योगैरपि गम्यते ।
एकं साङ्ख्यं च योगं च यः पत्र्यति स पत्र्यति ॥ ५ ॥

That state which is reached by Sankhyas
is reached by Yogins also. He sees, who sees
Sankhya and Yoga as one.

संन्यासस्तु महाबाहो दुःखमाप्तुमयोगतः ।
योगयुक्तो मुनिब्रेह्म न चिरेणाधिगच्छति ॥ ६ ॥

But renunciation, O mighty-armed, is
hard to attain except by Yoga; a sage equipped
with Yoga ere long reaches Brahman.

योगयुक्तो विद्युद्धात्मा विजितात्मा जितेन्द्रियः ।
सर्वभूतात्मभूतात्मा कुर्वन्नवि न लिप्यते ॥ ७ ॥

He who is equipped with Yoga, whose mind
is quite pure, by whom the self has been conquer-
ed, whose senses have been subdued, whose Self
has become the Self of all beings,-though doing,
he is not tainted.

नैव किञ्चित्करोमीति युक्तो मन्येत तत्त्ववित् ।
पश्यन्श्युण्वन्स्पृशञ्जिघन्नश्रनगच्छन्श्वसन्स्वपन् ॥ ८ ॥

प्रलपन्विसृजन्गृहन्नुन्मिषन्निमिषन्नपि ।
इन्द्रियाणीन्द्रियार्थेषु वर्तन्त इति धारयन् ॥ ९ ॥

8-9. 'I do nothing at all'; thus would the
truth-knower think, steadfast,-though seeing,
hearing, touching, smelling, eating, going, sleeping,
breathing, speaking, letting go, seizing, opening and
closing the eyes,-remembering that the senses
move among sense-objects.

ब्रह्मण्याघाय कर्माणि सङ्गं लक्त्वा करोति यः ।
लिप्यते न स पापेन पद्मपत्रमिवाम्भला ॥ १० ॥

He who does actions, offering them to
Brahman, abandoning attachment, is not tainted
by sin, as a lotus leaf by water.

कायेन मनसा बुद्धचा केवलैरिन्द्रियैरपि ।
योगिनः कर्म कुर्वन्ति सङ्गं त्यक्त्वाऽऽत्मग्लुद्धये ॥ ११ ॥

By the body, by the mind, by the intellect,
by mere senses also, Yogins perform action, without
attachment, for the purification of the self.

युक्तः कर्मफलं त्यक्त्वा शान्तिमाप्नोति नैष्ठिकीम् ।
अयुक्तः कामकारेण फले सक्तो निबध्यते ॥ १२ ॥

The steady-minded one, abandoning the
fruit of action, attains the peace born of devotion.
The unsteady one, attached to the fruit through
the action of desire, is firmly bound.

सर्वकर्माणि मनसा सन्यस्यास्ते सुखं वशी ।
नवद्वारे पुरे देही नैव कुर्वन्न कारयन् ॥ १३ ॥

Renouncing all actions by thought, and
Self-controlled, the embodied one rests happily
in the nine-gated city, neither at all acting nor
causing to act.

न कर्तृत्वं न कर्माणि छोकस्य सृजति प्रमुः ।
न कर्मफळसंयोगं स्वभावस्तु प्रवर्तते ॥ १४ ॥

Neither agency nor objects does the
Lord create for the world, nor union with the fruits
of actions. But it is the nature that acts.

नादत्ते कस्यचित्पापं न चैव सुकृतं विभुः ।
अज्ञानेनावृतं ज्ञानं तेन मुद्यान्ति जन्तवः ॥ १५ ॥

The Lord takes neither the evil nor even
the good deed of any; wisdom is enveloped by un-
wisdom; thereby mortals are deluded.

ज्ञानेन तु तद्ज्ञानं येषां नाशितमात्मनः ।
तेषामादित्यवज्ज्ञानं प्रकाशयति तत्परम् ॥ १६ ॥

But to those whose unwisdom is destroy-
ed by wisdom of the Self, like the sun wisdom
illuminates that Supreme.

तद्बुद्धयस्तदात्मानस्तन्निष्ठास्तत्परायणाः ।
गच्छन्त्यपुनरावृत्तिं ज्ञाननिर्धूतकल्मषाः ॥ १७ ॥

With their consciousness in That, their
Self being That, intent on That, with That for
their supreme goal, they go never again to return,
their sins shaken off by means of wisdom.

विद्याविनयसम्पन्ने ब्राह्मणे गवि हस्तिनि ।
ञ्चानि चैव श्वपाके च पण्डिताः समदर्शिनः ॥ १८ ॥

In a Brahmana endued with wisdom and
humility, in a cow, in an elephant, as also in a dog
and in a dog-eater,* the wise see the same.

इहैव तैर्जित: सर्गो येषां साम्ये स्थितं मनः ।
निर्दोषं हि समं ब्रह्म तस्माद्वह्मणि ते स्थिता: ॥ १९ ॥

Even here birth is overcome by them
whose mind rests on equality. Spotless, indeed, and
equal is Brahman; wherefore in Brahman they rest.

न प्रहृष्येत्प्रियं प्राप्य नोद्विजेत्प्राप्य चाप्रियम् ।
स्थिरबुद्धिरसम्मूढो ब्रह्माविद्बह्माणि स्थितः ॥ २० ॥

He who knows Brahman can neither
rejoice on obtaining the pleasant, nor grieve on
obtaining the unpleasant,-steady-minded, un-
deluded, resting in Brahman.

बाह्यस्पर्शेष्वसक्तात्मा विन्दत्यात्मनि यत्सुखम् ।
स ब्रह्मयोगयुक्तात्मा सुखमक्षय्यमइतुते ॥ २१ ॥

With the self unattached to external
contacts, he finds the joy which is in the Self;
with the Self engaged in the contemplation of
Brahman he attains the endless joy.

ये हि संस्पर्शजा भोगा दुःखयोनय एव ते ।
आद्यन्तवन्तः कौन्तेय न तेषु रमते बुधः ।। २२ ।।

For, those delights which are born of
contacts are only generators of pain, having a be-
ginning and an end, O son of Kunti; a wise man
rejoices not in them.

शकोर्ताहैव यः सोढुं पाक् शरीरविमोक्षणात् ।
कामक्रोघोद्भवं वेगं स युक्तः स सुली नरः ॥ २३ ॥

He that is able, while still here, to with-
stand, before liberation from the body, the impulse
of desire and anger, he is a Yogin, he is a happy
man.

योऽन्तः सुखोऽन्तरारामस्तथान्तर्ज्योतिरेव यः ।
स योगी ब्रह्मनिर्वाणं ब्रह्मभूतोऽधिगच्छति ॥ २४ ॥

Whoso has his joy within and his pastime
within, and whoso has his light within only,<br>that Yogin attains Brahman's bliss, himself be-
coming Brahman.

लभन्ते ब्रह्मनिर्वाणमृषयः क्षीणकल्मषाः ।
छिन्नद्वैघा यतात्मानः सर्वभूतहिते रताः ॥ २५ ॥

The sages attain Brahman's bliss,-they
whose sins have been destroyed and doubts remov-
ed, who are self-controlled and intent on the welfare
of all beings.

कामक्रोधवियुक्तानां यतीनां यतचेतसाय ।
अभितो ब्रह्मनिर्वाणं वर्तते विदितात्मनाम् ॥ २६ ॥

To the devotees who are free from desire
and anger, who have controlled their thought, and
who have known the Self, Brahman's bliss exists
everywhere.

स्पर्शान्कृत्वा बहिर्बाह्यां अञ्जुन्नैवान्तरे अवोः ।
प्राणापानौ समौ कृत्वा नासाभ्यन्तरचारिणौ ॥ २७ ॥

यतेन्द्रियमनोबुद्धिर्मुनिर्मोक्षपरायणः ।
विगतेच्छाभयकोघो यः सदा मुक्त एव सः ॥ २८ ॥

27-28. Shutting out all external contracts and
fixing the sight between the eye-brows, equalising
the out-going and the in-going breaths which pass
through the nostrils, controlling the senses, mind
and intellect, having moksha as his highest goal,
free from desire, fear and anger,-the sage who
ever (remains thus) is verily liberated.

भोक्तारं यज्ञतपसां सर्वेलोकमहेश्वरम् ।
सुहृदं सर्वभूतानां ज्ञात्वा मां शान्तिमृच्छति ॥ २९ ॥

On knowing Me,-the Lord of all
sacrifices and austerities, the Great Lord of all
Worlds, the Friend of all beings,-he goes to
Peace.

DHYANA-YOGA.

अनाश्रित: कर्मफलं कार्यं कर्म करोति य:।
स संन्यासी च योगी च न निरमिर्न चाकिय: ॥ १ ॥

He who, without depending on the fruits
of action, performs his bounden duty, he is a
Samnyasin and a Yogin : not he who is without
fire and without action.

यं संन्यासमिति प्राहुर्योगं तं विद्धि पाण्डव ।
न ह्यसंन्यस्तसङ्करणो योगी भवति कश्चन ॥ २ ॥

Do thou, O Pandava, know Yoga to be
that which they call renunciation; no one, verily,
becomes a Yogin who has not reno unced thoughts.

आरुरुक्षोर्मुनेर्योगं कर्मं कारणमुच्यते ।
योगारूढस्य तस्यैव शमः कारणमुच्यते ॥ ३ ॥

For a devotee who wishes to attain to
Yoga, action is said to be the means. For the
same (devotee), when he has attained to Yoga,
quiescence (sama) is said to be the means.

यदा हि चेन्द्रियार्थेषु न कर्मस्वनुषज्जते ।
सर्वसङ्करपसंन्यासी योगारूढस्तदोच्यते ॥ ४ ॥

When a man, renouncing all thoughts,
is not attached to sense-objects and actions, then
he is said to have attained to Yoga.

उद्धरेदात्मनाऽऽत्मानं नात्मानमवसादयेत् ।
आत्मैव ह्यात्मनो बन्धुरात्मैव रिपुरात्मनः ॥ ५ ॥

Let a man raise himself by himself, let
him not lower himself; for, he alone is the friend
of himself, he alone is the enemy of himself.

बन्धुरात्माऽऽत्मनस्तस्य थेनात्मैवात्मना जितः ।
अनात्मनस्तु शत्रुत्वे वर्तेतात्मैव शत्रुवत् ।। ६ ॥

To him who has conquered himself by
himself, his own self is the friend of himself, but
to him who has not (conquered) himself, his own
self stands in the place of an enemy like the
(external) foe.

जितात्मनः प्रशान्तस्य परमात्मा समाहितः ।
शीतोष्णसुखदुःखेषु तथा मानावमानयोः ॥ ७ ॥

The self-controlled and serene
man's
Supreme Self is steadfast in cold and heat, in
pleasure and pain, as also in honour and disgrace.

ज्ञानविज्ञानतृप्तात्मा कूटस्थो विजितेन्द्रियः ।
युक्त इत्युच्यते योगी समलोष्टाइमकाक्चनः ॥ ८ ॥

The Yogin whose self is satisfied with
knowledge and wisdom, who remains unshaken,
who has conquered the senses, he is said to be a
saint,-for whom a lump of earth, a stone and
gold are equal.

सुहृन्मित्रार्युदासीनमध्यस्रद्वेष्यबन्धुषु ।
साघुष्वपि च पापेषु समबुद्धिर्विशिष्यते ॥ ९ ॥

He is esteemed, who is of the same mind
to the good-hearted, friends, foes, the indifferent,
the neutral, the hateful, relatives, the righteous, and
the unrighteous.

योगी युञ्जीत सततमात्मानं रहसि स्थितः ।
एकाकी यतचित्तात्मा निराशीरपारिग्रहः ॥ १० ॥

Let the Yogin try constantly to keep the
mind steady, remaining in seclusion, alone, with
the mind and body controlled, free from desire,
and having no possessions.

ञ्जवौ देशे प्रतिष्ठाप्य स्थिरमासनमात्मनः ।
नात्युच्छ्रितं नातिनीचं चेळाजिनकुशोत्तरम् ॥ ११ ॥

Having in a cleanly spot established a
firm seat, neither too high nor too low, with cloth.
skin, and kusa grass thereon;

तत्रैकामं मनः ऋत्वा यतचित्तेन्द्रियकियः ।
उपविद्यासने युड्याद्योगमात्मविद्युद्धये ॥ १२ ॥

Making the mind one-pointed, with the
actions of the mind and the senses controlled, let
him, seated there on the seat, practise Yoga for
the purification of the self.

समं कायशिरोग्रीवं धारयन्नचळं खिरः ।
सम्प्रेक्ष्य नासिकामं खं दिशस्त्रानवलोकयन् ॥ १३ ॥

Holding erect and still the body, the head
and the neck, firm, gazing on the tip of his nose,
without looking around ;

प्रशान्तात्मा विगतभीत्रीह्यचारित्रते स्थितः ।
मनः संयम्य मचित्तो युक्त आसीत मत्परः ।। १४ ॥

Serene-minded, fearless, firm in the vow
of godly life, having restrained the mind, thinking
on Me, and balanced, let him sit, looking up to
Me as the Supreme.

युञ्जन्नेवं सदाऽऽत्मानं योगी नियतमानसः ।
ज्ञानित निर्वाणपरमां मत्संस्थामधिगच्छति ॥ १५ ॥

Thus always keeping the mind balanced,
the Yogin, with the mind controlled, attains to the
Peace abiding in Me, which culminates in Nirvana
(moksha,)

नात्यश्नतस्तु योगोऽस्ति न चैकान्तमनश्नतः ।
न चातिस्वप्रशीलस्य जामतो नैव चार्जुन ॥ १६ ॥

Yoga is not possible for him who eats
too much, nor for him who does not eat at all, nor
for him who is addicted to too much sleep, nor for
him who is (ever) wakeful, O Arjuna.

युक्ताहारविहारस्य युक्तचेष्टस्य कर्मस् ।
युक्तस्वप्नावबोधस्य योगो भवति दुःखहा ॥ १७ ॥

To him whose food and recreation are
moderate, whose exertion in actions is moderate,
whose sleep and waking are moderate, to him
accrues Yoga which is destructive of pain.

यदा विनियतं चित्तमात्मन्येवावतिष्ठते ।
निम्त्पृहः सर्वकामेभ्यो युक्त इत्युच्यते तदा ॥ १८ ॥

When the well-restrained thought is
established in the Self only, without longing for
any of the objects of desire, then he is said to be
a Saint.

यथा दीपो निवातस्थो नेङ्गते सोपमा स्मृता ।
योगिनो यतचित्तस्य युञ्जतो योगमात्मनः ॥ १९ ॥

As a lamp in a sheltered spot does not
flicker,'-this has been thought as the simile of a
Yogin of subdued thought, practising Yoga in the
Self.

यत्रोपरमते चित्तं निरुद्धं योगसेवया ।
यत्र चैवात्मनाऽऽत्मानं परयन्नात्मनि तुष्यति ॥ २० ॥

When thought is quiescent, restrained
by the practice of Yoga; when, seeing the Self by
the self, he is satisfied in his own Self;

सुखमात्यन्तिकं यत्तद् बुद्धिमाह्यमतीन्द्रियम् ।
वेत्ति यत्र न चैवाऽयं स्थितश्चलति तत्त्वतः ॥ २१ ॥

When he knows that Infinite Joy which,
transcending the senses, can be grasped by reason;
when, steady (in the Self), he moves never from
the Reality ;

यं लब्ब्वा चापरं लाभं मन्यते नाधिकं ततः ।
यस्मिन्स्थितो न दुःखेन गुरुणाऽपि विचाल्यते ॥ २२ ॥

When, having obtained it, he thinks no
other acquisition superior to it; when, therein
established, he is not moved even by a great pain ;

तं विद्याद् दुःखसंयोगवियोगं योगसंज्ञितम् ।
स निश्चयेन योक्तव्यो योगोऽनिर्विण्णचेतसा ॥ २३ ॥

This severance from union with pain, be
it known, is called <i>union</i> (Yoga). That Yoga must
be practised with determination and with unde-
pressed heart.

सङ्कल्पप्रमवान्कामांस्यक्त्वा सर्वानञ्चेषतः ।
मनसैवेन्द्रियमामं विनियम्य समन्ततः ॥ २४ ॥

Abandoning without reserve all fancy-
born desires, well-restraining all the senses from
all quarters by the mind;

शनैःशनैरुपरमेद् बुद्धचा घृतिगृहीतया ।
आत्मसंस्यं मनः कृत्वा न किञ्चिदपि चिन्तयेत् ॥ २५ ॥

Little by little let him withdraw, by
reason (buddhi) held in firmness; keeping the
mind established in the Self, let him not think of
anything.

यतो यतो निश्चरति मनश्चञ्चलमस्थिरम् ।
ततस्ततो नियम्यैतदात्मन्येव वशं नयेत् ॥ २६ ॥

By whatever cause the wavering and
unsteady mind wanders away, from that let him
restrain it and bring it back direct under the
control of the Self.

प्रशान्तमनसं ह्येनं योगिनं सुखमुत्तमम् ।
उपैति ज्ञान्तरजसं ब्रह्ममूतमकल्मषम् ॥ २७ ॥

Supreme Bliss verily comes to this
Yogin, whose mind is quite tranquil, whose passion
is quieted, who has become Brahman, who is
blemishless.

युञ्जन्नेवं सदाऽऽत्मानं योगी विगतकल्मषः ।
सुखेन ब्रह्मसंस्पर्शमत्यन्तं सुखमइनुते ॥ २८ ॥

Thus always keeping the self steadfast,
the Yogin, freed from sins, attains with ease to the
infinite bliss of contact with the (Supreme) Brah-
man.

सर्वभूतस्थमात्मानं सर्वभूतानि चात्मनि ।
ईक्षते योगयुक्तात्मा सर्वत्र समदर्शनः ।। २९ ॥

The Self abiding in all beings, and all<br>beings (abiding) in the Self, sees he whose self has
been made steadfast by Yoga, who everywhere
sees the same.

यो मां पत्त्यति सर्वत्र सर्वं च मयि पत्त्यति ।
तस्याहं न प्रणत्र्यामि स च मे न प्रणद्यति ।। ३० ।।

He who sees Me everywhere and sees
everything in Me, to him I vanish not, nor to Me
does he vanish.

सर्वभूतस्थितं यो मां भजत्येकत्वमास्थितः ।
सर्वेथा वर्तमानोऽपि स योगी मयि वर्तते ॥ ३१ ॥

Whoso, intent on unity, worships Me
who abide in all beings, that Yogin dwells in Me,
whatever his mode of life.

आत्मौपम्येन सर्वत्र समं पर्द्याति योऽर्जुन ।
सुखं वा यदि वा दुःखं स योगी परमो मतः ॥ ३२ ॥

Whoso, by comparison with himself,
sees the same everywhere, O Arjuna, be it pleasure
or pain, he is deemed the highest Yogin.

योऽयं योगस्त्वया प्रोक्तः साम्येन मधुसूदन ।
एतस्याहं न पत्र्यामि चंचलत्वात् स्थितिं स्थिराम् ॥ ३३ ॥

This Yoga in equanimity, taught by
Thee, O Destroyer of Madhu,-I see not its steady
continuance, because of the restlessness (of the
mind.)

चञ्चलं हि मनः कृष्ण प्रमाथि बलवदु दृढम् ।
तस्याऽहं निग्रहं मन्ये वायोरिव सुदुष्करम् ॥ ३४ ॥

The mind verily, is, O Krishna, restless,
turbulent, strong and obstinate. Thereof the
restraint I deem quite as difficult as that of the
wind.

असंशयं महाबाहो मना दुर्निग्रहं चलम् ।
अम्यासेन तु कौन्तेय वैराग्येण च गृह्यते ।। ३५ ॥

Doubtless, O mighty-armed, the mind is
hard to restrain and restless; but by practice, O
son of Kunti, and by indifference it may be
restrained.

असंयतात्मना योगो दुष्प्राप इति मे मतिः ।
वर्यात्मना तु यतता शक्योऽवाप्तुमुपायतः ॥ ३६ ॥

Yoga, methinks is hard to attain for a
man of uncontrolled self; but by him who (often)
strives, self-controlled, it can be acquired by
(proper) means.

अयतिः श्रद्धयोपेतो योगाचळितमानसः ।
अप्राप्य योगसंसिद्धिं कां गतिं कृष्ण गच्छति ।। ३७ ॥

He who strives not, but who is possessed
of faith, whose mind wanders away from Yoga,-
having failed to attain perfection in Yoga, what
end, O Krishna, does he meet?

काँचन्नोभयविभ्रष्टाः व्रव्नाभ्रमिव नज्ञ्यति ।
अप्रतिष्ठो महाबाहो विमूढो ब्रह्मणः पथि ।। ३८ ॥

Having failed in both, does he not perish
like a riven cloud, supportless, O mighty-armed,
and perplexed in the path to Brahman?

एतं मे संशयं कृष्ण छेत्तुमईस्यशेषतः ।
त्वदन्यः संशयस्यास्य छेत्ता नह्युपपद्यते ॥ ३९ ॥

This doubt of mine, O Krishna, do Thou
dispel completely; for none other than Thyself
can possibly destroy this doubt.

पार्थ नैवेह नामुत्र विनाशस्तस्य विद्यते ।
न हि कल्याणकुत्कश्चिद्दुर्गतिं तात गच्छति ॥ ४० ॥

O Partha, neither in this world nor in
the next is there destruction for him; none, verily,
who does good, My son, ever comes to grief.

प्राप्य पुण्यकृतां लोकानुषित्वा शाश्वतीः समाः ।
ह्यचीनां श्रीमतां गेहे योगभ्रष्टोऽभिजायते ।। ४१ ।।

Having attained to the worlds of the
righteous and having dwelt there for eternal years,
he who failed in Yoga is reborn in a house of the
pure and wealthy.

अथवा योगिनामेव कुळे भवति घीमताम् ।
एतद्धि दुर्लभतरं लोके जन्म यदीदृशम् ॥ ४२ ॥

Else, he is born in family of wise Yogins.
only. This, verily, a birth like this, is very hard to
obtain in this world.

तत्र तं बुद्धिसंयोगं लभते पौर्वदेहिकम् ।
यतते च ततो भूयः संसिद्धौ कुरुनन्दन ॥ ४३ ॥

There he gains touch with the knowledge
that was acquired in the former body and strives
more than before for perfection, O son of the
Kurus.

पूर्वाभ्यासेन तेनैव ह्रियते ह्यवशोऽपि सः ।
जिज्ञासुरपि योगस्य शब्दब्रह्मातिवर्तते ॥ ४४ ॥

By that very former practice is he borne
on, though unwilling. Even he who merely wishes
to know of Yoga rises superior to the Word-
Brahman.

प्रयत्नाद्यतमानस्तु योगी संद्युद्धकिल्बिषः ।
अनेकजन्मसंसिद्धस्ततो याति परां गतिम ॥ ४५ ॥

Verily, a Yogin who strives with assiduity,
purified from sins and perfected in the course of
many births, then reaches the Supreme Goal.

तपस्विभ्योऽधिको योगी ज्ञानिभ्योऽपि मतोऽधिकः ।
कर्मिभ्यइचाधिको योगी तस्माद्योगी भवार्जुन ॥ ४६ ॥

A Yogin is deemed superior to men of
austerity, and superior to even men of knowledge;
he is also superior to men of action; therefore be
thou a Yogin, O Arjuna.

योगिनामपि सर्वेषां मद्रतेनान्तरात्मना ।
श्रद्धावान्भजते यो मां स मे युक्तमो मतः ॥ ४७ ॥

Of all Yogins, whoso, full of faith,
worships Me with his inner self abiding in Me, he
is deemed by Me as most devout.

chapter 7

VIJNANA-YOGA.


मय्यासक्तमनाः पार्थं योगं युझन्मदाश्रयः ।
असंशयं सममं मां यथा ज्ञास्यसि तच्छ्रणु ॥ १ ॥

With the mind intent on Me, O Partha,
practising Yoga, and finding refuge in Me, how in
full without doubt thou shalt know Me, that do
thou hear.

ज्ञानं तेऽहं सविज्ञानमिदं वक्ष्याम्यशेषतः ।
यज्ज्ञात्वा नेह भूयोऽन्यज्ज्ञातव्यमवाशिष्यते ॥ २ ॥

I shall fully teach thee this knowledge
combined with experience, which being known,
nothing more besides here remains to be known.

मनुष्याणां सहस्रेषु कश्चिद्यतति सिद्धये ।
यततामपि सिद्धानां कश्चिन्मां वोत्ते तत्वतः ॥ ३ ॥

Among thousands of men, one perchance
strives for perfection; even among those
who
strive and are perfect, only one perchance knows
me in truth.

अपरेयमितस्त्वन्यां प्रकृतिं विद्धि मे पराम् ।
जीवभूतां महाबाहो ययेदं घार्यते जगत् ॥ ५ ॥

This is the inferior (Prakriti); but as
distinct from this know thou My superior Prakriti,
the very life, O mighty-armed, by which this
universe is upheld.

एतद्योनीनि भूतानि सर्वाणीत्युपधारय ।
अहं कृत्स्नस्य जगतः प्रभवः प्रलयस्तथा ॥ ६ ॥

Know that all beings have their birth in
these. So, I am the source and dissolution of the
whole universe.

मत्तः परतरं नान्यतिकञ्चिदस्ति घनञ्जय ।
मयि सर्वमिदं प्रोतं सूत्रे मणिगणा इव ॥ ७ ॥

There is naught else higher than I, O
Dhananjaya: in Me all this is woven as clusters of
gems on a string.

रसोऽहमप्तु कौन्तेय प्रभाऽस्मि शशिसूर्ययोः ।
प्रणवः सर्ववेदेषु शब्दः खे पौरुषं नृषु ॥ ८ ॥

I am the sapidity in water, O son of
Kunti. I am the light in the moon and the sun.
I am the syllable Om in all the Vedas, sound in
ether, humanity in men.

पुण्यो गन्धः पृथिव्यां च तेजश्चास्मि विभावसौ ।
जीवनं सर्वभूतेषु तपश्चास्मि तपत्विषु ॥ ९ ॥

And I am the agreeable odour in the
earth and the brilliance in the fire, the vitality in
all beings, and I am the austerity in ascetics.

बीजं मां सर्वभूतानां विद्धि पार्थं सनातनम् ।
बुद्धिबुद्धिमतामस्मि तेजस्तेजस्विनामहम् ॥ १० ॥

Know Me, O Partha, as the eternal seed
of all beings; I am the intelligence of the intelli-
gent, the bravery of the brave.

बळं बळवतामस्मि कामरागविवर्जितम् ।
धर्माविरुद्धो भूतेषु कामोऽस्मि भरतर्षभ ॥ ११ ॥

And of the energetic am I the energy
devoid of passion and attachment; and in (all)
beings I am the desire unopposed to dharma, O
lord of the Bharatas.

ये चैव सात्विका भावा राजसास्तामसाइच ये ।
मत्त एवेति तान्विद्धि न त्वहं तेषु ते मयि ॥ १२ ॥

And whatever beings are of Sattva or of
Rajas or of Tamas, know them to proceed from
Me: still, I am not in them, they are in me.

त्रिभिर्गुणमयैभावरेभिः सर्वमिदं जगत् ।
मोहितं नाभिजानाति मामेभ्यः परमव्ययम् ॥ १३ ॥

Deluded by these three (sorts of) things
composed of gunas, all this world knows not Me
as distinct from them and immutable.

दैवी ह्येषा गुणमयी मम माया दुरत्यया।
मामेव ये प्रपद्यन्ते मायामेतां तरन्ति ते ॥ १४ ॥

Verily this Divine Illusion of Mine, made
up of gunas, is hard to surmount. Whoever seek
Me alone, they cross over this Illusion.

न मां दुष्कृतिनो मूढाः प्रपद्यन्ते नराघमाः ।
मायगाऽपहृतज्ञाना आसुरं भावमाश्रिताः ॥ १५ ॥

Not Me do the evil-doers seek, the delud-
ed, the vilest of men, deprived of wisdom by
Illusion, following the ways of the Demons.

चतुर्विधा भजन्ते मां जनाः सुकृतिनोऽर्जुन ।
आर्तो जिज्ञासुरर्थार्थी ज्ञानी च भरतर्षभ ॥ १६ ॥

Four kinds of virtuous men worship Me,
O Arjuna-the distressed, the seeker of knowledge,
the seeker of wealth, and the wise man, O lord of
the Bharatas.

तेषां ज्ञानी नित्ययुक्त एकभक्तिर्विशिष्यते ।
प्रियो हि ज्ञानिनोऽत्यर्थमहं स च मम प्रिय: ॥ १७ ॥

Of them the wise man, ever steadfast
and devoted to the One, excels; for, excessively
dear am I to the wise, and he is dear to Me.

उदाराः सर्व एवैते ज्ञानी त्वात्मैव में मतम् ।
आस्थितः स हि युक्तात्मा मामेवानुत्तमां गतिम् ॥ १८ ॥

Noble indeed are all these; but the wise
man, I deem, is the very Self; for, steadfast in
mind, he resorts to Me alone as the unsurpassed
goal.

बहूनां जन्मनामन्ते ज्ञानवान्मां प्रपद्यते ।
वासुदेवः सर्वमिति स महात्मा सुदुर्रुभः ॥ १९ ॥

At the end of many births, the man of
wisdom comes to me, (realising) that Vasudeva is
the all: he is the noble-souled (Mahatman), very
hard to find.

कामैस्तैस्तैर्हृतज्ञानाः प्रपद्यन्तेऽन्यदेवताः ।
तं तं नियममास्थाय प्रकृत्या नियताः स्वया ॥ २० ॥

Those whose wisdom has been led away
by this or that desire resort to other Gods, engag-
ed in this or that rite, constrained by their own
nature.

यो यो यां यां ततुं भक्तः श्रद्धयाऽर्चितुमिच्छति ।
तस्य तस्याचलां श्रद्धां तामेव विद्घाम्यहम् ॥ २१ ॥

Whatever devotee seeks to worship with
faith what form soever, that same faith of his I
make unflinching.

स तया श्रद्धया युक्तस्त्याराघनमीहते ।
लभते च ततः कामान्मयैव विहितान्हितान् ॥ २२ ॥

Possessed of that faith he engages in
the worship of that (form); thence he obtains his
desires, these being indeed ordained by me.

अन्तवत्त्तु फलं तेषां तद्भवत्यल्पमेघसाम् ।
देवान्देवयजो यान्ति मद्भक्ता यान्ति मामपि ॥ २३ ॥

That result indeed is finite, (which
accrues) to those men of small intellect. Wor-
shippers of Gods (Devatas) go to Gods (Devatas);
My devotees come unto Me.

अव्यक्तं व्यक्तिमापन्नं मन्यन्ते मामबुद्धयः ।
परं भावमजानन्तो ममाव्ययमनुत्तमम् ॥ २४ ॥

The foolish regard Me as the unmanifest-
ed coming in manifestation, knowing not My
higher, immutable, unsurpassed nature.

नाहं प्रकाशः सर्वस्य योगमायासमावृतः ।
मूढोऽयं नाभिजानाति लोको मामजमव्ययम् ॥ २५ ॥

I am not manifest to all, veiled (as I am)
by Yoga-Maya. This deluded world knows not
Me, unborn and imperishable.

वेदाहं समतीतानि वर्तमानानि चार्जुन ।
भावेष्याणि च भूतानि मां तु वेद न कश्चन ।। २६ ॥
I know, O Arjuna, the past and the pre-
sent and the future beings, but Me nobody knows.

इच्छाद्वेषसमुत्थेन द्वन्द्वमोहेन भारत ।
सर्वभूतानि सम्मोहं सर्गे यान्ति परन्तप ॥ २७ ॥

From the delusion of pairs caused by
desire and aversion, O Bharata, all beings are sub-
ject to illusion at birth, O harasser of thy foes.

येषां त्वन्तगतं पापं जनानां पुण्यकर्मणाम् ।
ते द्वन्द्वमोहनिर्मुक्ता भजन्ते मां दृढत्रता: ।। २८ ।।

Those mortals of pure deeds whose sin
has come to an end, who are freed from the delu-
sion of pairs, they worship Me with a firm resolve

जरामरणमोक्षाय मामाश्रित्य यतन्ति ये ।
ते ब्रह्म तद्विदुः कृत्स्नमध्यात्मं कर्म चाखिलम् ॥ २९ ॥

Whoever resorting to Me strive for libera-
tion from decay and death, they realise in full that
Brahman, the individual Self and all action.

साधिभूताधिदैवं मां साधियज्ञं च ये विदुः ।
प्रयाणकालेऽपि च मां ते विदुर्युक्तचेतसः ॥ ३० ॥

Those who realise Me in the Adhibhuta
(physical region), in the Adhidaiva (the divine re-
gion) and in the Adhiyajna (region of Sacrifice),
realise Me even at the time of departure, steadfast
in mind.

chapter 8

ABHYASA-YOGA.

किं तद्बह्य किमध्यात्मं किं कर्म पुरुषोत्तम ।
अधिमूतं च किं प्रोक्तमधिदैवं किमुच्यते ॥ १ ॥

अधियज्ञः कथं कोऽत्र देहेऽस्मिन्मघुसूदन ।
प्रयाणकाले च कथं ज्ञेयोऽसि नियतात्मभिः ॥ २ ॥

1-2. What is that Brahman ? What about
the Individual Self (Adhyatma)? What is action
(Karma), O Purushottama ? And what is declared
to be the physical region (Adhibhuta)? And what
is the divine region (Adhidaiva) said to be ? And
how and who is Adhiyajna (the Entity concerned
with Sacrifice) here in this body, O Madhusudana,
and how at the time of death art Thou to be
known by the self-controlled?

अक्षरं ब्रह्म परमं स्वभावोऽध्यात्ममुच्यते ।
भूतभावोद्भवकरो विसर्गः कर्मसंज्ञितः ॥ ३ ॥

Brahman is the Imperishable (Akshara),
the Supreme. The Ego is said to be the Indivi-
dual Self (Adhyatma, He who dwells in the body).
The offering which causes the origin of physical
beings is called action (Karma).

अधिभूतं क्षरो भावः पुरुषश्चाधिदैवतम् ।
अधियज्ञोऽहमेवात्र देहे देहभृतां वर ॥ ४ ॥

The physical region (Adhibhuta) is the
perishable existence, and Purusha or the Soul is
the divine region (Adhidaivata). The Adhiyajna
(Entity concerned with Sacrifice) is Myself, here
in the body, O best of the embodied.

अन्तकाले च मामेव स्मरन्मुक्त्वा कलेबरम् ।
यः प्रयाति स मद्भावं याति नास्त्यत्र संशयः ॥ ५ ॥

And whoso, at the time of death, thinking
of Me alone, leaves the body and goes forth, he
reaches My being; there is no doubt in this.

यं यं वापि सारन्मावं त्यजत्यन्ते कलेबरम् ।
तं तमेवैति कौन्तेय सदा तद्भावभावितः ॥ ६ ॥

Of whatever Being thinking at the end a
man leaves the body, Him alone, O son of Kunti,
reaches he by whom the thought of that Being has
been constantly dwelt upon.

तस्मात्सर्वेषु काल्रेषु मामनुसार युध्य च ।
मय्यर्पितमनोबुद्धिमामेवैष्यस्यसंशयम् ॥ ७ ॥

Therefore at all times do thou mediate
on Me and fight : with mind and reason fixed on
Me thou shalt doubtless come to Me alone.

अभ्यासयोगयुक्तेन चेतसा नान्यगामिना ।
परमं पुरुषं दिव्यं याति पार्थानुचिन्तयन् ॥ ८ ॥

Meditating with the mind engaged in the
Yoga of constant practice, not passing over to any
thing else, one goes to the Suprema Purusha, the
Resplendent, O son of Pritha.

कर्वि पुराणमनुशासितारमणोरणीयांसमनुस्मरेद्यः ।
सर्वस्य धातारमचिन्त्यरूपमादित्यवर्णं तमसः परस्तात् ॥
प्रयाणकाले मनसाऽचलेन भक्त्या युक्तो योगबलेन चैव ।
ञ्जूबोर्मध्ये प्राणमावेरय सम्यक् स तं परं पुरुषमुपौति दिव्यम् ॥

9-10. Whoso meditates on the Sage, the
Ancient, the Ruler, smaller than an atom, the
Dispenser of all, of unthinkable nature, glorious
like the Sun, beyond the darkness, (whoso medi-
tates on such a Being) at the time of death, with a
steady mind endued with devotion and strength of
Yoga, well fixing the life-breath betwixt the
eye-brows, he reaches that Supreme Purusha
Resplendent.

यदक्षरं वेदविदो वदन्ति विशन्ति यद्यतयो वीतरागाः ।
यदिच्छन्तो ब्रह्मचर्यं चरन्ति तत्ते पदं सङ्ग्रहेण प्रवक्ष्ये ॥

That Imperishable Goal which the know-
ers of the Veda declare, which the self-controlled
and the passion-free enter, which desiring they
lead the godly life,-That Goal will I declare to
thee with brevity.

सर्वद्वाराणि संयम्य मनो हृदि निरुघ्य च ।
मूघ्न्याघायात्मनः प्राणमास्थितो योगधारणाम् ॥ १२ ॥

ओमिलेकाक्षरं त्रह्म व्याहरन्मामनुस्मरन् ।
यः प्रयाति त्यजन्देहं स याति परमां गतिम् ॥ १३ ॥

12-13. Having closed all the gates, having
confined mind in the heart, having fixed his life-
breath in the head, engaged in firm Yoga, uttering
Brahman, the one-syllabled 'Om,' thinking of Me,
whoso departs, leaving the body, he reaches the
Supreme Goal.

अनन्यचेताः सततं यो मां स्मरति नित्यशः ।
तस्याहं सुलभः पार्थ नित्ययुक्तस्य योगिनः ॥ १४ ॥

Whose constantly thinks of me and long,
to him I am easily accessible, O son of Pritha, to
the ever-devout Yogin.

मामुपेल पुनर्जन्म दुःखालयमशाश्वतम् ।
नाप्तुवन्ति महात्मानः संसिाद्धिं परमां गताः ॥ १५ ॥

Having attained to Me, they do not again
attain birth, which is the seat of pain and is not
eternal, they having reached highest perfection.

आब्रह्मभुवनाल्लोकाः पुनरावर्तिनोऽर्जुन ।
मामुपेत्य तु कौन्तेय पुनर्जन्म न विद्यते ।। १६ ।।

(All) worlds including the world of
Brahma are subject to returning again, O Arjuna ;
but, on reaching Me, O son of Kunti, there is no
rebirth.

सहस्रयुगपर्यन्तमहर्यद्वहाणो विदुः ।
रात्रिं युगसहस्रान्तां तेऽहोरात्रविदो जनाः ॥ १७ ॥

They-those people who know day and
night—know that the day of Brahma is a thousand
yugas long and the night is a thousand yugas long.

अव्यक्ताद्वचक्तयः सर्वाः प्रभवन्त्यहरागमे ।
रात्र्यागमे प्रलीयन्ते तत्रैवाव्यक्तसंज्ञके ॥ १८ ॥

From the Unmanifested all the mani-
festations proceed at the coming on of day; at the
coming on of night they dissolve there only, in
what is called the Unmanifested.

भूतमामः स एवायं भूत्वा भूत्वा प्रलीयते ।
रात्र्यागमेऽवशः पार्थं प्रभवत्यहरागमे ॥ १९ ॥

This same multitude of beings having
come into being again and again, is dissolved at
the coming on of night, not of their will, O son
of Pritha, and comes forth at the coming on of day.

परसासातु भावोऽन्योऽव्यक्तो ऽव्यक्तात्सनातनः ।
यः स सर्वेषु भूतेषु नइयत्सु न विनइयति ॥ २० ॥

But that other eternal Unmanifested
Being, distinct from this Unmanifested (Avyakta),
-He does not perish when all creatures perish.

अव्यक्तोऽक्षर इत्युक्तस्तमाहुः परमां गतिम् ।
यं प्राप्य न निवर्तन्ते तद्धाम परमं मम ॥ २१ ॥

What is called the Unmanifested and
the Imperishable, That, they say, is the highest
goal; which having reached none return. That is
My highest place.

पुरुषः स परः पार्थं भक्त्या लम्यस्त्वनन्यया ।
यस्यान्तःस्थानि भूतानि येन सर्वमिदं ततम् ॥ २२ ॥

Now, that Highest Purusha, O son of
Pritha, within Whom all beings dwell, by Whom
all this is pervaded, is attainable by exclusive
devotion

यत्र काळे त्वनावृत्तिमावृत्तिं चैव योगिनः ।
प्रयाता चान्ति तं काळं वक्ष्यामि भरतर्षेभ ॥ २३ ॥

Now, in what time departing, Yogins go
to return not, as also to return, that time will I
tell thee, O chief of the Bharatas.

अग्निज्योतिरहः ग्लक्लः षण्मासा उत्तरायणम् ।
तंत्र प्रयाता गच्छन्ति ब्रह्म ब्रह्मविदो जनाः ॥ २४ ॥

Fire, light, day-time, the bright fortnight,
the six months of the northern solstice,-then
departing, men who know Brahman reach Brahman.

धूमो रात्रिस्तथा कृष्णः षण्मासा दक्षिणायनम् ।
तत्र चान्द्रमसं ज्योतिर्योगी प्राप्य निवर्तते ॥ २५ ॥

Smoke, night-time, and the dark fortnight,
the six months of the southern solstice,-attaining
by these to the lunar light, the Yogin returns.

ग्लक्छकृष्णे गती द्येते जगतः शाश्वते मते ।
एकया यात्यनावृत्तिमन्ययाऽऽवर्तेते पुनः ॥ २६ ॥

These bright and dark Paths of the world
are verily deemed eternal ; by the one a man goes
to return not, by the other he returns again.

नैते सृती पार्थ जानन्योगी मुह्यति कश्चन ।
तस्मात्सर्वेषु कालेषु योगयुक्ता भवार्जुन ॥ २७ ॥

Knowing these paths, O son of Pritha,
no Yogin is deluded : wherefore at all times be
steadfast in Yoga, O Arjuna.

वेदेषु यज्ञेषु तपस्सु चैव दानेषु यत्पुण्यफळं प्रदिष्टम् ।
अत्येति तत्सर्वमिदं विदित्वा योगी परं स्थानमुपैति चाद्यम् ॥

Whatever fruit of merit is declared to
accrue from the Vedas, sacrifices, austerities and
gifts,-beyond all this goes the Yogin on knowing
this; and he attains to the Supreme Primeval
Abode.

chapter 9

SOVEREIGN WISDOM AND SECRET.

इदं तु ते गुह्यतमं प्रवक्ष्याम्यनसूयवे ।
ज्ञानं विज्ञानसहितं यज्ज्ञात्वा मोक्ष्यसेऽघ्रुमात् ॥ १ ॥

To thee who dost not cavil, I shall now
declare this, the greatest secret, knowledge com-
bined with experience, which having known thou
shalt be liberated from evil.

राजविद्या राजगुद्यं पवित्रमिद्मुत्तमम् ।
प्रत्यक्षावगमं घम्यं सुसुखं कर्तुमय्ययम् ॥ २ ॥

The Sovereign Science, the Sovereign
Secret, the Supreme Purifier is this; immediately
comprehensible, unopposed to Dharma, very easy
to perform, imperishable.

अश्रद्दधानाः पुरुषा धर्मस्यास्य परन्तप ।
अप्राप्य मां निवर्तन्ते मृत्युसंसारवर्त्मनि ।। ३ ।।

Persons having no faith in this Dharma,
O harasser of thy foes, without reaching Me.
remain verily in the path of the mortal world.

मया ततमिदं सर्वं जगदव्यक्तमूर्तिना ।
मत्स्थानि सर्वभूतानि न चाहं तेष्ववस्थितः ॥ ४ ॥

By Me all this world is pervaded, My
form unmanifested. All beings dwell in Me; and
I do not dwell in them.

न च मत्स्थानि भूतानि परय मे योगमैत्र्यरम् ।
भूतभृन्न च भूतस्यो ममात्मा भूतभावनः ॥ ५ ॥

Nor do those beings dwell in Me; behold
My Divine Yoga! Sustaining all the beings, but not
dwelling in them, is My Self, the cause of beings.

यथाऽऽकाशस्थितो नित्यं वायुः सर्वत्रगो महान् ।
तथा सवाार्ण मूतानि मत्स्थानीत्युपघारय ॥ ६ ॥

As the mighty wind moving everywhere
rests ever in the akasa, so, know thou, do all
beings, rest in Me.

सर्वभूतानि कौन्तेय प्रकृतिं यान्ति मामिकाम् ।
कल्पक्षये पुनस्तानि कल्पादौ विसृजाम्यहम् ॥ ७ ॥

All beings, O son of Kunti, go into My
Prakriti at the end of a kalpa. I send them forth
again at the beginning of (the next) kalpa.

प्रकृतिं स्वामवष्टभ्य विसृजामि पुनः पुनः ।
भूतमामामिमं ऋत्स्नमवशं प्रकृतेर्वशात् ॥ ८ ॥

Resorting to My Prakriti, I again and
again send forth the whole multitude of, beings,
powerless under the control of the Prakriti.

न च मां तानि कर्माणि निबघ्नन्ति घनञ्जय ।
उदासीनवदासीनमसक्तं तेषु कर्मेसु ॥ ९ ॥

Nor do these acts, O Dhananjaya, bind
Me, remaining like one unconcerned, unattached to
those acts.

मयाऽध्यक्षेण प्रकृतिः सूयते सचराचरम् ।
हेतुताऽनेन कौन्तेय जगद्विपरिवर्तते ॥ १० ॥

By Me presiding, Prakriti produces the
moving and the unmoving ; because of this, O son
of Kunti, the world revolves.

ं अवजानन्ति मां मूढा मानुषीं तनुमाश्रितम् ।
परं भावमजानन्तो मम भूतमहेश्वरम् ॥ ११ ॥

Fools disregard Me clad in human form,
not knowing My higher being as the Great Lord of
beings.

सोघाशा मोघकर्माणो मोघज्ञाना विचेतसः ।
राक्षसीमासुरी चैव प्रकृतिं मोहिनीं श्रिताः ॥ १२ ॥

Of vain hopes, of vain actions, of vain
knowledge, devoid of discrimination, partaking
only of the delusive nature of Rakshasas and
Asuras.

महात्मानस्तु मां पार्थ दैवीं प्रकृतिमाश्रिताः ।
भजन्त्यनन्यमनसो ज्ञात्वा भूतादिमव्ययम् ॥ १३ ॥

The Mahatmans, O son of Pritha, par-
taking of the nature of the Devas, worship Me
with mind turned to no other, t knowing (Me) as
the imperishable source of all beings.

सततं कीर्तयन्तो मां यतन्तश्च दृढव्रताः ।
नमस्यन्तश्च मां भक्त्या नित्ययुक्ता उपासते ॥ १४ ॥

Always talking of Me, strenuous, firm
in vows, and reverent, they worship Me with love,
always devout.

ज्ञानयज्ञेन चाप्यन्ये यजन्तो मामपासते ।
एकत्वेन पृथक्त्वेन बहुघा विश्वतोमुखम् ॥ १५ ॥

Worshipping by the wisdom-sacrifice,
others adore Me, the All-faced, in various ways, as
One, as different.

अहं कतुरहं यज्ञः खधाऽहमहमौषधम् ।
मन्त्रोऽहमहमेवाज्यमहमग्निरहं हृतम् ॥ १६ ॥

Iam kratu, Iam yajna, Iam svadha. I
am aushadha, I am mantra, Myself the butter, I
am fire, I the act of offering.

पिताऽहमस्य जगतो माता घाता पितामहः ।
वेद्यं पवित्रमोङ्कार ऋक् साम यजुरेव च ॥ १७ ॥

I am the father of this world, the mother,
the dispenser, and grandsire; I am the knowable,
the purifier, the syllable 'Om,' and also the Rik,
the Saman, and the Yajus also.

गतिर्मती प्रमुः साक्षी निवासः शरणं सुहृत् ।
प्रभवः प्रलयः स्थानं निधानं बीजमव्ययम् ॥ १८ ॥

I am the Goal, the Sustainer, the Lord,
the Witness, the Abode, the Shelter and the Friend,
the Origin, Dissolution and Stay, the Treasure-
house, the Seed imperishable.

तपाम्यहमहं वर्षं निगृह्णाम्युत्सृजामि च ।
अमृतं चैव मृत्युश्च सदसच्चाहमर्जुन ॥ १९ ॥

I give heat, I hold back and send forth
rain, I am the immortality as well as death, exist-
ence and non-existence, O Arjuna.

त्रैविद्या मां सोमपाः पुतपापाः
यज्ञैरिष्टा खर्गतिं प्रार्थयन्ते ।
ते पुण्यमासाद्य सुरेन्द्रलोक
मश्रन्ति दिव्यान्दिवि देवभोगान् । ॥ २० ॥

Men of the three Vedas, the
soma-
drinkers, purified from sin, worshipping Me by
sacrifices, pray for the goal of heaven; they reach
the holy world of the Lord of the Gods and enjoy
in heaven the heavenly pleasures of the Gods.

ते तं भुक्त्वा खर्गलोकं विशालम्
क्षीणे पुण्ये मर्त्यलोकं विशन्ति ।
एवं त्रयीधर्ममनुप्रपन्नाः
गतागतं कामकामा लभन्ते ॥ २१ ॥

They, having enjoyed that spacious world
of Svarga, their merit (punya) exhausted, enter the
world of the mortals; thus following the Dharma
of the Triad, desiring (objects of) desires, they
attain to the state of going and returning.

अनन्याश्चिन्तयन्तो मां ये जनाः पर्युपासते ।
तेषां नित्यामियुक्तानां योगक्षेमं वहाम्यहम् ॥ २२ ॥

Those men who, meditating on Me as
non-separate, worship Me all around,-to them
who are ever devout, I secure gain and safety.

येऽप्यन्यदेवताभक्ता यजन्ते श्रद्धयाऽन्विताः ।
तेऽपि मामेव कौन्तेय यजन्त्यविधिपूर्वकम् ॥ २३ ॥

Even those who, devoted to other Gods,
worship Them with faith, worship Myself, O son
of Kunti, in ignorance.

अहं हि सर्वयज्ञानां भोका च प्रमुरेव च ।
न तु माममिजानन्ति तत्त्वेनातरच्यवन्ति ते ॥ २४ ॥

I am indeed the Enjoyer, as also the
Lord, of all sacrifices; but they do not know Me
in truth; whence they fail.

याान्ति देवव्रता देवान् पितृन्यान्ति पितृत्रताः ।
भूतानि यान्ति भूतेज्या यान्ति मद्याजिनोऽपि माम् ॥२५॥

Votaries of the Gods go to the Gods; to
the Pitris go the votaries of the Pitris; to the
Bhûtas go the worshippers of the Bhûtas; My
worshippers come to Myself.

पत्रं पुष्पं फलं तोयं यो मे भक्ला प्रयच्छति ।
तद्दं भक्त्युपहृतमश्रामि प्रयतात्मनः ॥ २६ ॥

When one offers to Me with devotion a
leaf, a flower, a fruit, water,-that I eat, offered
with devotion by the pure-minded.

यत्करोषि यदश्नासि यन्जुहोषि ददासि यत् ।
यत्तपस्यासि कौन्तेय तत्कुरुष्व मदर्पणम् ॥ २७ ॥

Whatever thou doest, whatever thou
eatest, whatever thou sacrificest, whatever thou
givest, in whatever austerity thou engagest, do it
as an offering to Me.

ग्रभाग्रमफलैरेवं मोक्ष्यसे कर्मबन्धनैः ।
संन्यासयोगयुक्तात्मा विमुक्तो मामुपैष्यसि ॥ २८ ॥

Thus shalt thou be liberated from the
bonds of actions which are productive of good and
evil results; equipped in mind with the Yoga of
renunciation, and liberated, thou shalt come to Me.

समोऽहं सर्वभूतेषु न मे द्वेष्योऽस्ति न प्रियः ।
ये भजन्ति तु मां भक्त्या माये ते तेषु चाप्यहम् ॥ २९ ॥

The same I am to all beings; to Me
there is none bateful or dear; but whoso worship
Me with devotion, they are in Me, and I am also
in them.

अपि चेत्सुदुराचारो भजते मामनन्यभाकू ।
साघुरेव स मन्तव्यः सम्यग्व्यवसितो हि सः ॥ ३० ॥

If one of even very evil life worships Me,
resorting to none else, he must indeed be deemed
righteous, for he is rightly resolved.

क्षिप्रं भवति धर्मात्मा शश्वच्छान्ति निगच्छति ।
कौन्तेय प्रतिजानीहि न मे भक्तः प्रणत्यति ॥ ३१ ॥

Soon he becomes righteous and attains
eternal peace; do thou, O son of Kunti, proclaim
that my devotee never perishes.

मां हि पार्थ व्यपाश्रित्य येऽपि स्युः पापयोनयः ।
बियो वैदयास्तथा राद्रास्तेऽपि थान्ति परां गतिम् ॥ ३२ ॥

For, finding refuge in Me, they also who,
O son of Prithâ, may be of a sinful birth-women,
vaisyas as well as sudras,—even they attain to the
Supreme Goal.

किं पुनन्नीह्मणाः पुण्या भक्ता राजर्षयस्तथा ।
अनित्यमसुखं लोकमिमं प्राप्य भजस्व माम् ॥ ३३ ॥

How much more then the holy brâhmanas
and devoted royal saints ! Having reached this
transient joyless world, do thou worship Me.

मन्मना भव मद्भक्तो मद्याजी मां नमस्करु ।
मामेवैष्यसि युक्त्वैवमात्मानं मत्परायणः ॥ ३४ ॥

Fix thy mind on Me, be devoted to Me,
sacrifice to Me, bow down to Me. Thus steadied,
with Me as thy Supreme Goal, thou shalt reach
Myself, the Self.

chapter 10

DIVINE MANIFESTATIONS.

भूय एव महाबाहो श्रृणु मे परमं वच: ।
यत्तेऽहं प्रीयमाणाय वक्ष्यामि हितकाम्यया ॥ १ ॥

Again, O mighty-armed, listen to My
Supreme word, which I, from a desire for thy
well-being, shall speak to thee who art delighted.

न मे विदुः सुरगणाः प्रभवं न महर्षयः ।
अहमादिहि देवानां महर्षीणां च सर्वशः ॥ २ ॥

Neither the hosts of the Gods nor the
Great Rishis know my origin; for I am the source
of all the Gods and the Great Rishis.

यो मामजमनादिं च वेत्ति लोकमहेश्वरम् ।
असम्मूढः स मर्देषु सर्वपापैः प्रमुच्यते ।। ३ ॥

He who knows Me as unborn and begin-
ningless, as the great Lord of the worlds, he
among mortals is undeluded, he is liberated from
all sins.

बुद्धिर्ज्ञानमसम्मोद्दः क्षमा सत्यं दमः शमः ।
सुखं दुःखं भवोऽभावो भयं चाभयमेव च ॥ ४ ॥

अहिंसा समता तुष्टिस्तपो दानं यशोऽयशः ।
भवन्ति भावा भृतानां मत्त एव पृथग्विधाः ॥ ५ ॥

4-5. Intelligence, wisdom, non-illusion,
patience, truth, self-restraint, calmness, pleasure,
pain, birth, death, fear, and security; innocence,
equanimity, contentment, austerity, beneficence,
fame, shame, (these) different kinds of dispositions
of beings arise from Me alone.

महर्षयः सप्त पूर्वे चत्वारो मनवस्तया ।
मद्भावा मानसा जाता येषां लोक इमाः प्रजाः ॥ ६ ॥

The seven Great Rishis as well as the four
ancient Manus, with their being in Me, were born
of mind; and theirs are these creatures in the
world.

्एतां विभूतिं योगं च मम यो वेत्ति तत्त्वत: ।
सोऽविकम्पेन योगेन युज्यते नात्र संशयः ॥ ७ ॥

He who knows in truth this glory and
power of Mine is endowed with unshaken Yoga;
there is no doubt of it.

अहं सर्वस्य प्रभवो मत्तः सर्वे प्रवर्तते ।
इति मत्वा भजन्ते मां बुधा भावसमन्विताः ॥ ८ ॥

I am the source of all: from Me every-
thing evolves; thus thinking the wise worship Me,
endowed with contemplation.

मचित्ता मद्गतप्राणा बोधयन्तः परस्परम् ।
कथयन्तश्च मां नित्यं तुष्यन्ति च रमन्ति च ॥ ९ ॥

With their thought on Me, with their life
absorbed in Me, instructing each other, and ever
speaking of Me, they are content and delighted.

तेषां सततयुक्ताना भजतां प्रीतिपूर्वकम् ।
ददामि बुद्धियोगं तं येन मामुपयान्ति ते ॥ १० ॥

To these, ever devout, worshipping Me
with love, I give that devotion of knowledge by
which they come to Me.

तेषामेवानुकम्पार्थमहमज्ञानजं तमः ।
नाशयाम्यात्मभावस्थो ज्ञानदीपेन भास्वता ॥ ११ ॥

Out of mere compassion for them, I,
abiding in their self, destroy the darkness born of
ignorance, by the luminous lamp of wisdom.

परं ब्रह्म परं धाम पवित्रं परमं भवान् ।
पुरुषं शाश्वतं दिव्यमादिदेवमजं विसुम् ॥ १२ ॥

आहुस्त्वामृषयः सर्वे देवर्षिर्नारदस्तथा ।
असितो देवलो व्यासः स्वयं चैव व्रवीषि मे ॥ १३ ॥

12-13. The Supreme Brahman, the Supreme
Light, the Supreme Purifier art Thou. All the

the Primal God, Unborn, Omnipresent; so said
the divine sage Narada, as also Asita, Devala and
Vyasa; and Thou Thyself also sayest (so) to me.

सर्वमेतदृतं मन्ये यन्मां बदसि केञव ।
न हि ते भगवन्व्यक्तिं विदुर्देवा न दानवाः ॥ १४ ॥

I believe to be true all this which Thou
sayest to me; for neither the Gods nor the
Danavas, O Lord, know Thy manifestation.

स्वयमेवात्मनाऽऽत्मानं वेत्थ त्वं पुरुषोत्तम ।
भूतभावन भूतेश देवदेव जगत्पते ॥ १५ ॥

Thou Thyself knowest Thyself as the
Self,* O Purusha Supreme, O Source of beings,
O Lord of beings, O God of Gods, O Ruler of the
world.

वक्तुमईस्यशेषेण दिव्या ह्यात्मविभूतयः ।
याभिर्विभूतिभिर्छोकानिमांस्त्वं व्याप्य तिष्ठासि ॥ १६ ॥

Thou shouldst † indeed tell, without

Thou remainest pervading all these worlds.

कथं विद्यामहं योगिस्त्वां सदा परिचिन्तयन् ।

How ‡ shall I, ever meditating, know

विस्तरेणात्मनो योगं विभूतिं च जनार्दन ।
भूयः कथय तृप्तिर्हि श्रुण्वतो नास्ति मेऽमृतम् ॥ १८ ॥

Tell me again in detail, O Janardana, of

हन्त ते कथयिष्यामि दिव्या ह्यात्मविमूतयः ।
प्राधान्यतः कुरुश्रेष्ठ नास्त्यन्तो विस्तरस्य मे ॥ १९ ॥

अहमात्मा गुडाकेश सर्वभुताशयस्थित: ।
अहमादिश्च मध्यं च भूतानामन्त एव च ॥ २० ॥

I am the Self, O Gudakesa, seated in the
heart of all beings; I am the beginning and the
middle, as also the end, of all beings.

आदित्यानामहं विष्णुर्ज्योतिषां रविरंग्लमान् ।
मरीचिर्मरुतामस्मि नक्षत्राणामहं शशी । २१ ।।

Of the Adityas I am Vishnu; of the
radiances, the resplendent Sun; I am Marichi of
the Maruts; of the asterisms, the Moon.

वेदानां सामवेदोऽस्मि देवानामस्मि वामवः ।
इन्द्रियाणां मनश्चास्मि भूतानामस्मि चेतना ॥ २२॥

Of the Vedas I am the Sama-Veda, I am
Vasava of the Gods, and of the senses I am the
mind, I am the intelligence in living beings.

रुद्राणां शङ्करस्त्रास्मि वित्तेशो यक्षरक्षसाम् ।
वसूनां पावकश्चासि मेरुः शिखरिणामहम् ॥ २३ ॥

23 And of the Rudras I am Sankara, of the
Yakshas and Rakshasas the Lord of wealth, and
of the Vasus I am Agni, of the mountains I am
the Meru.

पुरोधसां च मुख्यं मां विद्धि पार्थं बृहस्पतिम् ।
सेनानीनामहं स्कन्दः सरसामस्मि सागरः ॥ २४ ॥

And of the household priests of Kings, O
son of Pritha, know Me the chief one, Brihaspati;
of generals I am Skanda, of lakes I am the Ocean.

महर्षाणां भृगुरहं गिरामस्न्येकमक्षरम् ।
यज्ञानां जपयज्ञोऽस्मि स्थावराणां हिमालयः ॥ २५ ॥

Of the Great Rishis I am Bhrigu; of
words I am the one syllable 'Om;' of offerings I
am the offering of Japa (silent repetition), of
unmoving things the Himalaya.

अश्वत्थः सर्ववृक्षाणां देवर्षीणां च नारदः ।
गन्धर्वाणां चित्ररथ: सिद्धानां कपिलो मुनि: ॥ २६ ॥

Of all trees (I am) the Asvattha, and
Narada of divine Rishis, Chitraratha of Gandhar-
vas, the Sage Kapila of the Saints (Siddhas).

उच्चै:श्रवसमघानां विद्धि माममृतोद्भवम् ।
ऐरावतं गजेन्द्राणां नराणां च नराधिपम् ॥ २७ ॥

27, Know Me among horses as Uchchais-
ravas, born of Amrita, of lordly elephants the
Airavata, and of men the king.

आयुधानामहं वज्रं घेनूनामस्मि कामधुक् ।
प्रजनश्चास्मि कन्द्र्पः सर्पाणामस्मि वासुकिः ॥ २८ ॥

Of weapons I am the thunderbolt, of
cows I am the Kamadhuk, I am the progenitor
Kandarpa, of serpents I am Vasuki.

अनन्तश्चासि नागानां वरुणो यादसामहम ।
पितृणामर्यमा चास्मि यमः संयमतामहम् ॥ २९ ॥

And Ananta of snakes I am, I am
Varuna of water-being, and Aryaman of Pitris I
am, I am Yama of controllers.

प्रह्लादश्चास्मि दैत्यानां कालः कल्रयतामहम् ।
मृगाणां च मृगेन्द्रोऽहं वैनतेयश्च पक्षिणाम् ॥ ३० ॥

And Prahlada am I of Diti's progeny, of
reckoners I am Time, and of beasts I am the lord
of beasts, and Vainateya of birds.

पवनः पवतामस्मि रामः राख्नभृतामहम् ।
झवाणां मकरस्रास्मि स्रोतसामस्मि जाह्नवी ।। ३१ ।।

Of purifiers I am the wind, Rama of
warriors am I, of fishes I am the shark, of streams
I am the Ganges.

सर्गाणामादिरन्तश्च मध्यं चैवाहमर्जुन ।
अध्यात्मविद्या विद्यानां वादः प्रवद्तामहम् ॥ ३२ ॥

Of creations I am the beginning and the
middle and also the end; of all knowledges I am
the knowledge of the Self, and Vada of disputants

अक्षराणामकारोऽस्मि द्वन्द्वः सामासिकस्य च ।
अहमेवाक्षयः कालो घाताऽहं विश्वतोमुखः ॥ ३३ ॥

Of letters the letter 'A' am I, and
dvandva of all compounds; I am, verily, the
inexhaustible Time; I am the All-faced Dispenser.

मृत्यः सर्वहरश्चाहमुद्भवश्च भविष्यताम् ।
कीर्ति: श्रीवाक्च नारीणां स्मृतिर्मेघा घृति: क्षमा ॥ ३४ ॥

And I am all-seizing Death, and the
prosperity of those who are to be prosperous; of
the feminine (I am) Fame, Fortune and Speech,
Memory, Intelligence, Constancy, Endurance.

ब्रह्टताम तथा सान्नां गायत्री छन्दसामहम् ।
मासानां मार्गशीर्षोऽहमृतूनां कुसुमाकरः ॥ ३५ ॥

Of Samans also I am the Brihat-Saman
of metres Gayatri am I, of months I am Marga-
sirsha, of seasons the flowery season.

द्यूतं छ्छयतामस्मि तेजस्तेजस्विनामहम् ।
जयोऽस्मि व्यवसायोऽस्मि सत्त्वं सत्त्ववतामहम् ॥ ३६ ॥

I am the gambling of the fraudulent, I
am the splendour of the splendid, I am victory, I
am effort, I am the goodness of the good.

वृष्णीनां वासुदेवोऽस्मि पाण्डवानां धनञ्जयः ।
मुनीनामप्यहं व्यासः कवीनामुराना कविः ॥ ३७ ॥

Of the Vrishnis I am Vasudeva, of the
Pandavas I am Dhananjaya, and of the saints
I am Vyasa, of the sages I am Usanas the sage.

दण्डो दमयतामस्मि नीतिरस्मि जिगीषताम् ।
मौनं चैवास्मि गुह्यानां ज्ञानं ज्ञानवतामहम् ॥ ३८ ॥

Of punishers I am the sceptre, of those
who seek to conquer I am the polity, and of things
secret I am also silence, the knowledge of knowers
am I.

यचापि सर्वभूतानां बीजं तद्हमर्जुन ।
न तदस्ति विना यत्स्यान्मया भूतं चराचरम् ॥ ३९ ॥

And what is the seed of all being,
that also am I, O Arjuna. There is no being,
whether moving or unmoving, that can exist
without me.

नान्तोऽस्ति मम दिव्यानां विभूतीनां परन्तप ।
एष तूद्देशतः प्रोक्तो विभूतेर्विस्तरो मया ॥ ४० ॥

There is no end of My heavenly Glories,
O harasser of thy foes; but the details of My
Glory have been declared only by way of instance.

यद्यद्विभृतिमत्सत्त्वं श्रीमदूर्जितमेव वा।
तत्तदेवावगच्छ त्वं मम तेजोंऽशसम्भवम् ॥ ४१ ॥

Whatever being is glorious, prosperous,
or strong, that know thou to be a manifestation of
a part of My Splendour.

अथवा बहुनैतेन किं ज्ञातेन तवार्जुन ।
विष्टभ्याहमिदं क्वत्स्नमेकांशेन स्थितो जगत् ॥ ४२ ॥

But, of what avail to thee is this vast
things being known, O Arjuna ? I stand sustaining
this whole world by one part (of Myself).

THE UNIVERSAL FORM.

मद्तुप्रहाय परमं गुह्यमध्यात्मसंज्ञितम् ।
यत्त्वयोक्तं वचस्तेन मोहोऽयं विगतो मम ॥ १ ॥

By that speech which has been delivered
by Thee for my benefit,--that highest secret which
is called Adhyatma,-this, my delusion, is gone.

भवाप्ययौ हि भूतानां श्रुतौ विस्तरशो मया ।
त्वत्तः कमलपत्राक्ष माहात्म्यमपि चाव्ययम् ॥ २ ॥

The origin and the dissolution of beings,
verily, have been heard by me in detail from Thee,
O Lotus-eyed, as also Thy inexhaustible greatness.

एवमेतद्यथाऽऽत्थ त्वमात्मानं परमेश्वर ।
द्रष्टुमिच्छामि ते रूपमैश्वरं पुरुषोत्तम ॥ ३ ॥

So it is, as Thou, Supreme Lord, hast
declared Thyself to be. (Still) I desire to see
Thy form as Isvara, O Purusha Supreme.

मन्यसे यदि तच्छक्यं मया द्रष्ट्रमिति प्रभो ।
योगेश्वर ततो मे त्वं दर्शयात्मानमव्ययम् ॥ ४ ॥

If Thou, O Lord, thinkest it possible for
me to see it, do Thou, then, O Lord of Yogins,
show me Thy Eternal Self,

पश्य मे पार्थ रूपाणि शतशोऽथ सहस्रशः ।
नानाविधानि दिव्यानि नानावर्णाकृतीनि च ॥ ५ ॥

See, O son of Pritha, My heavenly forms,
by hundreds and thousands, of different sorts, and
of various colours and shapes.

पत्र्यादित्यान्वसून्रुद्रानश्चिनौ मरुतस्तथा ।
बहून्यदृष्टपूर्वाणि परयाश्चर्याणि भारत ॥ ६ ॥

Behold the Adityas, the Vasus, the
Rudras, the Asvins, and also the Maruts; behold
many marvels never seen before, O Bharata.

इहैकस्थं जगत्कृत्स्न परयाद्य सचराचरम् ।
मम देहे गुडाकेश यचान्यद्रृष्टुमिच्छास ॥ ७ ॥

Now behold here in My body, Gudakesa,
the whole world established in one,-including the
moving and the unmoving-and whatever else
thou desirest to see.

न तु मां शक्यसे द्रष्टुमनेनैव खचक्षुषा ।
दिव्यं ददामि ते चक्षुः पत्र्य मे योगमैश्वरम् ॥ ८ ॥

Thou art not indeed able to see Me with
this thy eye alone; I give thee a divine eye; behold
My lordly Yoga.

एवमुक्तवा ततो राजन् महायोगेश्वरो हरिः ।
दर्शयामास पार्थाय परमं रूपमैश्वरम् ॥ ९ ॥

Having thus spoken, O King, then, Hari,
the great Lord of Yogins, showed to the son of
Pritha the Supreme Form as Isvara.

अनेकवक्रनयनमनेकाद्भुतद्र्शनम् ।
अनेकदिव्याभरणं दिव्यानेकोद्यतायुघम् ॥ १० ॥

Containing many mouths and eyes,
possessed of many wondrous sights, of many
heavenly ornaments, of many heavenly weapons
held up. Such a form He showed.

दिव्यमाल्याम्बरधरं दिव्यगन्धानुलेपनम् ।
सर्वाश्चर्यमयं देवमनन्तं विश्वतोमुखम् ॥ ११ ॥

Wearing heavenly garlands and vestures,
anointed with heavenly unguents, all-wonderful,
resplendent, boundless, with faces on all sides.

दिवि सूर्यसहस्रस्य भवेचुगपदुत्थिता ।
यदि भाः सदृशी सा स्याद्भासस्तस्य महात्मनः ॥ १२ ॥

If the splendour of a thousand suns were
ever to present itself at once in the sky, that
would be like the splendour of that Mighty Being.

तत्रैकस्थं जगत्करस्तं प्रविभक्तमनेकधा ।
अपत्र्यहेवदेवस्य शरीरे पाण्डवस्तदा ॥ १३ ॥

There, in the body of the God of Gods,
the son of Pandu then beheld the whole world
established in one, and separated into many
groups.

ततः स विस्मयाविष्टो हृष्टरोमा धनञ्जयः ।
प्रणम्य शिरसा देवं कताञ्जलिरभाषत ॥ १४ ॥

Then he, Dhananjaya, filled with amaze-
ment, with his hair standing on end; bowed down
with his head, and, with joined palms, thus
addressed the God.

पत्त्यामि देवांस्तव देव देहे
सर्वास्तथा भूतविशेषसङ्घान् ।
ब्रह्माणमीशं कमलासनस्थ-
मृषौत्र सर्वानुरगांश्च दिव्यान् ॥ १५ ॥

I see all the Gods, O God, in thy body,
as also hosts of various classes of beings : Brahma,
the Lord, seated on the lotus-seat, and all Rishis
and heavenly serpents.

अनेकबाहृदरवऋनेत्रम्
पत्र्यामि त्वां सर्वतोऽनन्तरूपम् ।
नान्तं न मध्यं न पुनस्तवादिम
पत्र्यामि विश्वेश्वर विश्वरूप ॥ १६ ॥

I see Thee of boundless form on every
side with multitudinous arms, stomachs, mouths
and eyes; neither Thy end nor the middle nor the
beginning do I see, O Lord of the Universe, O
Universal Form.

किरीटिनं गदिनं चक्रिणं च
तेजोराशि सर्वतो दीप्तिमन्तम ।
पर्यामि त्वां दुर्निरीक्ष्यं समन्तादु
दीप्तानलार्कचुतिमप्रमेयम् ॥ १७ ॥

I see Thee with diadem, club, and discus;
a mass of splendour shining everywhere, very hard
to look at, all around blazing like burning fire and
sun, and immeasurable.

त्वमक्षरं परमं वेदितव्यम् त्वमस्य विश्वस्य परं निघानम् ।
त्वमव्ययः शाश्वतधर्मगोप्ता सनातनस्त्वं पुरुषो मतो मे ॥

Thou art the Imperishable, the Supreme
Being worthy to be known. Thou art the great
Abode of this Universe; Thou art the undying
Guardian of the Eternal Dharma, Thou art the
ancient Purusha, I deem.

अनादिमध्यान्तमनन्तवीर्थ-
मनन्तबाहुं शशिसूर्यनेत्रम् ।
पदयामि त्वां दीप्तहुतारावकम्
खतेजसा विश्वमिदं तपन्तम् ॥ १९ ॥

I see Thee without beginning, middle or
end, infinite in power, of manifold arms; the sun
and the moon being Thy eyes, the burning fire
Thy face; heating the whole Universe with Thy
radiance.

द्यावाप्रथिव्योरिदमन्तरं हि
व्याप्तं त्वयैकेन दिशश्च सर्वाः ।
दृष्टाऽद्भुतं रूपमुप्रं तवेदम्
लोकत्रयं प्रव्यथितं महात्मन् ॥ २० ॥

This space betwixt heaven and earth and
all the quarters are filled by Thee alone. Having
seen This, Thy marvellous and awful form, the
three worlds are trembling, O High-souled Being.

अभी हि त्वां सुरसङ्खा विशन्ति
केचिद्भीताः प्राञ्जलयो गृणन्ति ।
स्वस्तीत्युक्तवा महर्षिसिद्धसङ्घाः
स्तुवन्ति त्वां स्तुतिभिः पुष्कञाभिः ॥ २१ ॥

Into Thee, indeed, enter these hosts of
Suras ; some extol Thee in fear with joined palms;
"May it be well!" thus saying, bands of great
Rishis and Siddhas praise Thee with hymns
complete.

रुद्रादित्या वसवो ये च साध्याः
विश्वेऽश्विनौ मरुतश्चोष्मपाश्च ।
गन्धर्वयक्षासुरासिद्धसङ्खाः
बीक्षन्ते त्वां विस्मिताश्चैव सर्वे ॥ २२ ॥

The Rudras, Adityas, Vasus, and
Sadhyas, Visvas and Asvins, Maruts and Ushma-
pas, hosts of Gandharvas, Yakshas, Asuras, and
Siddhas,-they are all looking at Thee, all quite
astonished.

रूपं महत्ते बहुवक्रनेत्रम्
महाबाहो बहुबाहूरुपादम् ।
बहूद्रं बहुदंष्ट्राकरालम्
दृष्ट्वा लोकाः प्रव्ययितास्तथाऽहम् ॥ २३ ॥

Having seen Thy immeasurable Form,
possessed, O Mighty-armed, of many mouths and
eyes, of many arms and thighs and feet, and of
many stomachs, and fearful with many tusks, the
worlds are terrified, and I also.

नभः स्पृशं दीप्तमनेकवर्णम
व्यात्ताननं दीप्तविशालनेत्रम् ।
दृष्टा हि त्वां प्रव्यथितान्तरात्मा
धातिं न विन्दामि शमं च विष्णो ॥ २४ ॥

On seeing Thee (Thy Form) touching
the sky, blazing in many colours, with mouths wide
open, with large fiery eyes, I am terrified at heart
and find no courage nor peace, O Vishnu.

दंष्ट्राकरालानि च ते मुखानि दृष्ट्वैव कालानलसन्निभानि ।
दिशो न जाने न लभे च शर्म प्रसीद देवेश जगन्निवास ॥

Having seen Thy mouths which are
fearful with tusks and resemble Time's Fires,
I know not the four quarters, nor do I find peace;
be Thou gracious, O Lord of Gods and Abode of
the Universe!

अमी च त्वां धृतराष्ट्रस्य पुत्राः
सर्वे सहैवावनिपालसङ्घेः ।
भीष्मो द्रोण: सूतपुत्रस्तथाऽसौ
सहासादीयैरपि योघमुख्यैः ॥ २६ ॥

वक्राणि ते त्वरमाणा विज्ञान्ति
दंष्ट्राकरालानि भयानकानि ।
केचिद्विलगा दशनान्तरेष
संदृइयन्ते चूर्णितैरुत्तमाङ्गैः ॥ २७ ॥

26-27. And all the sons of Dhritarashtra,
with hosts of princes, Bhishma, Drona and that
son (Karna) of a charioteer, with the warrior chiefs
of ours, enter hurrying into Thy mouth, terrible
with tusks and fearful to behold. Some are found
sticking in the gaps betwixt the teeth with their
heads crushed to powder.

यथा नदीनां बहवोऽम्बुवेगाः
समुद्रमेवाभिमुखा द्रवन्ति ।
तथा तवामी नरलोकवीराः
विज्ञान्ति वक्राण्यभिविज्वलन्ति ॥ २८ ॥

As many torrents of rivers flow direct
towards the sea, so do these heroes in the world of
men enter Thy flaming mouths.

यथा प्रदीप्तं ज्वलनं पतङ्गाः विशन्ति नाशाय समृद्धवेगाः ।
तथैव नाशाय विशन्ति लोकास्तवापि वक्राणि समृद्धवेगाः ॥

As moths hurriedly rush into a blazing
fire for destruction, just so do these creatures also
hurriedly rush into Thy mouths for destruction.

लेलिह्यसे प्रसमानः समन्ता-
झोकान्समग्रान्वद्नैज्वेलद्भिः ।
तेजोभिरापूर्य जगत्सममम्
भासस्तवोग्राः प्रतपन्ति विष्णो ॥ ३० ॥

Thou lickest up devouring all worlds on
every side with Thy flaming mouths, filling the
whole world with flames. Thy fierce rays are
blazing forth, O Vishnu.

आख्याहि मे को भवानुमरूपो
नमोऽस्तु ते देववर प्रसीद ।
विज्ञातुमिच्छामि भवन्तमाद्यम्
न हि प्रजानामि तव प्रवृत्तिम् ॥ ३१ ॥

Tell me who thou art, so fierce in form.
I bow to Thee, O God Supreme; have mercy. I
desire to know Thee, the Original Being. I know
not indeed Thy doing.

श्लीभगवानुवाच
कालोऽस्मि लोकक्षयकृत्प्रवृद्धो
लोकान्समाहर्त्तमिह प्रवृत्तः ।
ऋतेऽपि त्वां न भविष्यन्ति सर्वे
येऽवस्थिताः प्रत्यनीकेषु योघाः ॥ ३२ ॥

I am the mighty world-destroying Time,
now engaged in destroying the worlds. Even
without thee, none of the warriors arrayed in hostile
armies shalt live.

तस्मात्त्वमुत्तिष्ठ यशो लभस्व
जित्वा शत्रून्भुङ्क्ष्व राज्यं समृद्धम् ।
मयैवैते निहताः पूर्वमेव
निमित्तमात्रं भव सव्यसाचिन् ॥ ३३ ॥

Therefore do thou arise and obtain
fame. Conquer the enemies and enjoy the
unrivalled dominion. By Myself have they been
already slain; be thou a mere instrument, <b>O</b>
Savyasachin.

दोणं च भीष्मं च जयद्रथं च
कर्णं तथाऽन्यानपि योधवीरान् ।
मया हतांस्त्वं जहि मा व्यथिष्ठाः
युध्यख जेतासि रणे सपत्नान् ॥ ३४ ॥

Drona and Bhishma, Jayadratha, Karna
and other brave warriors,-these, killed by Me,
do thou kill; fear not, fight, thou shalt conquer
the enemies.

सक्षय उवाच—
एतडळत्वा वचनं केशवस्य
कृताञ्जलिवेपमानः किरीटी ।
नमस्कृत्वा भूय एवाह कृष्णम्
सगद्गदं भीतभीतः प्रणम्य ॥ ३५ ॥

Having heard that speech of Kesava, the
crowned one (Arjuna), with joined palms, trembling,
prostrating himself, again addressed Krishna,
stammering, bowing down, overwhelmed with fear.

स्थाने हृषीकेश तब प्रकीर्या
जगत्प्रहृष्यत्वनुरज्यते च ।
रक्षांसि भीतानि दिशो द्रवन्ति
सर्वे नमस्यन्ति च सिद्धसङ्घाः ॥ ३६ ॥

It is meet, O Hrishikesa, that the world
is delighted and rejoices by Thy praise; Rakshasas.
fly in fear to all quarters, and all hosts of Siddhas
bow to Thee.

कस्माच ते न नमेरन्महात्मन्
गरीयसे ब्रह्मणोऽप्यादिकर्त्रे ।
अनन्त देवेश जगन्निवास
त्वमक्षरं सदसत्तत्परं यत् ॥ ३७ ॥

And how should they not, O Mighty
Being, bow to Thee, Greater (than all else), the
Primal Cause even of Brahma, O Infinite Being,
O Lord of Gods, O Abode of the Universe; Thou
art the Imperishable, the Being and the non-
Being, That which is the Supreme.

त्वमादिदेव: पुरुष: पुराण-
स्त्वमस्य विश्वस्य परं निधानम् ।
वेत्ताऽसि वेद्यं च परं च घास
त्वया ततं विश्वमनन्तरूप ॥ ३८ ॥

Thou art the Primal God, the Ancient
Purusha; Thou art the Supreme Abode of all
this, Thou art the Knower and the Knowable and
the Supreme Abode. By Thee is all pervaded, O
Being of infinite forms.

वायुर्यमोऽग्निर्वरुणः शशाङ्कः
प्रजापतिस्त्वं प्रपितामहश्च ।
नमो नमस्तेऽस्तु सहस्रकृत्वः
पुनश्च भूयोऽपि नमो नमस्ते ॥ ३९ ॥

Thou art Vayu, Yama, Agni, Varuna,
the Moon, Prajapati, and the Great Grand-Father.
Hail! Hail to Thee! a thousand times, and again
and again hail ! hail to Thee !

नमः पुरस्ताद्थ पृष्ठतस्ते
नमोऽस्तु ते सर्वत एव सर्व ।
अनन्तवीर्यामितविक्रमस्त्वम
सर्वं समाप्नोषि ततोऽसि सर्वः ॥ ४० ॥

Hail to Thee before and behind! Hail
to Thee on every side! O All! Thou, infinite in
power and infinite in daring, pervadest all, where-
fore Thou art All.

सखेति मत्वा प्रसभं यदुक्तम्
हे कृष्ण हे यादव हे सखेति ।
अजानता महिमानं तवेदम्
मया प्रमादात्प्रणयेन वापि ॥ ४१ ॥

यचाऽवहासार्थमसत्कृतोऽसि
विहारशय्यासनभोजनेषु ।
एकोऽथवाऽप्यच्युत तत्समक्षम्
तत्क्षामये त्वामहमप्रमेयम् ॥ ४२ ॥

41-42. Whatever was rashly said by me from
carelessness or love, addressing Thee as "O
Krishna, O Yadava, O friend," looking on Thee
merely as a friend, ignorant of this Thy greatness,
-in whatever way I may have insulted Thee for
fun while at play, on bed, in an assembly, or at
meals, when alone, O Achyuta, or in company-
that I implore Thee, Immeasureable, to forgive.

पिताऽसि लोकस्य चराचरस्य
त्वमस्य पूज्यश्च गुरुर्गरीयान् ।
न त्वत्समोऽस्यभ्यधिकः क्रुतोऽन्यो
लोकत्रयेऽप्यप्रतिमप्रभावः ॥ ४३ ॥

Thou art the Father of this world,
moving and unmoving. Thou art to be adored by
this (world), Thou the Greatest Guru; (for) Thy
equal exists not; whence another, superior to
Thee, in the three worlds, O Being of unequalled
greatness?

तस्मात्प्रणम्य प्रणिघाय कायम्
प्रसाद्ये त्वामहमीशमीड्यम् ।
पितेव पुत्रस्य सखेव सख्युः
प्रियः प्रियायाईसि देव सोहुम् ॥ ४४ ॥

Therefore, bowing down, prostrating my
body, I implore Thee, adorable Lord, to forgive.
It is meet Thou shouldst bear with me as the
father with the son, as friend with friend, as the
lover with the beloved.

अदृष्टपूर्व हृषितोऽस्मि दृष्ट्वा
भयेन च प्रव्यथितं मनो मे ।
तदेव मे दर्शय देव रूपम
प्रसीद देवेश जगन्निवास ॥ ४५ ॥

I am delighted, having seen what was
unseen before; and (yet) my mind is confounded
with fear. Show me that form only, O God;
have mercy, O God of Gods, O Abode of the
Universe.

किरीटिनं गदिनं चक्रहस्त-
मिच्छामि त्वां द्रष्टुमहं तथैव ।
तेनैव रूपेण चतुर्भजेन
सहस्रबाहो भव विश्वमूर्ते ॥ ४६ ॥

I wish to see Thee as before, crowned,
possessd of the club, with the discus in the hand,
in Thy former form only, having four arms, O
Thousand-armed, O Universal Form.

मया प्रसन्नेन तवार्जनेदम
रूपं परं दर्शितमात्मयोगात ।
तेजोमयं विश्वमनन्तमाद्यम
यन्मे त्वदन्येन न दृष्टपूर्वम् ॥ ४७ ॥

By Me, gracious to thee, O Arjuna, this
Supreme Form has been shown,-by my sovereign
power-full of splendour, the All, the Boundless,
the Original Form of Mine, never before seen by
any other than thyself.

न वेदयज्ञाध्ययनैर्ने दानै-
र्ने च क्रियाभिने तपोभिष्ठप्रैः ।
एवंरूपः शक्य अहं नृलोके
द्रष्टुं त्वदन्येन कुरुप्रवीर ॥ ४८ ॥

Not by study of the Vedas and of the
sacrifices, nor by gifts, nor by rituals, nor by
severe austerities, can I be seen in this Form in
the world of man by any other than thyself, O
hero of the Kurus.

मा ते व्यथा मा च विमूढभावो
दृष्टा रूपं घोरमीदृज्जमेदम् ।
व्यपेतभी: प्रीतमना: मनस्त्वम
तदेव मे रूपमिदं प्रपञ्च ॥ ४९ ॥

Be not afraid nor bewilderd on seeing
such a terrible form of Mine as this; free from
fear and cheerful at heart, do thou again see this
My former form.

इयर्जुनं वासुदेवस्तथोत्तवा
खकं रूपं दर्शयामास भूयः ।
आश्वासयामास च भीतमेनम्
भूत्वा पुनः सौम्यवपुर्महात्मा ॥ ५० ॥

Having thus spoken to Arjuna, Vasu deva
again showed His Own form; and the Mighty
Being, becoming gentle in form, consoled him
who was terrified.

दृष्ट्वेदं मानुषं रूपं तव सौम्यं जनार्दन ।
इदानीमस्मि संवृत्तः सचेताः प्रकृतिं गतः ॥ ५१ ॥

Having seen Thy gentle human form,
O Janardana, now I have grown serene, and
returned to my nature.

सुदुर्दर्शामेदं रूपं दृष्टवानसि यन्मम ।
देवा अप्यस्य रूपस्य नित्यं दर्शनकाङ्क्षिणः ॥ ५२ ॥

Very hard to see is this Form of Mine
which thou hast seen; even the Devas ever long to
behold this Form.

नाहं वेदैने तपसा न दानेन न चेज्यया।
शक्य एवंविधो द्रष्टुं दृष्टवानसि मां यथा ॥ ५३ ॥

Not by Vedas, nor by austerity, nor
by gifts, nor by sacrifice, can I be seen in this
Form as thou hast seen Me.

भक्तया त्वनन्यया शक्य अहमेवंविधोऽर्जुन ।
ज्ञातुं द्रष्टुं च तत्त्वेन प्रवेष्टुं च परन्तप ॥ ५४ ॥

But by undistracted devotion can I, of
this Form, be known and seen in reality, and
entered into, O harasser of thy foes.

मत्कर्मकुन्मत्परमो मद्भक्तः सङ्गवर्जितः ।
निर्वेरः सर्वभूतेषु यः स मामेति पाण्डव ॥ ५५ ॥

He who does works for Me, who looks
on Me as the Supreme, who is devoted to Me,
who is free from attachment, who is without hat-
red for any being, he comes to Me, O Pandava.

chapter 12

BHAKTI-YOGA.

एवं सततयुक्ता ये भक्तास्वां पर्युपासते ।
ये चाप्यक्षरमव्यक्तं तेषां के योगवित्तमाः ? ॥ १ ॥

Those devotees who, always devout, thus
contemplate Thee, and those also who (contem-
plate) the Imperishable, the Unmanifest,-which
of them are better versed in Yoga ?

मय्यावेरय मनो ये मां नित्ययुक्ता डपासते ।
श्रद्धया परयोपेतास्ते मे युक्ततमा मताः ॥ २ ॥

Those who, fixing their thought on Me,
contemplate Me, always devout, endued with
supreme faith, those in my opinion are the best
Yogins.

ये त्वक्षरमनिर्देश्यमव्यक्तं पर्युपासते ।
सर्वत्रगमचिन्त्यं च कूटस्थमचलं ध्रुवम् ॥ ३ ॥

संनियम्येन्द्रियप्रामं सर्वेत्र समबुद्धय: ।
ते प्राप्नुवन्ति मामेव सर्वभूतहिते रताः ॥ ४ ॥

3-4. Those who ever contemplate the
Imperishable, the Indefinable, the Unmanifest, the
Omnipresent and the Unthinkable, the Unchange-
able, the Immutable, the Eternal,-having res-
trained all the senses, always equanimous, intent
on the welfare of all beings,-they reach Myself.

क्वेशोऽधिकतरस्तेषामव्यक्तासक्तवेतसाम् ।
अव्यक्ता हि गतिर्दुःखं देहवद्भिरवाप्यते ॥ ५ ॥

Greater is their trouble whose thoughts
are set on the Unmanifest; for, the Goal, the Un-
manifest, is very hard for the embodied to reach.

ये तु सर्वाणि कर्माणि मयि संन्यस्य मत्पराः ।
अनन्येनैव योगेन मां घ्यायन्त उपासते ॥ ६ ॥

तेषामहं समुद्धर्ता मृत्युसंसारसागरात् ।
भवामि न चिरात्पार्थं मय्यावेशितचेतसाम् ॥ ७ ॥

6-7. But those who worship Me, renouncing
all actions in Me, regarding Me Supreme, meditat-
ing on Me with exclusive devotion (Yoga); for
them whose thought is fixed on Me, I become
ere long, O son of Pritha, the deliverer out of the
ocean of the mortal samsara.

मय्येव मन आधत्स्व मयि बुद्धि निवेशय ।
निवासिष्यासि मय्येव अत ऊर्ध्वं न संज्ञय: ॥ ८ ॥

Fix thy mind in Me exclusively, apply thy
reason to Me. Thou shalt no doubt live in Me
alone hereafter.

अथ चित्तं समाधातुं न शकोषि मयि स्थिरम्।
अभ्यासयोगेन ततो मामिच्छाप्तुं घनञ्जय ॥ ९ ॥

If thou art unable to fix thy thought
steadily on Me, then by yoga of constant practice
do thou seek to reach Me, O Dhananjaya.

अभ्यासेऽप्यसमर्थोऽसि मत्कर्मपरमो भव ।
मदर्थमपि कर्माणि कुर्वन्तिद्धिमवाप्स्यासि ॥ १० ॥

(If) thou art not equal to practise either,
then be thou intent on (doing) actions for My sake.
Even doing actions for My sake, thou shalt attain
perfection.

अथैतद्प्यज्ञक्तोऽसि कर्तुं मद्योगमाश्रितः ।
सर्वकर्मफळत्यागं ततः कुरु यतात्मवान् ॥ ११ ॥

If thou art unable to do even this, then
refuged in devotion to Me, do thou abandon the
fruits of all actions, self controlled.

श्रेयो हि ज्ञानमम्यासाज्ज्ञानाख्यानं विशिष्यते ।
घ्यानात्कर्मेफऌत्यागस्त्यागाच्छान्तिरनन्तरम् ॥ १२ ॥

Better indeed is knowledge than prac-
tice; than knowledge is meditation more esteemed;
than meditation the abandonment of the fruits of
actions; on abandonment, peace follows immedi-
ately.

अद्वेष्टा सर्वभूतानां मैत्र: करुण एव च ।
निर्ममो निरहङ्कारः समदुःखसुखः क्षमी ॥ १३ ॥

सन्तुष्टः सततं योगी यतात्मा दृढनिश्चयः ।
मच्यर्पितमनोबुद्धियों मे भक्त: स मे प्रिय: ॥ १४ ॥

13-14. He who hates no single being, who is
friendly and compassionate to all, who is free from
attachment and egoism, to whom pain and pleasure
are equal, who is enduring, ever content and
balanced in mind, self-controlled, and possessed of
firm conviction, whose thought and reason are
directed to Me, he who is (thus) devoted to Me is
dear to Me.

यस्मान्नोद्विजते छोको छोकान्नोद्विजते च यः ।
इर्षामर्षभयोद्वेगैर्मुक्तो यः स च मे प्रियः ॥ १५ ॥

He by whom the world is not afflicted
and who is not afflicted by the world, who is free
from joy, envy, fear and sorrow, he is dear to Me.

अनपेक्षः ह्यचिर्देक्ष उदासीनो गतव्यथः ।
सर्वारम्भपरित्यागी यो मद्भक्त: स मे प्रिय: ।। १६ ॥

He who is free from wants, who is pure,
clever, unconcerned, untroubled, renouncing all
undertakings, he who is (thus) devoted to Me is
dear to Me.

यो न हृष्यति न द्वेष्टि न शोचति न काङक्षति ।
ञ्चभाञ्चमपरित्यागी मक्तिमान्यः स मे प्रियः ॥ १७ ॥

He who neither rejoices, nor hates, nor
grieves, nor desires, renouncing good and evil, he
who is full of devotion is dear to Me.

समः शत्रौ च मित्रे च तथा मानावमानयोः ।
शीतोष्णसुखदुःखेषु समः सङ्गविवर्जितः ॥ १८ ॥

तुल्यनिन्दास्तुतिमौंनी सन्तुष्टो येनकेनचित् ।
अनिकेतः स्थिरमतिभेक्तिमान्मे प्रियो नरः ॥ १९ ॥

18-19. He who is the same to foe and friend,
and also in honor and dishonor; who is the same in
cold and heat, in pleasure and pain; who is free
from attachment; to whom censure and praise are
equal; who is silent, content with anything, home-
less, steady-minded, full of devotion; that man is
dear to me.

ये तु धर्म्यामृतमिदं यथोक्तं पर्युपासते ।
श्रद्दधाना मत्परमा भक्तास्तेऽतीव मे प्रिया: ॥ २० ॥

They, verily, who follow this immortal
Law described above, endued with faith, looking
up to me as the Supreme, and devoted, they are
exceedingly dear to Me.

इदं शरीरं कौन्तेय क्षेत्रमित्यभिघीयते ।
एतद्यो वेत्ति तं प्राहुः क्षेत्रज्ञ इति तद्विदः ॥ १ ॥

This, the body, O son of Kunti, is called
Kshetra; him who knows it, they who know of
them call Kshetrajna.

क्षेत्रज्ञं चापि मां विद्धि सर्वक्षेत्रेषु भारत ।
क्षेत्रक्षेत्रज्ञयोर्ज्ञानं यत्तं मम ।। २ ॥

And do thou also know Me as Kshetraina
in all Kshetras, O Bharata. The knowledge of
Kshetra and Khetrajna is deemed by Me as the
knowledge.

तत्क्षेत्रं यच यादृक् च यदिकारि यतश्च यत् ।
स च यो यत्प्रभावश्च तत्समासेन मे ऋणु ॥ ३ ॥

And what that Kshetra is, and of what
nature, and what its changes; and whence is what;
and who He is and what His powers; this hear
thou briefly from Me.

ऋषिभिर्बेहुधा गीतं छन्दोभिर्विविधैः पृथक् ।
ब्रह्मसूत्रपदेश्वैव हेतुमद्भिविनिश्चितै: ॥ ४ ॥

Sung by sages, in many ways and distinct-
ly, in various hymns, as also in the suggestive words
about Brahman, full of reasoning and decisive.

महाभूतान्यहङ्कारो बुद्धिरव्यक्तमेव च।
इन्द्रियाणि दशैकं च पञ्च चेन्द्रियगोचराः ॥ ५ ॥

The Great Elements, Egoism, Reason, as
also the Unmanifested, the ten senses and one, and
the five objects of the senses ;

इच्छा द्वेष: सुखं दु:खं सङ्घातश्चेतना घृतिः ।
एतत्क्षेत्रं समासेन सविकारमुदाहृतम् ॥ ६ ॥

Desire, hatred, pleasure, pain, the aggre-
gate, intelligence, courage ;- the Kshetra has been
thus briefly described with its modifications.

अमानित्वमद्म्भित्वमहिंसा क्षान्तिरार्जवम् ।
आचार्योपासनं शौचं स्वैर्यमात्मविनिग्रहः ॥ ७ ॥

Humility, modesty, innocence, patience,
uprightness, service of the teacher, purity, stead-
fastness, self-control ;

इन्द्रियार्थेषु वैराग्यमनहङ्कार एव च ।
जन्ममृत्युजराव्याधिदुःखदोषानुदर्शनम् ॥ ८ ॥

Absence of attachment for objects of the
senses, and also absence of egoism ; perception of
evil in birth, death and old age, in sickness and
pain ;

असक्तिरनभिष्वङ्गः पुत्रदारगृहादिषु ।
नित्यं च समचित्तत्वभिष्टानिष्टोपपत्तिषु ॥ ९ ॥

Unattachment, absence of affection for
son, wife, home and the like, and constant equani-
mity on the attainment of the desirable and the
undesirable ;

मयि चानन्ययोगेन भक्तिरव्यभिचारिणी ।
विविक्तदेशसेवित्वमरतिर्जनसंसदि ॥ १० ॥

Unflinching devotion to Me in Yoga of
non-separation, resort to solitary places, distaste
for the society of men;

अध्यात्मज्ञाननित्यत्वं तत्त्वज्ञानार्थद्र्शनम् ।
एतब्ज्ञानमिति पोक्तमज्ञानं यदतोऽन्यथा ॥ ११ ॥

Constancy in Self-knowledge, perception
of the end of the knowledge of truth. This is
declared to be knowledge, and what is opposed to
it is ignorance.

ज्ञेयं यत्तत्प्रवक्ष्यामि यज्ज्ञात्वाऽमृतमइनुते ।
अनादिमत्परं ब्रह्म न सत्तन्नासदुच्यते ॥ १२ ॥

That which has to be known I shall
describe; knowing which one attains the Immortal.
Beginningless is the Supreme Brahman. It is not
said to be 'sat' or 'asat.'

सर्वतःपाणिपादं तत्सर्वतोऽक्षिशिरोमुखम् ।
सर्वतः श्रुतिमञ्जोके सर्वमावृत्य तिष्ठति ।। १३ ।।

With hands and feet everywhere, with
eyes and heads and mouths everywhere, with
hearing everywhere, That exists enveloping all.

सर्वेन्द्रियगुणाभासं सर्वेन्द्रियविवर्जितम ।
असक्तं सर्वभृचैव निर्गुणं गुणभोक्तृ च ॥ १४ ॥

Shining by the functions of all the
senses, (yet) without the senses; unattached, yet
supporting all ; devoid of qualities.

बहिरन्तश्च भूतानामचरं चरमेव च ।
सूक्ष्मत्वात्तदविज्ञेयं दूरस्थं चान्तिके च तत् ॥ १५ ॥

Without and within (all) beings; the
unmoving as also the moving. Because subtle,
That is incomprehensible; and near and far away
is That,

अविभक्तं च भूतेषु विभक्तमिव च स्थितम् ।
भूतभर्तृ च तज्ज्ञेयं प्रसिष्णु प्रभविष्णु च ॥ १६ ॥

And undivided, yet remaining divided as
it were in beings; supporter of beings, too is That,
the Knowable; devouring, yet generating.

ज्योतिषामपि तज्जयोतिस्तमसः परमुच्यते ।
ज्ञानं ज्ञेयं ज्ञानगम्यं हृदि सर्वस्य घिष्ठितम् ॥ १७ ॥

The Light even of lights, That is said to
be beyond darkness. Knowledge, the Knowable,
the Goal of knowledge, (It) is implanted in the
heart of every one.

इति क्षेत्रं तथा ज्ञानं ज्ञेयं चोक्तं समासतः ।
मद्भक्त एतद्विज्ञाय मद्भावायोपपद्यते ॥ १८ ॥

Thus the Kshetra, as well as knowledge
and the Knowable, have been briefly set forth.
My devotee, on knowing this, is fitted for My
state.

प्रकृतिं पुरुषं चैव विच्चनादी उभावपि ।
विकारांश्च गुणांश्चेव विद्धि प्रकृतिसम्भवान् ॥ १९ ॥

Know thou that Prakriti as well
Purusha are both beginningless; and know thou
also that all forms and qualities are born of
Prakriti.

कार्यकारणकर्तृत्वे हेतुः प्रकृतिरुच्यते ।
पुरुष: सुखदु:खानां भोक्तृत्वे हेतुरुच्यते ।। २० ॥

As the producer of the effect and the
instruments §, Prakriti is said to be the cause ; as
experiencing pleasure and pain, Purusha is said to
be the cause.

पुरुष: प्रकृतिस्थो हि सुङ्क्ते प्रकृतिजान्गुणान् ।
कारणं गुणसङ्गोऽस्य सदसचोानिजन्मसु ॥ २१ ॥

Purusha, when seated in Prakriti, expe-
riences the qualities born of Prakriti. Attachment
to the qualities is the cause of his birth in good
and evil wombs.

उपद्रष्टाऽनुमन्ता च भर्ता भोक्ता महेश्वरः ।
परमात्मेति चाप्युक्तो देहेऽस्मिन्पुरुषः परः ॥ २२ ॥

Spectator and Permitter, Supporter, En-
joyer, the Great Lord, and also spoken of as the
Supreme Self, (is) the Purusha Supreme in this
body.

य एवं वेत्ति पुरुषं प्रकृतिं च गुणैः सह।
सर्वथा वर्तमानोऽपि न स भूयोऽभिजायते ॥ २३ ॥

He who thus knows Purusha and Prakriti
together with qualities, whatever his conduct, he
is not born again.

ध्यानेनात्मनि पत्र्यन्ति केचिदात्मानमात्मना ।
अन्ये साङ्ख्येन योगेन कर्मयोगेन चापरे ॥ २४ ॥

By meditation some behold the Self in
the self by the self others by Sankhya Yoga, and
others by Karma-Yoga.

अन्ये त्वेवमजानन्तः श्रुत्वाऽन्येभ्य उपासते ।
तेऽपि चातितरन्त्येव मृत्युं श्रुतिपरायणाः ॥ २५ ॥

Yet others, not knowing thus, worship,
having heard from others ; they, too, cross beyond
death, adhering to what they heard.

यावत्सञ्जायते किञ्चित्सन्त्वं स्थावरजङ्गमम् ।
क्षेत्रक्षेत्रज्ञसंयोगात्तद्विद्धि भरतर्षभ ॥ २६ ॥

Whatever being is born, the unmoving or
the moving, know thou, O best of the Bharatas,
that to be owing to the union of Kshetra and
Kshetrajna.*

समं सर्वेषु भूतेषु तिष्ठन्तं परमेश्वरम् ।
विनइयत्स्वविनइयन्तं यः पइयति स पइयति ॥ २७ ॥

He sees who sees the Supreme Lord,
remaining the same in all beings, the andying in
the dying.

समं पद्मयन्हि सर्वत्र समवस्थितमीश्वरम ।
न हिनस्त्यात्मनाऽऽत्मानं ततो याति परां गतिम् ॥ २८ ॥

Because he who sees the Lord, seated
the same everywhere, destroys not the self by the
self, therefore he reaches the Supreme Goal.

प्रकृत्यैव च कर्माणि क्रियमाणानि सर्वेज्ञ: ।
यः पत्त्यति तथाऽऽत्मानमकर्तारं स पत्र्यति ॥ २९ ॥

He sees, who sees all actions performed
by Prakriti alone and the Self not acting.

यदा भूतपृथग्भावमेकस्थमनुपरयति ।
तत एव च विस्तारं ब्रह्म सम्पद्यते तदा ॥ ३० ॥

When a man realises the whole variety
of beings as resting in the One, and is an evolu-
tion from that (One) alone, then he becomes
Brahman.

अनादित्वान्निर्गुणत्वात्परमात्माऽयमव्ययः ।
शरीरस्थोऽपि कौन्तेय न करोति न लिप्यते ।। ३१ ।।

Having no beginning, having no quali-
ties, this Supreme Self, imperishable, though
dwelling in the Body, O son of Kunti, neither acts
nor is tainted.

यथा सर्वगतं सौक्ष्म्यादाकाशं नोपलिप्यते ।
सर्वत्रावस्थितो देहे तथाऽऽत्मा नोपलिप्यते ॥ ३२ ॥

As the all-pervading akasa is, from its
subtlety <sup>†</sup>, never soiled, so the Self seated in the
body everywhere is not soiled.

यथा प्रकाशयलेकः कृत्स्नं लोकमिमं रविः ।
क्षेत्रं क्षेत्री तथा कृत्स्नं प्रकाशयति भारत ।। ३३ ॥

As the one sun illumines all this world,
so does the embodied One, O Bharata, illumines
all bodies.

क्षेत्रक्षेत्रज्ञयोरेवमन्तरं ज्ञानचक्षुषा ।
भूतप्रकृतिमोक्षं च ये विदुर्यान्ति ते परम् ॥ ३४ ॥

They who by the eye of wisdom perceive
the distinction between Kshetra and Kshetrajna,
and the dissolution of the Cause of beings,-they
go to the Supreme.

परं भूयः प्रवक्ष्यामि ज्ञानानां ज्ञानमुत्तमम् ।
यन्ज्ञात्वा मुनयः सर्वे परां सिद्धिमितो गताः ॥ १ ॥

I shall again declare that sublime know-
ledge, the best of all knowledges; which having
learnt, all the sages have passed to high perfection
from here.

इदं ज्ञानमुपाश्रिल मम लाधर्म्यमागताः ।
सर्गेऽपि नोपजायन्ते प्रलये न व्यथन्ति च ॥ २ ॥

They who, having resorted to this know-
ledge, have attained to unity with Me, are neither
born in the creation, nor disturbed in the dissolution.

मम योनिर्महद्वद्वा तस्मिन् गर्भं द्धाम्यहम् ।
सम्भवः सर्वभूतानां ततो भवति भारत ॥ ३ ॥

My womb is the great Brahman ; in that
I place the germ; thence, O Bharata, is the birth
of all beings.

सर्वयोनिषु कौन्तेय मूर्तयः सम्भवन्ति याः ।
तासां ब्रह्म महद्योनिरहं बीजप्रदः पिता ॥ ४ ॥

Whatever forms are produced, O son of
Kunti, in any wombs whatsoever, the Great.
Brahman is their womb, I the seed-giving Father.

सत्त्वं रजस्तम इति गुणाः प्रकृतिसम्भवाः ।
निबभ्नान्ति महाबाहो देहे देहिनमव्ययम् ॥ ५ ॥

Sattva, Rajas, Tamas,-these gunas, O
mighty-armed, born of Prakriti, bind fast in the
body the embodied, the indestructible.

तत्र सत्त्वं निर्मळत्वात्प्रकाशकमनामयम् ।
सुखसङ्गेन बघ्नाति ज्ञानसङ्गेन चानघ ॥ ६ ॥

Of these, Sattva, which, from its stainless-
ness, is lucid and healthy, binds by attachment to
happiness and by attachment to knowledge, O
sinless one.

रजो रागात्मकं विद्धि तृष्णासङ्गसमुद्भवम् ।
तन्निबघ्नाति कौन्तेय कर्मसङ्गेन देहिनम् ॥ ७ ॥

Know thou Rajas (to be) of the nature of
passion, the source of thirst and attachment ; it
binds fast, O son of Kunti, the embodied one by
attachment to action.

तमस्त्वज्ञानजंविद्धि मोहनं सर्वदेहिनाम ।
प्रमादाऌस्यनिद्राभिस्तन्निबघ्नाति भारत ॥ ८ ॥

But, know thou Tamas to be born of
unwisdom, deluding all embodied beings; by heed-
lessness, indolence and sloth, it binds fast, O
Bharata.

सत्त्वं सुखे सञ्जयति रजः कर्मणि भारत ।
ज्ञानमावृत्य तु तमः प्रमादे सञ्जयत्युत ॥ ९ ॥

Sattva attaches to happiness, Rajas to
action, O Bharata, while Tamas, enshrouding
wisdom, attaches, on the contrary, to heedlessness.

रजस्तमश्चाभिभूय सत्त्वं भवति भारत ।
रजः सत्त्वं तमश्चैव तमः सत्त्वं रजस्तथा ॥ १० ॥

Sattva arises, O Bharata, predominating
over Rajas and Tamas; and Rajas, over Sattva
and Tamas ; so Tamas, over Sattva and Rajas.

सर्वद्वारेषु देहेऽस्मिन्प्रकाश उपजायते ।
ज्ञानं यदा तदा विद्याद्विदृद्धं सत्त्वमित्युत ॥ ११ ॥

When at every gate in this body there
shoots up wisdom-light, then it may be known that
Sattva is predominant.

लोभः प्रवृत्तिरारम्भः कर्मणामरामः स्पृहा ।
रजस्येतानि जायन्ते विवृद्धे भरतर्षेभ ॥ १२ ॥

Greed, activity, the undertaking of works,
unrest, desire-these arise when Rajas is predomi-
nant, O lord of the Bharatas.

अप्रकाशोऽप्रवृत्तिश्च प्रमादो मोह एव च ।
तमस्येतानि जायन्ते विवृद्घे क़रुनन्दन ॥ १३ ॥

Darkness, heedlessness, inertness, and
error,-these arise when Tamas is predominant, O
descendant of Kuru.

यदा सत्त्वे प्रवृद्धे तु प्रलयं याति देहभृत् ।
तदोत्तमविदां लोकानमलान्प्रतिपद्यते ॥ १४ ॥

If the embodied one meets death when
Sattva is predominant, then he attains to the spot-
less regions * of the knowers of the Highest.

रजसि प्रलयं गत्वा कर्मसङ्गिषु जायते ।
तथा प्रळीनस्तमसि मूढयोनिषु जायते ॥ १५ ॥

Meeting death in Rajas, he is born
among those attached to action; and, dying in
Tamas, he is born in the wombs ot the irrational.

कर्मणः सुकृतस्याद्वः सात्त्विकं निर्मेलं फलम् ।
रजसस्तु फलं दुःखमज्ञानं तमसः फलम् ॥ १६ ॥

The fruit of good action, they say, is
Sattvic † and pure; while the fruit of Rajas is
pain, and ignorance is the fruit of Tamas.

सत्त्वात्सञ्जायते ज्ञानं रजसो लोभ एव च ।
प्रमादमोहौ तमसो भवतोऽज्ञानमेव च ॥ १७ ॥

From Sattva arises wisdom, and greed
from Rajas; heedlessness and error arise from
Tamas, and also ignorance.

ऊर्ध्वं गच्छन्ति सत्त्वस्था मध्ये तिष्ठन्ति राजसाः ।
जघन्यगुणवृत्तिस्था अघो गच्छन्ति तामसा: ॥ १८ ॥

Those who follow Sattva go upwards :
the Rajasic remain in the middle; and the Tama-
sic, who follow in the coarse of the lowest guna,
go downwards.

नान्यं गुणेभ्यः कर्तारं यदा द्रष्टाऽनुपरयति ।
गुणेभ्यश्च परं वेत्ति मद्भावं सोऽधिगच्छति ॥ १९ ॥

When the seer beholds not an agent
other than the gunas and knows Him who is
higher than the gunas, he attains to My being.

गुणानेतानतील त्रीन्देही देहसमुद्भवान् ।
जन्ममृत्यूजरादुःखैर्विमुक्तोऽमृतमद्गुते ॥ २० ॥

Having crossed beyond these three
gunas, which are the source of the body, the
embodied one is freed from birth, death, decay
and pain, and attains the immortal.

कैर्लिङ्गैबान्गुणानेतानतीतो भवति प्रभो ।
किमाचारः कथं चैतांबीन्गुणानतिवर्तते ।। २१ ।।

By what marks, O Lord, is he known
who has crossed beyond those three gunas?
What is his conduct, and how does he pass beyond
those three gunas.

प्रकाशं च प्रवृत्तिं च मोहमेव च पाण्डव ।
न द्वेष्टिसम्प्रवृत्तानि न निवृत्तानि काङ्कृति ॥ २२ ॥

Light and activity and delusion present,
O Pandava, he hates not, nor longs for them
absent.

डदासीनवदासीनो गुणैयों न विचाल्यते ।
गुणा वर्तन्त इत्येव योऽवतिष्ठति नेङ्गते ॥ २३ ॥

He who, seated as a neutral, is not
moved by gunas ; who, thinking that gunas act, is
firm and moves not;

समदुःखसुखः खश्चः समलोष्टाइमकाञ्चनः ।
तुल्यप्रियाप्रियो धीरस्तुल्यनिन्दात्मसंस्तुतिः ॥ २४ ॥

He to whom pain and pleasure are alike.
who dwells in the Self, to whom a clod of earth
and stone and gold are alike, to whom the dear
and the undear are alike, who is a man of wisdom,
to whom censure and praise are same;

मानापमानयोस्तुल्यस्तुल्यो मित्रारिपक्षयोः ।
सर्वारम्भपरित्यागी गुणातीतः स उच्यते ॥ २५ ॥

The same in honour and disgrace, the
same towards friends and enemies, abandoning all
undertakings, - he is said to have crossed beyond
the gunas.

मां च योऽव्यभिचारेण भक्तियोगेन सेवते ।
स गुणान्समतीलैतान् ब्रह्मभूयाय कल्पते ॥ २६ ॥

And he who serves Me with unfailing
Devotion of Love, he, crossing beyond those three
gunas, is fitted for becoming Brahman.

त्रह्मणो हि प्रतिष्ठाऽहममृतस्याव्ययस्य च ।
शाश्वतस्य च धर्मस्य सुखस्पैकान्तिकस्य च ॥ २७ ॥

For I am the abode of Brahman, the
Immortal and the Immutable, the Eternal Dharma,
and the unfailing Bliss.

chapter 15

THE SUPREME SPIRIT.

अर्थ्वमूलमधःशाखमश्वत्थं प्राहुरव्ययम् ।
छन्दांसि यस्य पर्णानि यस्तं वेद स वेदवित् ।। १ ।।

They speak of the indestructible Asvattha
having its root above and branches below, whose
leaves are the metres. He who knows it knows
the Vedas.

अध्योध्वे प्रसृतास्तस्य शाखाः
गुणप्रवृद्धा विषयप्रवालाः ।
अधश्व मूलान्यनुसन्ततानि
कर्मानुबन्धीनि मनुष्यळोके ॥ २ ॥

Below and above are its branches spread.
nourished by the gunas, sense-objects its buds;
and below in the world of man stretch forth the
roots ending in action.

न रूपमस्येह तथोपलभ्यते नान्तो न चादिने च सम्प्रतिष्ठा ।
अश्वत्थमेनं सुविरूढमूलमसङ्गरास्त्रेण दृढेन छित्त्वा ॥ ३ ॥

Its form is not perceived as such here,
neither its end nor its origin nor its existence.
Having cut asunder this firm-rooted Asvattha
with the strong sword of dispassion.

ततः पदं तत्परिमार्गितव्यं यस्मिन्गता न निवर्तेन्ति भूयः ।
तमेव चाद्यं पुरुषं प्रपद्ये यतः प्रवृत्तिः प्रसृता पुराणी ॥ ४ ॥

Then That Goal should be sought for,
whither having gone none return again. "I seek
refuge in that Primeval Purusha whence streamed
forth the Ancient Current."

निर्मानमोहा जितसङ्घदोषाः
अध्यात्मनित्या विनिवृत्तकामाः ।
द्वन्दैविमुक्ताः सुखदुःखसंज्ञे-
र्गच्छन्त्यमूढाः पद्मव्ययं तत् ॥ ५ ॥

Free from pride and delusion, with the
evil of attachment conquered, ever dwelling in the
Self, their desires having completely turned away,
liberated from the pairs of opposites known as
pleasure and pain, the undeluded reach that Goal
Eternal.

न तद्भासयते सूर्यो न शशाङ्को न पावकः ।
यद्गत्वा न निवर्तन्ते तद्धाम परमं मम ॥ ६ ॥

That the sun illumines not, nor the moon,
nor fire; That is My Supreme Abode, to which
having gone none return.

ममैवांशो जीवळोके जीवभूतः सनातनः ।
मनःषष्ठानीन्द्रियाणि प्रकृतिस्थानि कर्षति ॥ ७ ॥

A ray of Myself, the eternal Jiva in the
world of Jivas, attracts the senses, with manas the
sixth, abiding in Prakriti.

शरीरं यद्वाप्नोति यचाप्युत्कामतीश्वरः ।
गृहीत्वैतानि संयाति वायुर्गन्घानिवाशयात् ॥ ८ ॥

When the Lord acquires a body, and
when He leaves it, He takes these and goes, as the
wind takes scents from their seats.

श्रोत्रं चक्षुः स्पर्शनं च रसनं घाणमेव च ।
अधिष्ठाय मनश्चायं विषयानुपसेवते ॥ ९ ॥

The ear, the eye and the touch, the taste
and the smell, using these and the manas, he
enjoys the sense-objects.

उत्क्रामन्तं स्थितं वापि भुञ्जानं वा गुणान्वितम् ।
विमृढा नानुपर्यन्ति पर्यन्ति ज्ञानचञ्चुषः ॥ १० ॥

Him who departs, stays and enjoys, who
is conjoined with gunas, the deluded perceive not;
they see, who possess the eye of knowledge.

यतन्तो योगिनश्चैनं पत्रयन्त्यात्मन्यवस्थितम् ।
यतन्तोऽप्यकृतात्मानो नैनं पइयन्त्यचेतसः ॥ ११ ॥

Those who strive, endued with Yoga,
perceive Him dwelling in the self ; though striving,
those of unrefined self, devoid of wisdom, perceive
Him not.

यदादित्यगतं तेजो जगद्भासयतेऽखिलम् ।
यचन्द्रमसि यचामौ तत्तेजो विद्धि मामकम ॥ १२ ॥

That light which residing in the sun illu-
mines the whole world, that which is in the moon
and in the fire, that light do thou know to be
Mine.

गामाविइय च भूतानि घारयाम्यहमोजसा ।
पुष्णामि चौषधीः सर्वाः सोमो भूत्वा रसात्मकः ॥ १३ ॥

Penetrating the earth I support all beings
by (My) Energy ; and having become the watery
moon I nourish all herbs.

अहं वैश्वानरो भूत्वा प्राणिनां देहमाश्रितः ।
प्राणापानसमायुक्तः पचाम्यन्नं चतुर्विधम् ॥ १४ ॥

Abiding in the body of living beings as
Vaisvanara, associated* with Prana and Apana, I
digest the fourfold food.

सर्वस्य चाहं हृदि सन्निविष्टो मत्तः स्मृतिर्ज्ञानमपोहनं च ।
वेदैश्च सर्वेरहमेव वेद्यो वेदान्तकृद्वेदविदेव चाहम् ॥ १५ ॥

And I am seated in the hearts of all : from
Me are memory, knowledge, as well as their loss;
it is I who am to be known by all the Vedas, I am
indeed the author of the Vedanta as well as the
knower of the Vedas.

द्वाविमौ पुरुषौ लोके क्षरश्चाक्षर एव च ।
क्षरः सर्वाणि भूतानि कूटस्योऽक्षर उच्यते ॥ १६ ॥

There are these two beings in the world
the perishable and the imperishable: the perisha-
ble comprises all creatures, the immutable is ealled
the imperishable.

उत्तमः पुरुषस्त्वन्यः परमात्मेत्युदाहृतः ।
यो लोकत्रयमावित्त्य बिभर्त्यव्यय ईश्वरः ॥ १७ ॥

But distinct is the Highest Spirit spoken
of as the Supreme Self, the indestructible Lord
who penetrates and sustains the three worlds.

यस्मात्क्षरमतीतोऽहमक्षरादपि चोत्तमः ।
अतोऽस्मि लोके वेदे च प्रथित: पुरुषोत्तम: ॥ १८ ॥

Because I transcend the perishable and
am even higher than the imperishable, therefore
am I known in the world and in the Veda as
'Purushottama,' the Highest Spirit.

यो मामेवमसम्मूढो जानाति पुरुषोत्तमम् ।
स सर्वविद्भजति मां सर्वभावेन भारत ॥ १९ ॥

He who, undeluded,* thus knows Me, the
Highest Spirit, he, knowing all, worships Me with
his whole being, O Bharata.

इति गुद्यतमं शास्त्रमिद्मुक्तं मयाऽनघ ।
एतद्बुध्वा बुद्धिमान् स्यात्कृतकृत्यश्च भारत ॥ २० ॥

Thus, this most Secret Science has been
taught by Me, O sinless one; on knowing this,
(a man) becomes wise, O Bharata, and all his
duties are accomplished.

SPIRITUALITY AND MATERIALISM

अभयं सत्त्वसंञ्जब्रिज्ञोनयोगव्यवस्थितिः ।
दानं दमश्च यज्ञश्च स्वाध्यायस्तप आर्जवम् ॥ १ ॥

Fearlessness +, purity of heart, steadfast-
ness in knowledge and Yoga; alms-giving, self-
restraint and worship, study of one's own (scrip-
tures), austerity, uprightness;

अहिंसा सत्यमक्रोघस्त्यागः शान्तिरपैग्लनम् ।
द्या भूतेष्वळोळुप्त्वं मार्देवं ह्रीरचापलम् ॥ २ ॥

Harmlessness, truth, absence of anger,
renunciation, serenity, absence of calumny, com-
passion to creatures, uncovetousness, gentleness,
modesty, absence of fickleness;

तेजः क्षमा घृतिः शौचमद्रोहो नातिमानिता ।
भवन्ति सम्पदं दैवीमभिजातस्य भारत ॥ ३ ॥

Energy, forgiveness; fortitude, purity.
absence of harred, absence of pride; these belong
to one born for a divine lot, O Bharata.

दम्भो दर्पोऽभिमानश्च ऋोधः पारुष्यमेव च ।
अज्ञानं चाभिजातस्य पार्थं सम्पदमासुरीम् ॥ ४ ॥

Ostentation, arrogance and self-conceit
anger as also insolence, and ignorance, belong to
one who is born, O Partha, for a demoniac lot.

दैवी सम्पद्विमोक्षाय निबन्धायासुरी मता ।
मा ग्लुचः सम्पदं दैवीमभिजातोऽसि पाण्डव ॥ ५ ॥

The divine nature is deemed for liberation,
the demoniac for bondage. Grieve not, O Pan-
dava; thou art born for a divine lot.

द्वौ भूतसर्गों लोकेऽस्मिन्दैव आसुर एव च ।
दैवो विस्तरशः प्रोक्त आसुरं पार्थ मे शृणु ॥ ६ ॥

There are two creations of beings in this
world, the divine and the demoniac. The divine
has been described at length; hear from Me, O
Partha, of the demoniac.

प्रवृत्ति च निवृत्ति च जना न विदुरासुरा: ।
न शोंचं नापि चाचारो न सत्यं तेषु विद्यते ॥ ७ ॥

Neither action nor inaction do the
demoniac men know; neither purity nor good
conduct nor trurh is found in them.

असत्यमप्रतिष्ठं ते जगदाहुरनीश्वरम् ।
अपरस्परसम्भूतं किमन्यत्कामहैतुकम् ॥ ८ ॥

They say, "the universe is unreal, without
a basis, without a Lord, born of mutual union,
brought about by lust; what else?"

एतां दृष्टिमवष्टभ्य नष्टात्मानोऽल्पबुद्धयः ।
प्रभवन्त्युप्रकर्माणः क्षयाय जगतोऽहिताः ॥ ९ ॥

Holding this view, these ruined souls of
small intellect, of fierce deeds, rise as the enemies
of the world * for its destruction.

काममाश्रित्य दुष्पूरं दम्भमानमदान्विताः ।
मोहादुगृहीत्वाऽसद्बाहान्प्रवर्तन्तेऽञ्जवित्रताः ॥ १० ॥

Filled with insatiable desires, full of
hypocrisy, pride and arrogance, holding unwhole-
some views through delusion, they work with
unholy resolve;

चिन्तामपरिमेयां च प्रछयान्तामुपाश्रिताः ।
कामोपभोगपरमा एतावदिति निश्चिताः ॥ ११ ॥

Beset with immense cares ending only
with death, sensual enjoyment their highest
aim.
assured that that is all †;

आशापाशशतैर्बद्धाः कामक्रोधपरायणाः ।
ईहन्ते कामभोगार्थमन्यायेनार्थसञ्चयान् ॥ १२ ॥

Bound by hundreds of bands of hope,
given over to lust and wrath, they strive to secure
by unjust means hoards of wealth for sensual enjoy-
ment.

इदमद्य मया लब्धमिमं प्राप्स्ये मनोरथम् ।
इदमस्तीदमपि मे भविष्यति पुनर्धनम् ।। १३ ।।

This to-day has been gained by me; this
desire I shall attain; this is mine, and this wealth
also shall be mine in future.

असौ मया हतः शत्रुईनिष्ये चापरानपि ।
ईश्वरोऽहमहं भोगी सिद्धोऽहं बळवान्सुखी ॥ १४ ॥

"That enemy has been slain by me, and
others also shall I slay. I am a lord, I enjoy, I
am successful, strong and healthy."

आढ्योऽभिजनवानस्मि कोऽन्योऽस्ति सदृशो मया ।
यक्ष्ये दास्यामि मोदिष्य इत्यज्ञानविमोहिताः ॥ १५ ॥

"I am rich and well-born. Who else is
equal to me? I will sacrifice, I will give, I will
rejoice." Thus deluded by unwisdom.

अनेकचित्तविभ्रान्ता मोहजालसमावृताः ।
प्रसक्ताः कामभोगेषु पतन्ति नरकेऽग्लुचौ ॥ १६ ॥

Bewildered by many a fancy, entangled
in the snare of delusion, addicted to the gratifica-
tion of lust, they fall into a foul hell.

आत्मसम्भाविताः स्तब्धा घनमानमदान्विताः ।
यजन्ते नामयज्ञैस्ते दम्भेनाविधिपूर्वकम् ॥ १७ ॥

Self-honored, stubborn, filled with the
pride and intoxication of wealth, they perform
sacrifices in name with hypocrisy, without regard
to ordinance.

अहङ्कारं बळं दुपै कामं क्रोघं च संश्रिताः ।
मामात्मपरदेहेषु प्रद्विषन्तोऽभ्यसूयकाः ॥ १८ ॥

Given over to egotism, power, haughti-
ness, lust, and anger, these malicious people hate
Me in their own and others' bodies.

तानहं द्विषतः क्रूरान्संसारेषु नराधमान् ।
क्षिपाम्यजस्रमञ्जमानासुरीष्वेव योनिषु ॥ १९ ॥

These cruel haters, worst of men, I hurl
these evil-doers for ever in the worlds into the
wombs of the demons only.

आसुरी योनिमापन्ना मूढा जन्मनि जन्मनि ।
मामप्राप्यैव कौन्तेय ततो यान्त्यधमां गतिम् ॥ २० ॥

Entering into demoniac wombs, the de-
luded ones, in birth after birth, without ever
reaching Me, O son of Kunti, pass into a condi-
tion still lower than that.

त्रिविधं नरकस्येदं द्वारं नाजनमात्मनः ।
कामः क्रोघस्तथा लोभस्तस्मादेतत्त्रयं त्यजेत ॥ २१ ॥

Triple is this, the gate to hell, destruc-
tive of the self : LUST, WRATH, and GREED. There-
fore, these three, one should abandon.

एतैर्विमुक्तः कौन्तेय तमोद्वारौन्निमिर्नरः ।
आचरत्यात्मनः श्रेयस्ततो याति परां गतिम् ॥ २२ ॥

A man who is released from these, the
three gates to darkness, O son of Kunti, does good
to the self, and thereby reaches the Supreme Goal.

यः शास्त्रविधिमुत्सृज्य वर्तते कामकारतः ।
न स सिद्धिमवाप्नोति न सुखं न परां गतिम् ॥ २३ ॥

He who, neglecting the scriptural ordin-
ance, acts under the impulse of desire, attains not
perfection, nor happiness, nor the Supreme Goal.

तस्माच्छास्नं प्रमाणं ते कार्याकार्यव्यवस्थितौ ।
ज्ञात्वा शास्त्रविधानोक्तं कर्म कर्तुमिहार्हसि ॥ २४ ॥

Therefore, the scripture is thy authority
in deciding as to what ought to be done and what
ought not to be done. Now, thou oughtest to
know and perform thy duty laid down in the scrip-
ture-law.

chapter 17

THE THREEFOLD FAITH.

ये शास्त्रविधिमुत्सृज्य यजन्ते श्रद्धयाऽन्विता. ।
तेषां निष्ठा तु का कृष्ण सत्त्वमाहो रजस्तमः ॥ १ ॥

Whoso worship, setting aside the ordi-
nance of the scripture, endued with faith,-what
faith is theirs ? Is it Sattva, or Rajas, or Tamas ?

त्रिविधा भवति श्रद्धा देहिनां सा स्वभावजा ।
सारिवकी राजसी चैव तामसी चेति तां ऋणु ॥ २ ॥

Threefold is that faith born of the indivi-
dual nature of the embodied,-Sattvic, Rajasic,
and Tamasic. Do thou hear of it.

सत्त्वानुरूपा सर्वस्य श्रद्धा भवति भारत ।
श्रद्धामयोऽयं पुरुषो यो यच्छद्धः स एव सः ॥ ३ ॥

The faith of each is in accordance with
his nature, O Bharata. The man is made up of
his faith ; as a man's faith is, so is he.

यजन्ते सारिवका देवान्यक्षरक्षांसि राजसाः ।
प्रेतान्भूतगणांश्चान्ये यजन्ते तामसा जना: ॥ ४ ॥

Sattvic men worship the Gods; Rajasic
the Yakshas * and the Rakshasas; the others-
Tamasic men,-the Pretas and the hosts of
Bhutas.

अज्ञास्त्रविहितं घोरं तप्यन्ते ये तपो जनाः ।
दम्भाहङ्कारसंयुक्ताः कामरागबलान्विताः ॥ ५ ॥

Those men who practise terrific austerities
not enjoined by the scripture, given to hypocrisy
and egotism, endued with the strength of lust and
passion;

कर्शयन्तः शरीरस्यं भूतमाममचेतसः ।
मां चैवान्तःशरीरस्यं तान्विध्द्यासुरनिश्चयान् ॥ ६ ॥

Weakening all the elements in the body-
fools they are-and Me who dwell in the body
within; know thou these to be of demoniac
resolves.

आहारस्वपि सर्वस्य त्रिविधो भवति प्रियः ।
यज्ञस्तपस्तथा दानं तेषां भेदमिमं शृणु ॥ ७ ॥

The food also which is dear to each is
threefold, as also worship, austerity and gift. Do
thou hear of this, their distinction.

आयुःसस्वबलारोग्यसुखप्रीतिविवर्धनाः ।
रस्याः स्निग्धाः स्थिरा हृद्या आहाराः सात्त्विकप्रियाः ॥ ८॥

The foods which increase life, energy,
strength, health, joy and cheerfulness, which are
savoury and oleaginous, substantial and agreeable,
are dear to the Sattvic.

कटुम्छछवणात्युष्णतीक्ष्णरूक्षविदाहिनः ।
आहारा राजसस्येष्टा दुःखशोकामयप्रदाः ॥ ९ ॥

The foods that are bitter, sour, saline,
excessively hot, pungent, dry and burning, are
liked by the Rajasic, causing pain, grief and
disease.

यातयामं गतरसं पातिपर्युषितं च यत् ।
उच्छिष्टमपि चामेघ्यं भोजनं तामसप्रियम् ॥ १० ॥

The food which is stale, tasteless, putrid
and rotten, refuse and impure, is dear to the
Tamasic.

अफलाकाङ्क्षिभिर्यज्ञो विधिदृष्टो य इज्यते ।
यष्टव्यमेवेति मनः समाधाय स सात्तिवकः ।। ११ ॥

That worship is Sattvic which is offered
by men desiring no fruit, as enjoined in the Law
with a fixed resolve in the mind that they should
merely worship.

अभिसन्धाय तु फलं दम्भार्थमपि चैव यत्।
इज्यते भरतश्रेष्ठ तं यज्ञं विद्धि राजसम् ॥ १२ ॥

That which is offered, O best of the
Bharatas, with a view to reward and for osten-
tation, know it to be a Rajasic worship.

विधिहीनमसृष्टान्नं मन्त्रहीनमदक्षिणम् ।
श्रद्धाविरहितं यज्ञं तामसं परिचक्षते ॥ १३ ॥

They declare that worship to be
Tamasic which is contrary to the ordinances, in
which no food is distributed, which is devoid of
mantras and gifts, and which is devoid of faith.

देवद्विजगुरुपाज्ञपूजनं शौचमार्जवम् ।
ब्रह्मचर्यमहिंसा च शारीरं तप उच्यते ॥ १४ ॥

Worshipping the Gods, the twice-born,
teachers and wise men,-purity, straightforward-
ness, continence, and abstinence from injury are
termed the bodily austerity.

अनुद्वेगकरं वाक्यं सत्यं प्रियहितं च यत् ।
स्वाध्यायाभ्यसनं चैव वाङ्ययं तप उच्यते ॥ १५ ॥

The speech which causes no excitement
and is true, as also pleasant and beneficial, and also
the practice of sacred recitation, are said to form
the austerity of speech.

मनःप्रसादः सौम्यत्वं मौनमात्मविनिम्रहः ।
भावसंञ्चद्विरित्येतत्तपो मानसमुच्यते ॥ १६ ॥

Serenity of mind, good-heartedness,
silence, self-control, purity of nature, -this is called
the mental austerity.

श्रद्धया परया तप्तं तपस्तत्त्रिविधं नरैः ।
अफलाकाङ्क्षिभिर्युक्तैः सात्त्विकं परिचक्षते ॥ १७ ॥

This threefold austerity, practised by
devout* men with utmost faith, desiring no fruit,
they call Sattvic.

सत्कारमानपूजार्थं तपो दम्भेन चैव यत् ।
क्रियते तदिह प्रोक्तं राजसं चलमध्रुवम् ॥ १८ ॥

That austerity which is practised with
the object of gaining good reception, honour and
worship, and with hypocrisy †, is said to be of
this ‡ world, to be Rajasic, unstable and uncertain.

मूढघाहेणात्मनो यत्पीडया क्रियते तपः ।
परस्योत्सादनार्थं वा तत्तामसमुदाहृतम् ॥ १९ ॥

The austerity which is practised out of
a foolish notion, with self-torture, or for the
purpose of ruining another, is declared to be
Tamasic.

दातव्यमिति यद्दानं दीयतेऽनुपकारिणे ।
देशे काले च पात्रे च तद्दानं सात्त्विकं स्मृतम् ॥ २० ॥

That gift which is given-knowing it to
be a duty to give-to one who does no service, in
place and in time and to a worthy person, that
gift is held Sattvic.

यत् प्रत्युपकारार्थं फलमुद्दिय वा पुनः ।
दीयते च परिष्ठिष्टं तद्दानं राजसं स्मृतम् ॥ २१ ॥

And that gift which is given with a view
to a return of the good, or looking for the fruit, or
reluctantly, that gift is held to be Rajasic.

अदेशकाले यद्दानमपात्रेभ्यश्च दीयते ।
असत्कृतमवज्ञातं तत्तामसमुदाहृतम् ॥ २२ ॥

The gift that is given at a wrong place or
time, to unworthy persons, without respect or with
insult, that is declared to be Tamasic.

ओं तत्सदिति निर्देशो ब्रह्मणस्त्रिविधः स्मृतः ।
ब्राह्मणास्तेन वेदाश्च यज्ञाश्च विहिताः पुरा ॥ २३ ॥

"OM,TAT, SAT": this has been taught to be
the triple designation of Brahman*. By that
were created of old* the brahmanas and the Vedas
and the sacrifices.

तस्मादोमित्युदाहृत्य यज्ञदानतपःक्रियाः ।
प्रवर्तन्ते विधानोक्ताः सततं ब्रह्मवादिनाम् ॥ २४ ॥

Therefore, with the utterance of 'Om,'
are the acts of sacrifice, gift and austerity, as
enjoined in the scriptures, always begun by the
students of Brahman

तदित्यनभिसन्घाय फलं यज्ञतपःक्रियाः ।
दानऋियाश्च विविधाः क्रियन्ते मोक्षकाङ्क्षिभिः ॥ २५ ॥

With 'Tat,' without aiming at the fruits,
are the acts of sacrifice and austerity and the
various acts of gift performed by the seekers of
moksha.

सद्भावे साधुभावे च सदित्येतत्प्रयुज्यते ।
प्रज्ञस्ते कर्माणे तथा सच्छब्द: पार्थं युज्यते ।। २६ ।।

The word 'Sat' is used in the sense of
reality and of goodness; and so also, O Partha, the
word 'Sat' is used in the sense of an auspicious act.

यज्ञे तपसि दाने च स्थितिः सदिति चोच्यते ।
कर्म चैव तदर्थीयं सदित्येवाभिघीयते ॥ २७ ॥

Devotion to sacrifice, austerity and gift is
also spoken of as 'Sat'; and even action in connec-
tion with these is called 'Sat.'

अश्रद्धया हुतं दत्तं तपस्तप्तं कृतं च यत् ।
असदित्युच्यते पार्थं न च तत्प्रेत्य नो इह ॥ २८ ॥

Whatever is sacrificed, given, or done,
and whatever austerity is practised, without faith,
it is called 'asat,' O Partha; it is naught here or
hereafter.

chapter 18

CONCLUSION.

संन्यासस्य महाबाहो तत्त्वमिच्छामि वेदितुम् ।
त्यागस्य च हृषीकेश पृथकेशिनिषूदन ॥ १ ॥

'Of samnyasa' O Mighty-armed, I desire
to know the truth, O Hrishikesa, as also of
'tyaga', severally, O Slayer of Kesin.

काम्यानां कर्मणां न्यासं संन्यासं कवयो विदुः ।
सर्वकर्मफल्ल्यागं प्राहुस्त्यागं विचक्षणाः ॥ २ ॥

Sages understand 'samnyasa' to be the
renouncement of interested works ; the abandon-
ment of the fruits of all works, the learned declare,
is 'tyaga'

त्याज्यं दोषवदियेके कर्म प्राहुर्मनीषिण: ।
यज्ञदानतपःकर्म न त्याज्यमिति चापरे ॥ ३ ॥

That action should be abandoned as an
evil, some philosophers declare; while others
(declare) that acts of sacrifice, gift and austerity
should not be given up.

Now, as to these divergent views,
निश्चयं शृणु मे तत्र त्यागे भरतसत्तम ।
लागो हि पुरुषव्याघ्र त्रिविधः सम्प्रकीर्तितः ॥ ४ ॥

Learn from Me the truth about this aban-
donment, O best of the Bharatas; abandonment,
verily, O best of men, has been declared to be of
three kinds.

यज्ञदानतपःकर्मे न त्याज्यं कार्यमेव तत् ।
यज्ञो दानं तपश्चैव पावनानि मनीषिणाम् ॥ ५ ॥

Practice of worship, gift, and austerity
should not be given up ; it is quite necessary ; wor-
ship, gift and austerity are the purifiers of the
wise.

एतान्यपि तु कर्माणि सङ्गं त्यक्तवा फळानि च ।
कर्तव्यानीति मे पार्थं निश्चितं मतमुत्तमम् ॥ ६ ॥

But even those actions should be perform-
ed, setting aside attachment and the fruits; this,
O son of Pritha, is My firm and highest belief.

नियतस्य तु संन्यासः कर्मणो नोपपद्यते ।
मोहात्तस्य परित्यागस्तामसः परिकीर्तितः ॥ 🏼 ॥

Verily, the abandonment of an obligatory
duty is not proper; the abandonment thereof from
ignorance is declared to be Tamasic.

दुःखमिलेव यत्कर्म कायञ्जेशभयात्त्यजेत् ।
स कृत्वा राजसं त्यागं नैव त्यागफलं लभेत् ॥ ८ ॥

Whatever act one may abandon because
it is painful, from fear of bodily trouble, he
practises Rajasic abandonment, and he
shall
obtain no fruit whatever of abandonment.

कार्यमित्येव यत्कर्म नियतं क्रियतेऽर्जुन ।
सङ्गं त्यक्तवा फलं चैव स त्यागः सात्त्विको मतः ॥ ९ ॥

Whatever obligatory work is done, by
Arjuna, merely because it ought to be done, by
abandoning attachment and also the fruit, that
abandonment is deemed to be Sattyic.

न द्वेष्ट्यकुशलं कर्म कुशलं नानुषज्जते ।
त्यागी सत्त्वसमाविष्टो मेघावी छिन्नसंशय: ॥ १० ॥

He hates not evil action, nor is he attach-
ed, to a good one,--he who has abandoned,
pervaded by Sattva and possessed of wisdom, his
doubts cut asunder.

न हि देहभृता शक्यं त्यक्तुं कर्माण्यशेषतः ।
यस्तु कर्मफल्ल्यागी स त्यागीत्यमिघीयते ॥ ११ ॥

Verily, it is not possible for an embodied
being to abandon actions completely; he who-
abandons the fruits of actions is verily said to be
an abandoner.

अनिष्टमिष्टं मिश्रं च त्रिविधं कर्मणः फलम ।
मवत्यत्यागिनां प्रेल न तु संन्यासिनां कचित् ॥ १२ ॥

The threefold fruit of action,-evil, good,
and mixed,-accrues after death to non-abandon-
ers, but never to abandoners.

पञ्चैतानि महाबाहो कारणानि निबोध मे ।
साङ्ख्ये कृतान्ते प्रोक्तानि सिद्धये सर्वकर्मणाम् ॥ १३ ॥

These five factors in the accomplishment
of all action, know thou from Me, O mighty-
armed, as taught in the Sankhya which is the end
of action.

अधिष्ठानं तथा कर्ता करणं च पृथग्विधम्।
विविधाश्च पृथकवेष्टा दैवं चैवात्र पञ्चमम् ॥ १४ ॥

The seat and actor and the various
organs, and the several functions of various sorts,
and the Divinity also, the fifth among these;

शरीरवाब्द्यनोभिर्यत्कमे प्रारमते नरः ।
न्याय्यं वा विपरीतं वा पञ्चैते तस्य हेतवः ॥ १५ ॥

Whatever action a man does by the body,
speech and mind, right or the opposite, these five
are its causes.

तत्रैवं सति कर्तारमात्मानं केवळं तु यः ।
पइयत्यक्तबुद्धित्वान्न स पइयति दुर्मति: ॥ १६ ॥

Now, such being the case, verily, he who
as untrained in understanding, looks on the pure
Self as the agent, that man of perverted intelli-
gence sees not.

यस्य नाहङ्कृतो भावो बुद्धिर्यस्य न छिप्यते ।
हत्वाऽपि सं इमांल्लोकान्न हन्ति न निबच्यते ॥ १७ ॥

He who is free from egotistic notion,
whose mind is not tainted,-though he kills these
creatures, he kills not, he is not bound.

ज्ञानं ज्ञय परिज्ञाता त्रिविधा कर्मचोदना ।
करणं कर्म कर्तेति त्रिविधः कमसङ्ग्रहः ॥ १८ ॥

Knowledge, the object known, the knower.
(form) the threefold impulse to action; the organ,
the end, the agent, from the threefold basis of
action.

ज्ञानं कर्भ च कर्तां च त्रिधेव गुणभेदतः ।
प्रोच्यते गुणसङ्ख्याने यथावच्छृणु तान्यापे ।। १९ ।।

Knowledge and action, and the agent,
are said in the science of gunas to be of three
kinds only, according to the distinction in gunas.
Hear thou duly of them.

सर्वभूतेषु येनैकं भावमव्ययमीक्षते ।
अविभक्तं विभक्तेषु तज्ज्ञानं विद्धि सात्त्विकम् ॥ २० ॥

That by which a man sees the one Indes-
tructible Reality in all beings, inseparate in the
separated,-that knowledge know thou as Sattvic.

पृथक्तवेन तु यज्ज्ञानं नानाभावान्प्रथग्विघान् ।
वेत्ति सर्वेषु भूतेषु तज्ज्ञानं विद्धि राजसम् ॥ २१ ॥

But that knowledge which by differen-
tiation, sees in all the creatures various entities of
distinct kinds, that knowledge know thou
Rajasic.

यतु कृत्स्नवदेकस्मिन् कार्ये सक्तमहेतुकम् ।
अतत्त्वार्थवदल्पं च तत्तामसमुदाहृतम् ॥ २२ ॥

But that which clings to one single effect
as if it were all, without reason, having no real
object, and narrow, that is declared to be Tama-
sic.

नियतं सङ्करहितमरागद्वेषतः कृतम् ।
अफलप्रेप्सुना कर्म यत्तत्सात्त्विकमुच्यते ॥ २३ ॥

An action which is ordained, which is
free from attachment, which is done without love
or hatred by one not desirous of the fruit, that
action is declared to be Sattvic.

यत्त कामेप्सुना कर्म साहङ्कारेण वा पुनः ।
क्रियते बहुलायासं तद्राजसमुदाहृतम् ॥ २४ ॥

But the action which is done by one
longing for pleasures, or done by the egotistic,
costing much trouble, that is declared to be Rajasic.

अनुबन्धं क्षयं हिंसामनपेक्ष्य च पौरुषम् ।
मोहादारभ्यते कर्म यत्तत्तामसमुच्यते ॥ २५ ॥

The action which is undertaken from
delusion, without regarding the consequence, loss,
injury, and ability, that is declared to be Tamasic.

मुक्तसङ्गोऽनहंवादी धृत्युत्साहसमन्वितः ।
सिद्ध्यसिद्ध्योर्निर्विकारः कर्ता सात्त्विक उच्यते ॥ २६ ॥

Free from attachment, not given to ego-
tism, endued with firmness and vigour, unaffected
in success and failure, an agent is said to be
Sattvic.

रागी कर्मफलप्रेप्सुर्छुब्घो हिंसात्मकोऽञ्जविः ।
हर्षशोकान्वितः कर्ता राजसः परिकीर्तितः ॥ २७ ॥

Passionate, desiring to attain the fruit of
action, greedy, cruel, impure, subject to joy and
sorrow, such an agent is said to be Rajasic.

अयुक्तः प्राकृतः स्तब्धः शठो नैष्कृतिकोऽल्लाः ।
विषादी दीर्घसूत्री च कर्ता तामस उच्यते ॥ २८ ॥

Unsteady, vulgar, unbending, deceptive,
wicked, indolent, desponding, and procrastinating,
(such) an agent is said to be Tamasic.

बुद्धेर्भेदं घृतेश्रेव गुणतस्त्रिविधं शृणु ।
प्रोच्यमानमञेषेण प्रथक्तवेन घनञ्जय ॥ २९ ॥

The threefold division of intellect and
firmness according to qualities, about to be taught
fully and distinctively (by Me), hear thou, O Dha-
nanjaya.

प्रवृत्तिं च निवृत्तिं च कार्याकार्ये भयाभये ।
बन्धं मोक्षं च या वेत्ति बुद्धिः सा पार्थं सात्त्विकी ।। ३० ॥

That which knows action and inaction.
what ought to be done and what ought not to be
done, fear and absence of fear, bondage and libera-
tion, that intellect is Sattvic, O Partha.

यया धर्ममधर्मे च कार्य चाकार्यमेव च ।
अयथावत्प्रजानाति बुद्धिः सा पार्थं राजसी ॥ ३१ ॥

That by which one wrongly understands
dharma and a-dharma, and also what ought to be
done and what ought not to be done, that intellect,
O Partha, is Rajasic.

अघर्मं धर्मामिति या मन्यते तमसा वृता ।
सर्वार्थान् विपरीतांश्च बुद्धिः सा पार्थं तामसी ।। ३२ ।।

That which, enveloped in darkness, sees
adharma as dharma and all things perverted, that
intellect, O Partha, is Tamasic.

घुत्या यया घारयते मनःप्राणेन्द्रियक्रियाः ।
योगेनाव्यभिचारिण्या घृतिः सा पार्थं सात्त्विकी ॥ ३३ ॥

The firmness which is ever accompanied
by Yoga, and by which the activities of thought, of
life-breaths and sense-organs, O Partha, are held
fast, such a firmness is Sattvic.

यया तु धर्मकामार्थान् भृत्या घारयतेऽर्जुन ।
प्रसङ्गेन फलाकाङ्की घृतिः सा पार्थ राजसी ।। ३४ ।।

But the firmness with which one holds
fast to dharma and pleasures and wealth, desirous
of the fruit of each on its occasion, that firmness,
O Partha, is Rajasic.

यया खप्नं भयं शोकं विषादं मदमेव च ।
न विमुद्घति दुर्भेघा घृतिः सा पार्थ तामसी ॥ ३५ ॥

That with which a stupid man does not
give up sleep, fear, grift, depression and lust, that
firmness, O Partha, is Tamasic.

सुखं त्विदानीं त्रिविधं श्रृणु मे भरतर्षभ ।
अभ्यासाद्रमते यत्र दुःखान्तं च निगच्छाति ॥ ३६ ॥

And now hear from Me,-O lord of the
Bharatas,-of the threefold pleasure, in which one
delights by practice and surely comes to the end
of pain*.

यत्तद्भे विषमिव परिणामेऽमृतोपमम् ।
तत्सुखं सारिवकं प्रोक्तमात्मबुद्धिप्रसाद्जम् ॥ ३७ ॥

That which is like poison at first, at the
end, like nectar that pleasure is declared to be
Sattvic, born of the purity of one's own mind.

विषयेन्द्रियसंयोगाद्यत्तदमेऽमृतोपमम् ।
परिणामे विषमिव तत्सुखं राजसं स्मृतम् ॥ ३८ ॥

That pleasure which arises from the
contract of the sense-organ with the object, at first
like nectar, in the end like poison, that is declared
to be Rajasic.

यद्ये चानुबन्धे च सुखं मोहनमात्मनः ।
निद्राळस्पप्रमादोत्थं तत्तामसमुदाहृतम् ॥ ३९ ॥

The pleasure which at first and in the
sequel is delusive of the self, arising from sleep,
indolence, and heedlessness, that pleasure is
declared to be Tamasic.

न तदस्ति प्रथिव्यां वा दिवि देवेषु वा पुनः ।
सत्त्वं प्रकृतिजैर्मुक्तं यदेभिः स्यात्त्रिभिर्गुणैः ॥ ४० ॥

There is no being on earth, or againlin
heaven among the Devas, that can be free from
these three gunas born of Prakriti.

बाह्यणक्षत्रियविशां शूद्राणां च परन्तप ।
कर्माणि प्रविभक्तानि स्वभावप्रभवैर्गुणैः ॥ ४१ ॥

Of Brahmanas and Kshatriyas and
Vaisyas, as also of Sudras, O Parantapa, the duties
are divided according to the qualities born of
nature.

शमो दमस्तपः शौचं क्षान्तिरार्जवमेव च ।
ज्ञानं विज्ञानमास्तिक्यं त्रह्मकर्म स्वभावजम् ॥ ४२ ॥

Serenity, self-restraint, austerity, purity,
forgiveness, and also uprightness, knowledge,
wisdom, faith,-these are the duties of the Brah-
manas, born of nature.

शौर्यं तेजो घृतिर्दाक्ष्यं युद्धे चाप्यपळायनम् ।
दानमीघरभावश्च क्षात्रं कर्म स्वभावजम् ॥ ४३ ॥

Bravery, boldness, fortitude, promptness,
not flying from battle, generosity and lordliness
are the duties of the Kshatriyas, born of nature.

कृषिगोरक्ष्यवाणिज्यं वैइयकर्म खभावजम् ।
परिचर्यात्मकं कर्म शूद्रस्यापि स्वभावजम् ॥ ४४ ॥

Ploughing, cattle-rearing, and trade are
the duties of the Vaisyas, born of nature. And of
the nature of service is the duty of the Sudra,
born of nature.

खे खे कर्मण्यभिरतः संसिद्धि लभते नरः ।
स्वकर्मनिरतः सिद्धिं यथा विन्दति तच्छ्रणु ॥ ४५ ॥

Devoted each to his own duty, man
attains perfection ; how one, devoted to one's own
duty, attains success, that do thou hear.

यतः प्रवृत्तिर्भूतानां येन सर्वमिदं ततम् ।
खकर्मणा तमभ्यच्ये सिद्धिं विन्दति मानवः ॥ ४६ ॥

Him from whom is the evolution of (all)
beings, by whom all this is pervaded,--by worship-
ping Him with his proper duty, man attains
perfection.

श्रेयान् खधर्मो विगुणः परधर्मात्खनुष्ठितात् ।
खभावनियतं कर्म कुर्वन्नाप्नोति किल्बिषम् ॥ ४७ ॥

Better is one's own duty (though) desti-
tute of merits, than the duty of another well
performed. Doing the duty ordained according to
nature one incurs no sin.

सहजं कर्म कौन्तेय सदोषमपि न लजेत ।
सर्वारम्भा हि दोषेण धूमेनामिरिवावृताः ॥ ४८ ॥

The duty born with oneself, O son of
Kunti, though faulty, one ought not to abandon;
for, all undertakings are surrounded with evil, as
fire with smoke.

असक्तबुद्धिः सर्वत्र जितात्मा विगतस्पृहः ।
तैब्बर्म्यसिद्धिं परमां संन्यासेनाधिगच्छति ॥ ४९ ॥

He whose reason is not attached any-
where, whose self is subdued, from whom desire
has fled, he by renunciation attains the supreme
state of freedom from action.

सिद्धिं प्राप्तो यथा ब्रह्म तथाऽऽप्नोति निबोध मे ।
समासेनैव कौन्तेय निष्ठा ज्ञानस्य या परा ॥ ५० ॥

How he who has attained perfection
reaches Brahman, that in brief do thou learn from
Me, O son of Kunti,-that supreme consummation
of knowledge.

बुद्धा विद्युद्धया युक्तो घृत्याऽऽत्मानं नियम्य च ।
शब्दादीन्विषयांस्यक्तवा रागद्वेषौ व्युदस्य च ॥ ५१ ॥

Endued with a pure reason, controlling
the self with firmness, abandoning sound and other
objects, and laying aside love and hatred ;

विविक्तसेवी लघ्वाशी यतवाकायमानसः ।
भ्यानयोगपरो नित्यं वैराग्यं समुपाश्रितः ॥ ५२ ॥

Resorting to a sequestered spot, eating
but little, speech and body and mind subdued,
always engaged in meditation and concentration,
endued with dispassion ;

अहङ्कारं बलं दर्पं कामं क्रोघं परिप्रहम ।
विमुच्य निर्ममः ज्ञान्तो ब्रह्मभूयाय कल्पते ॥ ५३ ॥

Having abandoned egotism, strength,
arrogance, desire, enmity, property, free from the
notion of "mine," and peaceful, he is fit for be-
coming Brahman.

ब्रह्मभूतः प्रसन्नात्मा न शोचति न काङ्कति ।
समः सर्वेषु भूतेषु मद्भक्ति लभते पराम् ॥ ५४ ॥

Becoming Brahman, of serene self, he
neither grieves nor desires, treating all beings
alike ; he attains supreme devotion to Me.

भक्तत्या मामभिजानाति यावान्यश्चासि तस्वतः ।
ततो मां तत्त्वतो ज्ञात्वा विशते तदनन्तरम् ॥ ५५ ॥

By Devotion he knows Me in truth, what
and who I am; then, knowing Me in truth, he
forthwith enters into Me.

सर्वकर्माण्यापे सदा कुर्वाणो मट्यपाश्रय: ।
मत्प्रसादाद्वाप्नोति शाश्वतं पद्मव्ययम् ॥ ५६ ॥

Doing continually all actions whatsoever.
taking refuge in Me,-by My Grace he reachesthe
eternal undecaying Abode.

चेतसा सर्वकर्माणि मयि संन्यस्य मत्परः ।
बुद्धियोगमुपाश्रिय मचित्तः सततं भव ॥ ५७ ॥

Mentally resigning all deeds to Me.
regarding Me as the Supreme, resorting to mental
concentration, do thou ever fix thy heart in Me.

मचित्तः सर्वदुर्गाणि मत्प्रसादात्तरिष्यसि ।
अथ चेन्त्वमहङ्खारान्न श्रोष्यसि विनङ्खबाति ॥ ५८ ॥

Fixing thy heart in Me, thou shalt, by
My Grace, cross over all difficulties ; but if from
egotism thou will not hear (Me), thou shalt perish.

यदहङ्कारमाश्रित्य न योत्स्य इति मन्यसे ।
मिथ्यैष व्यवसायस्ते प्रकृतिस्त्वां नियोक्ष्यति ॥ ५९ ॥

If, indulging egotism, thou thinkest '1
will not fight,' vain is this, thy resolve ; nature will
constrain thee.

स्वभावजेन कौन्तेय निवद्धः खेन कर्मणा ।
कर्तुं नेच्छसि यन्मोहात्करिष्यस्यवशोऽपि तत् ॥ ६० ॥

Bound (as thou art), O son of Kunti, by
thy own nature-born act, that which from delusion
thou likest not to do, thou shalt do, though against
thy will.

ईश्वरः सर्वमूतानां हृद्देशेऽर्जुन तिष्ठति ।
भ्रामयन्सर्वभूतानि यन्त्रारूढानि मायया ॥ ६१ ॥

The Lord dwells in the hearts of all
beings, O Arjuna, whirling by Maya all beings (as
if) mounted on a machine.

तमेव शरणं गच्छ सर्वभावेन भारत ।
तत्प्रसादात्परां शान्ति स्थानं प्राप्स्यासि शाश्वतम् ॥ ६२ ॥

Fly unto Him for refuge with all thy
being, O Bharata; by His Grace shalt thou obtain
supreme peace (and) the eternal resting place.

इति ते ज्ञानमाख्यातं गुद्याद्गुद्यतरं मया ।
विमृत्यैतदशेषेण यथेच्छसि तथा कुरु ॥ ६३ ॥

Thus has wisdom, more secret than all
that is secret, been declared to thee by Me; reflect
thou over it all and act as thou pleasest.

सर्वगुह्यतमं भूय: श्रृणु मे परमं वच: ।
इष्टोऽसि मे दृढमिति ततो वक्ष्यामि ते हितम् ॥ ६४ ॥

Hear thou again My word supreme, the
most secret of all; because thou art My firm
friend, therefore will I tell thee what is good.

मन्मना भव मद्भक्तो मद्याजी मां नमस्कुरु ।
मामेवैष्यसि सत्यं ते प्रतिजाने प्रियोऽसि मे ॥ ६५ ॥

Fix thy thought on Me, he devoted to
Me, worship Me, do homage to Me. Thou shalt
reach Myself. The truth do I declare to thee;
(for) thou art dear to Me.

सर्वधर्मान्पारिखन्य मामेकं शरणं व्रज ।
अहं त्वा सर्वपापेभ्यो मोक्षयिष्यामि मा छुनः ॥ ६६ ॥

Abandoning all righteous deeds, seek me
as thy sole Refuge; I will liberate thee from all
sins; do thou not grieve.

इदं ते नातपरकाय नाभक्ताय कदाचन ।
न चाञ्चभ्रूषवे बाच्यं न च मां योऽम्यसूयति ॥ ६७ ॥

This (which has been taught) to thee is
never to be taught to one who is devoid of austeri-
ties, nor to one who is not devoted, nor to one who
does not do service, nor to one who speaks ill of
Me.

य इदं परमं गुह्यं मद्भक्तेष्वभिधास्यति ।
भक्तिं मयि परां क्रुत्वा मामेवैष्यत्यसंज्ञयम् ॥ ६८ ॥

He who with supreme devotion to Me
will teach this Supreme Secret to My devotees,
shall doubtless come to Me.

न च तस्मान्मनुष्येषु कश्चिन्मे प्रियकृत्तमः ।
भविता न च मे तस्मादन्यः प्रियतरो भुवि ॥ ६९ ॥

Nor is there any among men who does
dearer service to Me than he; nor shall there be
another on earth dearer to Me than he.

अध्येष्यते च य इमं धर्म्यं संवादमावयोः ।
ज्ञानयज्ञेन तेनाहमिष्टः स्यामिति मे मति: ॥ ७० ॥

And he who will study this sacred dia-
logue of ours, by him I shall have been worshipped
by the sacrifice of wisdom, I deem.

श्रद्धावाननसूयश्च शृणुयाद्पि यो नरः ।
सोऽपि मुक्त: ग्रुमाँझोकान्त्राप्तुयात्पुण्यकर्मणाम् ॥ ७१ ॥

And the man also who hears, full of
faith and free from malice even he, liberated,
shall attain to the happy worlds of the righteous.

कचिदेतच्छूतं पार्थं त्वयैकाप्रेण चेतसा ।
कचिदज्ञानसम्मोह: प्रणष्टत्ते घनञ्जय ।। ७२ ॥

Has it been heard by thee, O Partha-
with an attentive mind ? Has the delusion of igno-
rance been destroyed, O Dhananjaya ?

नष्टो मोहः स्मृतिर्छेब्धा त्वत्प्रसादान्मयाऽच्युत ।
स्थितोऽस्मि गतसन्देहः करिष्ये वचनं तव ॥ ७३ ॥

Destroyed is delusion, and I have gained
recognition through Thy Grace, O Achyuta. I am
firm, with doubts gone. I will do Thy word.

इत्यहं वासुदेवस्य पार्थस्य च महात्मनः ।
संवादमिममश्रौषमद्भुतं रोमहर्षणम् ॥ ७४ ॥

Thus have I heard this wonderful dialogue
between Vasudeva and the high-souled Partha,
which makes the hair stand on end.

ं व्यासप्रसादाच्छूतवानेतद् गुह्यमहं परम् ।
योगं योगेश्वरात्कृष्णात्साक्षात्कथयतः स्वयम् ॥ ७५ ॥

Through the grace of Vyasa have I heard
this Supreme and most secret Yoga direct from
Krishna, the Lord of Yoga, Himself declaring it.

राजन्संस्मृत्य संस्मृत्य संवादमिममद्भुतम् ।
केशवार्जुनयोः पुण्यं हृष्यामि च मुहुर्मुहुः ॥ ७६ ॥

O king, remembering every moment this
wonderful and holy dialogue between Kesava and
Arjuna, I rejoice again and again.

तच संस्मृत्य संस्मृत्य रूपमत्यद्भुतं हरेः ।
विस्मयो मे महान् राजन् हृष्यामि च पुनः पुनः ॥ ७७ ॥

And remembering every moment the most
wonderful Form of Hari, great is my wonder, O
king ; and I rejoice again and again.

यत्र योगेश्वरः कृष्णो यत्र पार्थो धनुर्धरः ।
तत्र श्रीर्विजयो भ्तिर्ध्रुवा नीतिर्मतिर्मम ॥ ७८ ॥

Wherever is Krishna, the Lord of Yoga,
wherever is Arjuna, the archer, there fortune,
victory, prosperity and polity are established, I
deem.
